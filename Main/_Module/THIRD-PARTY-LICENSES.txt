NOT TAOM's own work. That is four things: the NuGet runtime libraries under
_Module/bin/, the audio under _Module/ModuleSounds/, the fonts under
_Module/GUI/Fonts/, and the runtime fonts under _Module/GUI/FactionUI/RuntimeFonts/.
Each is listed below.

in the TAOM repository for the path-by-path scope, and

The TAOM name and branding are covered by neither license. See TRADEMARK.md.

The BUTR / Harmony / Microsoft / Serilog stack is redistributed by the companion
TAOM.Dependencies module and is covered by its own notice file at

Scene scripts under Main/SceneScripts/ take behavioural inspiration from the
Alliance mod (https://github.com/Byak0/Alliance, GPL v3). They are TAOM original
work produced by a clean-room procedure: Alliance source was read once to extract a
behavioural spec, the spec was committed, and the implementation was written from
the spec. No Alliance source code appears in TAOM. Procedure and per-file mapping:
docs/scene-scripts/ATTRIBUTION.md in the TAOM repository.

WARG ASSETS  --  companion module LOTRLOME_Armory

The warg skeleton, meshes, textures, animation clips and sound bank distributed in the
companion LOTRLOME_Armory module are the work of Byak0, author of the Alliance mod
(https://github.com/Byak0/Alliance), and are redistributed with the author's permission.
TAOM does not claim them and does not license them onward. They are excluded from the
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 grant that covers TAOM's own content.

They shipped as a separate Alliance.Wargs module until 2026-08-28, when they were absorbed
into LOTRLOME_Armory to reduce the number of modules a player installs. The warg voice
definition at _Module/ModuleData/VoiceDefinitions/LOTR/ came across in the same change.

TAOM's warg behaviour -- the behaviour tree, rage mode, attack service and the surrounding
code under Main/Features/Warg/ and Main/Features/AdvancedCombat/ -- is original TAOM
software under the MIT License, ported from the maintainer's own LOTRAOM project. It is not
derived from Byak0's work.

FONTS  --  _Module/GUI/Fonts/  (aniron, minionpro, ringbearer)

Third-party typefaces, redistributed in Bannerlord's compiled .fnt/.bfnt form.
TAOM does not hold rights in these faces, does not claim them, and does not

docs/reference/asset-provenance.md.
