# TAOM v2.0.29

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.2 required

This is the first TAOM build for **Bannerlord v1.5.2**, which is on the Steam **beta** branch as of
2026-09-14. It does not run on 1.4.8. Switch the game to the beta branch, let Steam update, then
install this build. The campaign map was re-baked with the v1.5.2 Modding Kit, which the 1.5.0 engine
had made necessary, and a new campaign reaches it (checked 2026-09-14 on the maintainer's machine).
Loading a save made on an earlier TAOM build is **untested** on this engine, so start a new campaign
if you can.

Under the hood, the four modules ship as one version again: TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and
LOTRLOME_Armory all read **v2.0.29**. The bundled BUTR stack moved with the engine (ButterLib 2.12.0,
MCM 5.12.3, UIExtenderEx 2.13.3). If you run ButterLib or MCM as separate mods, remove them: TAOM
ships its own.

## 🗺️ Advanced Starting Options speak Middle-earth

The v1.5 **Advanced Starting Options** screen now lists the **14 kingdoms of Middle-earth** instead
of Calradia's, and the "United Empire" option is gone, since there is no Empire to unite. Start as a
vassal of Rohan, an outlaw in the shadow of Mordor, or a landed lord of Gondor, from the same screen
vanilla players use.

## ⚔️ Executions on the new engine

Bannerlord 1.5 replays an execution as a cutscene with the culture's own headsman, and a culture
without one crashed the game the moment you executed a lord. **Every TAOM culture has its
headsman** now. The alignment rule survived the engine's rewrite of execution fallout: beheading an
enemy of your side still costs you no honour, and only the victim's own side takes it personally.

## 🏰 Lords fight at the strength their sheets promise

Bannerlord 1.5 changed how a lord's starting skills are read, and 83 lords (64 in the TAOM roster,
19 vanilla-id lords including Sauron, the Witch-king and the Nazgûl) would have started with stale
numbers instead of their intended ones. Their sheets match their templates again.

⚠️ **New campaigns only.** A lord's skills are written into your save when the campaign starts.

## 💰 Recruitment costs follow the party leader

Since 1.5.2 the recruitment discounts (Head Hunter, Renowned Archer, Frugal and the rest) belong to
the **party leader's** perks rather than whoever is doing the buying. TAOM's extended cost table now
applies them the same way, so a companion buying troops for your party gets your discounts.

## 🧭 Map figures, party screen, nameplates

The map figure scale slider works on 1.5.2's split code path, the party screen and the nameplates are
re-based on the new vanilla layouts (including the blood-feud icon on nameplates), and the clan
screen's weighted party sizes show on every row again.

## 🧰 For bug reports

A crash bundle now names the engine it ran on and whether the game shut down cleanly, and carries
the diagnostics log. A new `[MapLoad]` heartbeat in the log says within thirty seconds whether the
campaign map came up, which is the first thing we will ask for if it does not.

## 🔧 Also in this build

- The map-load path renumbered its diagnostics to Patch89; nothing to do on your side.
- Two leftover career HUD prefabs from an old import are gone.
- Prisoner recruitment morale keeps Bannerlord 1.5.2's Honor-trait reduction on top of TAOM's
  culture rules.
