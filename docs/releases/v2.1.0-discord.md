# TAOM v2.1.0

## ⚠️ Read this before you update

**This build requires Bannerlord v1.5.0, which is currently a Steam beta-branch release.** You will
not be able to run it on the default branch. In Steam: right-click Mount & Blade II Bannerlord,
Properties, Betas, and opt into `beta`.

**War Sails (NavalDLC) must be off.** TAOM now declares it incompatible, so the launcher refuses the
pairing instead of letting you find out in a broken campaign. War Sails ships its own settlements,
kingdoms, clans, heroes and cultures, all of which collide with a map that strips every vanilla
settlement.

The minor version bump is the engine cut-over, not a content milestone. If you want to stay on
Bannerlord v1.4.8, stay on v2.0.22.

## 💥 Crashes Fixed

The v1.5.0 update broke four things in ways that produced no error until you hit them.

- **Executing a lord crashed the game.** v1.5.0 made every culture name an executioner and then read
  it without checking. Vanilla sets one on 6 of its 16 cultures; TAOM had it on none of 24, so any
  execution by a lord of a TAOM culture was a hard crash. Executions are a headline v1.5.0 feature
  through the new Blood Feuds, so this was on the path to almost everyone.
- **The campaign map threw an error every frame** from a party nameplate that had not been told
  about the new blood-feud icon.
- **Starting as a female Trader crashed at campaign start.** The new start picks a random female
  notable from your culture, and no TAOM culture had one. Every culture now has female notables,
  built from that culture's own people so dwarves still look like dwarves.
- **Every new campaign started with 1000 denars.** v1.5.0 now sets your starting gold directly,
  after TAOM has already granted its own, so the culture values were being thrown away and elves and
  dwarves lost their differentiation. You get the intended 2000 / 3500 / 4000 again.

## ⚔️ Black Númenóreans

**Mordor's first human troops, and its first horse cavalry.** A full elite line of **13 troops from
T5 to T9**, splitting into Cavalry, Infantry and Archer, each four deep.

They wear **113 new Armory entries**: 78 armour pieces, 22 crafting pieces, 7 crafted weapons
(three one-handed swords, three two-handed, one lance) and 6 shields, built on art that had been
sitting in the repo unreferenced since August.

This is a **standalone elite line**. No recruitment pool changed, so you will meet them in the field
rather than hire them: they come through Mordor's lord party templates, its level-3 patrols, the
vassal reward and prisoner recruitment.

## 🎲 Advanced Starting Options

v1.5.0 added a pre-campaign screen for picking a scenario, a player start and global modifiers.
Out of the box it did not know Middle-earth existed.

- **The faction lists are hardcoded in vanilla**, so the menu offered you "Western Empire" and then
  started you in Gondor. All eight now read their real names, and **TAOM's fourteen kingdoms have
  been added** to every faction picker: Erebor, Rivendell, Mirkwood, Lothlórien, Isengard, Gundabad,
  Umbar, Dol Guldur, Lindon, Goblin-town and the rest.
- **The United Empire scenario is gone.** It merges the three imperial factions into a hardcoded
  "Calradian Empire", which here means Gondor, Mordor and Dunland in one realm, and two of its three
  unifier choices left you ruling an empty shell with no fiefs.
- **Civil Unrest now does something.** It works by raising vanilla's rebellion thresholds, which TAOM
  overrides, so picking it changed nothing at all. TAOM's softer values now scale with it.
- 49 menu strings translated. Sea and naval scenarios stay out.

## ⚖️ Balance

- **AI lord parties keep the roster they spawn with.** Parties were spawning with 500 to 3000 troops
  and collapsing to 50 to 150 within a couple of ticks. Neither starvation nor unpaid wages removes a
  single troop in vanilla, so both were red herrings: it was TAOM's own troop-weight shedding running
  every day against the stock party-size limit.
- **Evil cultures actually have their party bonus now.** Mordor was the only one below the intended
  floor, sitting at +20% after a cut in May took it from +30% to +10%. Blue Craig gets its own +40%
  instead of borrowing Goblin-town's.
- **Lord parties across 193 templates were resized** by faction: goblins field the largest hosts,
  then orcs and uruks, then Erebor, then men, then elves.
- **Captured fiefs spread across clans.** Players reported the AI handing holding after holding to
  the same clan. That was stock vanilla behaviour, where a clan's self-vote barely moves the ballot.
- **AI startup wealth is priced off what troops actually cost.** A flat grant is not flat in effect:
  troops cost between 6.91 and 21.66 denars a day per head depending on culture, so an identical
  250,000 funded nearly twice as much campaign for one faction as another.

## 🔧 Also Fixed

**Javelins no longer ignore shields** (this was the "javelins do massive damage" report; the shield
penetration mechanic ships off) · **map party icons scale properly again**, instead of a half-size
horse carrying a full-size rider · **special-resource icons in the map bar** stopped installing
silently when v1.5.0 rearranged the bar · **conversation lines matching the wrong speaker**, from 23
of 35 overridden strings losing the tags that pick the culture, persona and trait variant · **88
dwarf action sets** missing an animation type v1.5.0 added, the same shape as the old dwarf water
crash.

⚠️ **MCM keeps your existing settings.** If you had shield penetration on, it stays on. Reset it
yourself or delete `Configs/ModSettings/Global/TAOM/`.

## 🌍 Localisation

The outstanding backlog was translated across all **12 languages**. The 49 new Advanced Starting
Options strings and the new female notable names are in every language file as English for now, with
a translation pass to follow.

## 📦 Under the hood

**The dependency guard is back.** TAOM's module file explained at length why it must refuse to load
against a stale TAOM.Dependencies, and the element enforcing that had been deleted out from under
the comment. v2.0.21 and v2.0.22 both shipped documenting a guard that was not there. A mismatch is
what makes every character render in bind pose.

Four new automated gates now cover the failure classes this engine bump exposed: engine code that
moves out from under a patch, interface injections whose target has been rearranged, culture data
missing an entry the engine reads without checking, and transform output losing information the
source had.
