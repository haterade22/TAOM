# TAOM v2.0.39

This note covers the changes since v2.0.38.

## ⚠️ Before you install

- **Bannerlord v1.5.7 only** (Steam **beta** branch), the same as v2.0.38.
- **Modules:** TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory all read **v2.0.39**. Install them together,
  and take TAOM and TAOM_Map from the same package. The package also carries **DualWield v0.10.7**.
- **Install into a clean folder.** Remove the old `Modules\TAOM` folder first, then copy the new one in.
- **Realms is now part of TAOM.** You no longer need the separate TAOM.Realms module.
- **Saves:** a v2.0.38 campaign should load. Heroes in an existing save keep the faces stored in that save; the new
  face ranges show on heroes a new campaign creates.
- **Settings:** the Marshal's Word **Outlines** setting now starts off, and a value you saved earlier is reset to off.
  Turn it on again under Marshal's Word if you want the company outlines. The new switches below are on by default.
- **Co-op:** do not start a co-op session on this build yet.

## 👑 Realms: titles, claims and the Return of the King

- Every village, castle, town and realm has a title, a holder by right, a succession law and claims.
- Gondor's throne starts vacant under Steward Denethor. A clan serving Gondor with Aragorn can restore the crown and
  found the House of Telcontar in Minas Tirith. Playing as Aragorn, restoring the throne crowns you.
- Claims cost your culture's special resource and are pressed with influence. A duchy or higher can be usurped or
  formed once your side holds most of the lands beneath it.
- Kingdom, Empire and Union titles rename the kingdom whose ruler holds them, and Khazad-dûm can be formed by the
  dwarves over Moria's duchies.
- Tribute is now a daily share of each vassal clan's fief income and shows in your expected gold. Each side has an
  on/off switch and a percentage (1 to 25, default 5).
- The encyclopedia has a page for every title. Options are under **World / Realms** and apply with no restart.

## ⚔️ Battles

- **Hero auras:** named heroes strengthen the allied soldiers within 15 m of them, each in their own way. Aragorn's
  men hold their nerve and take less damage, Dáin's dwarves keep their feet, Théoden and Éomer's riders strike harder,
  and the elves near Elrond, Thranduil and Galadriel draw faster. Options: **Battle Tactics / Hero Auras** (strength 0
  to 2; 0 turns them off).
- **Lost Footing:** soldiers stuck off the walkable ground or in water step back to firm ground after 10 s, and to
  their formation after 30 s when you are not close by. On by default.
- **Battle HUD colours** for colour blind players: Deuteranopia, Protanopia or Tritanopia, under **Accessibility**.
- The battle action bar no longer darkens a field battle.
- With DualWield on, an arena practice fight started from the town menu no longer reloads and crashes.

## 🏰 Helm's Deep story mission

- When the War Chronicle's Battle of the Hornburg opens and the map is free, you are offered the defence of Helm's
  Deep. Ride there and choose **Defend the walls** or **Let fate decide**.
- The mission has its own music track, "Isengard vs. Rohan".
- You can turn the offer off with the War Chronicle setting **Offer Story Missions**.

## 🗣️ Encounters and lords

- Lords, caravans, villagers, bandits and hideout chiefs now speak in their own people's voice, and the words change
  with who you are to them. 681 new lines replace the base game's Calradian text. Option: **World / Encounter Lines**.
- Lords introduce themselves, and minor factions greet you, in Middle-earth terms.
- Haldir, Rúmil and Orophin join Lothlórien.
- A lord's hall seats only as many lords as it holds, so you no longer meet 20 to 40 lords packed at the door.
  Option: **Fief Management / Hall Seats**.
- AI lords take an idle relative along as scout, surgeon, engineer or quartermaster. Option: **World / Lord Retinue**.
- Lord faces now match the peoples of the books: Rohirrim fair and mostly blond, elf lords fair, Variags swarthy.
- Young female dwarves now use the female body.

## 🌍 War of the Ring

- In Full War, the side far behind on the war meter can call a Neutral realm that leans its way into the war.
- A messenger now waits for a hero who is a fugitive, just released, or travelling on your orders, instead of being
  lost.
- Switching the War of the Ring off during the full war now lets peace be made.
- A raid now counts toward momentum only when the village is looted.

## 🛡️ Troops and items

- Troops no longer upgrade into a troop with a weaker melee weapon. Lower tiers take vanilla weapons, and unique
  weapons stay with the higher tiers.
- A new character's crafted starter weapon is no stronger than the weakest one the shops of that culture sell.
- War Spoils from a battle now follow the size of the enemy, and Elite Emissary troops cost more, so one small fight
  no longer buys several tier 9 and 10 troops.

## 🎨 Banners and screens

- A new layered banner editor replaces the base game's, in character creation and in a running campaign.
- Custom Battle has the TAOM muster screen: two armies, commanders, factions, battlefield settings and 32 map
  previews.
- New character page and career screen, and live troop previews in the encyclopedia.
- New banners for Lindon, Ered Luin, Nos Nowë, Nos Thavn a Minas Loss and the House of Telcontar.

## 🗺️ Map and Armory

- The Orthanc prisons now use the stealth dungeon scene, which has the spawn points a prison break needs.
- Helm's Deep scene update.
- Many melee weapon damage values are retuned in the Armory.

## 🔧 Behind the scenes

- TAOM's crash net now covers its patches from the first game of a session, and the second game start is about half a
  second faster.
- The weekly AI realm check no longer freezes the map.
- Many crash guards and fixes, and test and documentation updates.

## 🌐 Translations

The new encounter lines and the other new text are in English in all 12 languages until the next translation pass.
