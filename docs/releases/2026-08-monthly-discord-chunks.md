# TAOM: August 2026

Six updates, v2.0.18 through v2.0.23. Bannerlord v1.4.8 supported since 10 August.

## ⚔️ Enlist (new)

- Serve as a common soldier in a lord's party. 365 day term, four ranks (Recruit, Soldier, Veteran, Sergeant), 5/8/14/22 gold a day paid from his purse, held as arrears if he is broke.
- Pick Infantry, Archer, Cavalry or Support. You stand in his formation, and the assignment sets your training and which duties you are offered.
- 27 duties and camp incidents: 13 field duties, 11 camp jobs on a skill check, 3 incidents over late pay or short rations.
- Quartermaster kit once per rank, in your own faction's gear. 84 sets covering every culture at all four ranks.
- Battles score capped kills, formation discipline, and whether you fought your assignment.
- The commander feeds you, shares his morale and heals you with his surgeon. Shore leave when the column stops at a town.
- Losing your commander gives you a choice and seven days of grace instead of silently cutting you loose.
- Off switch in Mod Options > Enlistment.

**Fixed during the month:** joining your commander's battles had never worked once, under any circumstance. You deployed 20 units behind the line on your own team. A rank one recruit commanded the entire battle side while the lord commanded nothing. You starved at 19 health a day, and your survival odds were rolled with no surgeon, so serving made you more likely to die. Asking to be released counted as desertion and forfeited your back pay. Rank progress stopped at Soldier because 8 of 13 duties were arithmetically impossible at zero skill. Also fixed: two dead end menus, a battle that froze if you saved inside it, a hunt duty that killed the game, a commander conversation that crashed after every reload, and being teleported between towns.

## 🏕️ Camps, Refuges and Supply Lines (new)

- **Field camps.** Pitch a camp on the map with a timed raise and a progress bar. Field and fortified camps give morale and forage grain; ambush and lookout camps need the right terrain.
- **Refuges.** Raise a finished camp into a permanent base: garrison, prisoners, stash, a warden, and a Refuge to Stronghold upgrade. Defenders get a damage bonus and a militia rallies to it.
- **Supply lines.** Order a convoy from a town, castle or friendly lord. Goods come from that place's real market stock and troops from its actual volunteers, and you pay when the caravan sets off.

## 👑 Play as an Existing Lord (new)

- At character creation, pick an existing lord of your culture and play as them: their face, gear, skills, clan, fiefs and kingdom. Wanderers are on the list too.
- You keep your own clan and banner, so you start independent and are asked once whether to join your culture's kingdom.
- Sauron and the Nazgûl are off by default.

## 👁️ Aura of Unnatural Dread (new)

- Nazgûl and Sauron drain the morale of nearby enemies until the formation breaks, after which the game's own panic spreads the rout outward.
- It takes sustained contact: about 30 seconds for a line infantryman, about a minute for a veteran. You can walk a formation out of it.
- Elves take 40% of the drain, dwarves 50%, and an elven hero never breaks at all. The player is immune.
- Routed troops are lost, as with any rout. Heroes, companions and siege defenders are safe.
- Battle only, never during deployment, hideouts or tournaments. Tuning in Mod Options > Aura of Dread.

## 🔥 Sauron and the Nine

- They can no longer be taken prisoner. A lost battle now leaves them fugitives rather than ransomable captives. They still lose the army and still take days to raise another.
- It does not make them immortal, and it can be switched off.

## 🎖️ Battlefield Promotions (new)

- Raise a veteran line troop into a named companion, keeping his gear and a skill sheet that fits his level.
- Merit is only earned in fights won without heavily outnumbering the enemy, and the threshold was raised fourfold, so promotions are rarer.
- Declining is remembered instead of re-asked forever. Human, dwarf and elf only.
- Firing a promoted companion no longer wrecks the campaign.

## 🏰 Cultures & Kingdoms

- **Blue Craig and Lindon are real cultures now.** Both were kingdoms running on a borrowed culture, so Blue Craig started you in Goblin-town and Lindon started you in Rivendell. Both have their own troop trees, lords, gear, wanderers, careers and settlements. Lindon is Círdan's Falathrim, not a Rivendell reskin.
- **Nine kingdoms were fielding Calradian troops.** Imperial infantry patrolled Dunland and Vlandian crossbowmen patrolled Rohan, and the same gap ran through villagers, militia, rebels and caravans.
- **Every culture's new lords were being generated as Dunlendings,** including rebel leaders in dwarf and Gondorian towns.
- **Khand was producing Easterling notables, volunteers and market stock** because 26 settlements were still tagged to the wrong culture. This one applies to existing saves.
- **Three goblin kingdoms shared one identical troop tree.** The duplicates are retired, each keeping its top tier unit, and the encyclopedia drops from 69 goblin entries to 25.
- Dale had no caravans or villagers of its own and was spawning Sturgians. An Uruk-hai lord could appear with a Calradian face. The wanderer Noxix has been removed.

## ⚔️ Mordor: the Black Numenoreans (new)

- 13 troops across tiers 5 to 9 with Cavalry, Infantry and Archer branches. Mordor's first human troops and its only horse cavalry.
- 113 new pieces of gear: 78 armour pieces, 22 crafting parts, 7 crafted weapons, 6 shields. They out-armour every other Men or Orc troop, yielding only to Elves and Dwarves.
- Two houses field them properly, Dolgubeth and Wawrim, and thirteen lords were renamed from Adûnaic.

## 🐏 Beasts & Mounts

- **War ram for the Dwarves.** Two mounts, an eight piece barding ladder, a new Ironpass cavalry branch, and twelve lords across three clans riding them. The Ram Rider career had been putting dwarves on ordinary horses.
- **Fell warg for Gundabad.** Previously unused art, now its own creature, heavier and tougher than a common warg and ridden by Gundabad's three cavalry troops.
- **Wargs render again.** A rebuild had cost them their materials, physics and ragdoll, which is why they were turning up invisible and brown.
- **One fewer module to install.** Alliance.Wargs is folded into the Armoury, with no renames, so existing saves are unaffected.

## 🏹 Troops, Lords & Gear

- **Dale's spearmen never used their spears.** All four Dale spears counted as strictly two handed, so 18 shield carrying troops drew a sidearm the instant combat started. 16 more troop kits and the Black Numenorean lance had the same fault.
- **Rohan's spears couch now,** and are still usable with a shield.
- **Upgrading a troop could make it worse.** Anórien Archer Militia into Skirmisher dropped Bow from 130 to 85. All 698 upgrade paths were checked and 222 troops changed. The worst cause silently discarded the authored skills of 44 militia troops, one of which was written at 850 points and delivering 215. Militia are now 2 to 4 times stronger.
- **Settlement militia was being deleted back to about 20 every day** while the overlay reported +5 a day. Minas Tirith should have been holding around 266.
- **Named lords across eight cultures wore the wrong armour.** Denethor and 27 others wore Dol Amroth Swan Knight kit, of whom nine were actually from Dol Amroth. Dale's 53 lords were in Sturgian gear while a full Dale set sat unused, and Isengard's lords were in mid tier plate while their elite plate went unworn.
- **The encyclopedia described the wrong people.** Gríma Wormtongue was married to Éowyn with three children by her, one lord was called RandomDude, and 57 biographies were attached to the wrong lord. 36 lords who had never spawned now exist.
- **There were Dwarves in Dale.** Eight Tolkien Dwarves sat as Dale lords rendered as men, two duplicating real Erebor lords.
- A lord called "Harad Place Holder" shipped in all twelve languages, and seven Haradrim carried elf names, three of them Lothlórien's marchwardens. Grimbold of Grimslade and Pelendur were both rendering as women, and two elf-maidens had beards.
- Khamûl's barding now exists as an item. Armour tiers respect modifiers, so a legendary low tier piece no longer out-armours a plain high tier one.

## ⚖️ Balance

- **Javelins stop ignoring shields.** This caused the "javelins do massive damage" reports: a javelin landing about 40 damage against a shield was landing about 133 and carrying into the soldier behind, and shields broke about three times faster. Now at base game parity.
- **AI lord parties keep the troops they spawn with.** They were spawning at 500 to 3,000 and collapsing to 50 to 150 within a couple of ticks.
- **AI armies fight on their own front** instead of treating the war as one map wide brawl. Applies to existing saves.
- **Evil culture party bonuses restored.** Mordor had been cut from +20% to +10% in May. Blue Craig has its own party size feat, and 193 lord party templates were retargeted.
- **Starting money repriced off what each culture's troops actually cost.** Wages range from 6.91 to 21.66 per head per day, so a flat 250,000 funded nearly twice the campaign for Mordor as for Gundabad. Eight fief-starved cultures got an income floor.
- **Captured fiefs spread across clans** instead of piling onto one. The clan that stormed the place gets a real claim, and a ruling clan holding most of the kingdom can no longer overrule the council. Nine sliders in Mod Options.
- Career bonuses are literal now. A "+4 party size" perk was worth about +13.

## 🧙 Character Creation & Careers

- **Both orc cultures were selectable and gave you nothing:** no backstory, gear, career or starting money. Their 96 backstory lines had never been hooked up.
- **Cave Troll Master could be picked and granted nothing,** its perk tree switched off behind an expired note. Shaghâna and Âbanissa had no careers at all and now have three each.
- **New career screen:** diamond layout, an icon per choice, an Active Effects panel, bronze/silver/gold tiers, correct centring on ultrawide, and no more collapsing on level up.
- **Career balance pass.** Troop damage came down from 3-20% to 2-8% and max health from 25-100 to 5-10. Both were miswired: the damage bonus only sped up village raids and the health bonus only worked in battle.

## 🗺️ Map & Armoury

These two live outside the main mod folder and do not normally appear in a changelog.

**Armoury**
- 179 pieces of art were deleted upstream and 149 were still being worn, which would have rendered 153 items as nothing. All re-dressed: Gondor's lords wear their own region's gear, Easterling kit moved to Loke-Rim, Erebor lost its blue, green and red variants.
- 168 stray entries crashed every dedicated server on boot, which is why operators had to use the single player module order.
- Character creation's poses had never loaded, so 221 animations resolved to nothing, including the father and mother poses behind every face screen.
- Isengard's Uruk-hai rendered as black silhouettes. 79 items corrected, and the White Hand is legible again.
- Female dwarves crashed the game on sight, from one filename one letter off.
- Rhûn helmets, shields, and all the Black Numenorean, war ram and fell warg art landed here, and everything was repacked at month end.
- Around 250 files of Armoury text translated into all twelve languages.

**Map**
- Two castles shipped named after their internal codes, "Castle E9" and "Castle G1", translated that way into every language. They were the only 2 of 988 settlements without a real name, and are now Bergfast and Bagbûrz.
- Settlement prosperity, village hearths and starting fief ownership were written into the live map. 25 settlements were retagged onto Blue Craig and Lindon.

## 💥 Other Crashes Fixed

A kingdom owning no settlements crashing the daily tick · the Erebor tournament crashing repeatedly · the tournament winner panel on "Skip All Rounds" · warg bites · talking to your commander after a reload · clicking "Wait here for some time" after discharge in Minas Tirith · Mod Options hanging on open · characters lying flat in every menu portrait · the career screen collapsing on level up · named companions with no home settlement · a co-op host turning every hero human · a new campaign inheriting the previous one's camps · a first field camp reading as a frozen game · dismantling a refuge killing its warden.

## ⚡ Performance

- **About 4.8 GB of memory freed** by sizing art to what the screen shows. Banner sigils went from 2,624 MB to 128 MB and faction culture art from 2,474 MB to 78 MB. Quality goes up, since the game was downscaling on the fly before.
- **Battle loading went from an 11.9 second gap to 2.5 seconds.**
- Log spam cut hard across battles, tournaments, startup and warg combat. One session's log was 93% two repeating blocks.

## 🌐 Co-op & Dedicated Servers

- TAOM now recognises BannerlordCoop and stands down instead of fighting it. It had been looking for the wrong name, so every protection it had was inert.
- Solo players who merely had a co-op mod installed were having TAOM's War of the Ring rules silently switched off.
- Fixed: a frame rate collapse alongside BannerlordCoop, host at war while client was at peace, and joining a session giving you the wrong race and starting package.
- Dedicated servers crashed on boot and were running a vanilla simulation over TAOM's map because the files were never copied there.
- ⚠️ Co-op is still not verified end to end. These fixes stop TAOM breaking it. They are not a promise that it works.

## 🌍 Localisation

- **317 lines of text no translator could reach** are now registered and translated into all 12 languages, 55% of the mod's own writing. 258 were the cultural feats shown on the culture screen and in the encyclopedia.
- **Polish had never been through the translator.** Five documents claimed it was hand translated, so every run had skipped it. 4,009 entries cleared.
- Enlistment, the orc and goblin backstories, duty messages, the Player Switcher and the Armoury backlog all translated across 12 languages. Four shipped strings had garbled characters.

## 🎮 Compatibility

- Bannerlord v1.4.8 supported since 10 August, with no changes needed.
- The bundled dependency module is at v2.0.6, and a mismatched pair is now blocked at the launcher instead of appearing later as T-posed characters.
- The "Pre-compile Shaders" main menu button is gone. The feature behind it is parked, not deleted.

## 🔨 In Progress

- **Lond Cirion,** Gondor's harbour city. Walls, gatehouse, barracks and ships are built, not yet in a playable build.
- **Install size.** A packaged build can drop about 93 GB of editor only data, 147 GB down to 55 GB in testing.
- **Voice acting.** A recording brief, a 200 take dwarf script and a Khuzdul lexicon are ready for the voice actor.
- **A memory crash on 16 GB machines.** The game now logs a memory reading every 30 seconds so a crash log identifies the cause itself.
