# TAOM v2.4.1

**Gold mode.** Players asked for a way to skip special resources (Castar, War Spoils, Gems and the rest) and pay for elite troops in gold instead. This release adds it as an option. Nothing changes until you switch it on.

Ships with: `TAOM_Map v2.4.0 sha256:6e9d81ad9c79 (49 files), LOTRLOME_Armory v2.4.0 sha256:1c3827bf3679 (464 files)`

## 📦 Install and saves
- **Replace `TAOM` and `TAOM.Dependencies` only.** TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory are unchanged: keep the v2.4.0 ones, which is why they still read v2.4.0 in the launcher.
- **Existing saves load.** The option is a setting, not save data, so you can switch it on or off at any time in a running campaign.

## 🪙 Gold instead of special resources
- **Where:** Mod Options > TAOM - Tales From the Age of Men > **Special Resources**. Turn off **Use Special Resources**. **Gold Per Resource Point** (default **100**) sets the price of each resource point.
- **Upgrades:** upgrading into an elite costs its resource price times the rate in gold, on top of the normal gold cost, right in the party screen.
- **Recruits:** an elite volunteer or tavern mercenary costs its resource price times the rate in gold on top of its normal price. Recruiting an elite from your prisoners costs the same gold, taken when you close the party screen. Supply Lines orders carry it too, with their own markup on top.
- **Upkeep:** elite upkeep is added to your daily wages; hover your party wage to see the "Elite troop upkeep" line. Unpaid wages work like any other wages, and there is no resource desertion.
- **Elite Emissary:** sells its elites for gold, and only your own faction's emissaries serve you, as before.
- **While it is off:** no resource is earned, the resource counter leaves the map bar, and the encyclopedia shows a coin with the gold prices on elite troops.
- **Your resource balance is kept.** Switch the option back on and your old Castar (or War Spoils, Gems...) is there again.
- **Big beasts cost big gold:** at the default rate an elephant rider costs 1,000 gold a day to keep and a Mumakil rider 50,000. Lower the rate if that is too steep.
- **Main party only:** AI lords pay what they paid before.
