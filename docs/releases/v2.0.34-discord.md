# TAOM v2.0.34 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.3

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam **beta** branch). All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies,
TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.34**. If you installed Kysaro's separate **TAOM_FactionUI** module,
remove it: its screens are part of TAOM, and both patch the same places.

- **Coming from the v2.0.33 tester build of 2 October:** keep your campaign; nothing new here needs a new one.
- **Coming from v2.0.32 or older:** start a new campaign. The wild troll bands exist only in a campaign started
  with v2.0.33 or later.

Everything in the v2.0.33 tester build is still here. What is new since then:

## ⚔️ Race and culture abilities (new)

AI soldiers now fight with a battle ability of their race, or for men, of their culture. A soldier waits for the
moment (wounded in melee, a kill, cavalry closing, a target in bow range, kin falling), fires the ability for a few
seconds, and kin nearby whose ability is ready join in, so a whole line braces or a pack goes berserk at once. Each
ability comes round every one to two minutes. Elite troops hit harder and pay more; lords most of all.

- **Races:** berserkers go berserk (every swing breaks a block, no guard, then spent), Uruk-hai fall into
  bloodlust (kills heal and lengthen it), dwarves stand fast (nothing crushes through them), elves quicken (draw,
  aim and feet), orcs swarm (stronger the bigger the mob), goblins scurry, Mordor's uruks hold with iron
  discipline, Gundabad's run their prey down and Dol Guldur's spread dread.
- **Cultures:** Gondor closes ranks, Rohan's riders spur on, Dale aims true, Dunland rages, Harad's arrows bite,
  Rhûn locks its wall, Umbar's corsairs press, Khand's Variags fight savage and the Shadow's men frighten.
- **Seen across the field:** while a fury, guard or dread ability is active, the soldier wears a coloured outline
  (red for fury, steel blue for a guard, violet for dread), and sparks burst on the soldier who fires it. Only the
  40 soldiers nearest the camera are outlined, and Hide Battle UI hides it.
- Works in campaign battles and in Custom Battle. MCM **Battle Tactics > Race Abilities** has the switches: the
  abilities, war cries, a message line for big waves, the glow and sparks, and a debug log.

## 💍 Loading screens

The first loading screen shows TAOM's own background, and the galloping horse in the corner of every loading
screen is now the One Ring's inscription turning in gold.

## 🗺️ Field battles fit the land

Field battles load terrain that fits where you fight: plains in the green kingdoms, desert in Harad, steppe in
Rhûn and Khand, swamp in the Dead Marshes. TAOM's own field scenes, Mordor's and Rohan's among them, are back.

## 🏹 Custom Battle

- Mordor, Gondor and Rohan field their full commanders: Sauron, the Witch-king and the Nazgûl, Boromir, Faramir,
  Imrahil, Théoden and his captains. Khamûl heads Dol Guldur's list.
- Each formation defaults to the culture's own troop where one fits (Rohan's infantry used to be the Vlandian
  Swordsman).

## 🧌 Troops

- The Uruk-hai **Champion** and **Berserker** now wear the Berserker Skirt as body armour: about 90 more armour
  each, and slower for its weight.
- The **Mouth of Sauron's** helm now gives 50 head armour.

## 🌍 Translations

- In a language other than English, 313 strings that were already translated no longer show in English, the
  Abanissa and Shaghana culture names, clans and notables among them.
- Still English in every language for now: the race ability names and wave messages, and the tournament prize
  and training dialogs.

## ⚡ Performance

- Loading a campaign or a Custom Battle should be faster: TAOM now merges the modules' XML in one pass. That merge
  took about 28 of the 50 seconds of a new campaign's loading screen on the dev PC; tell us your load times.
- Battles and the campaign map do less work per blow, per frame and per party.
- A slow battle frame now writes a [Hitch] line to taom_debug.log saying where the time went: send the log with
  any stutter report.

## 📜 Coming from v2.0.32 or older

The v2.0.33 tester build also brought: tournament prizes and training you choose at Join, bets with no cap, and
renown and influence that grow with the field; realm borders and the parchment map; Kysaro's front end and playing
as the lord you pick; the town armoury and the lord's gear ladder; wild troll bands and twenty spider broods;
KEYforce's elephant and spider art; Custom Battle sieges loading again, the Edoras siege, and no crash when a side
picks Nord, Vakken or Darshi; battle corpses that fade; and about 4,600 more names and texts translated into all 12
languages.
