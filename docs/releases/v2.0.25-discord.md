# TAOM v2.0.25

## 🛡️ Gondor Houses Wear Their Own Colours

All **14 Gondor noble houses** now field troops in the colours on their own banner. Thirteen of the
fourteen were wrong before, seven of them completely: **House Malandilionath** flew a dark red
banner and put its soldiers in blue, **House Danuhirionath** flew a white field with a black orb and
dressed its men in olive green, and **House Hýarthulionath** flew gold while its troops wore purple.

Line up two Gondor lords now and you can tell their houses apart at a glance:

- **Húrinionath** (Minas Tirith, the Stewards): black and white
- **Imrazôrionath** (Dol Amroth, the Swan Knights): navy and white
- **Barahirionath** (Lamedon): amber and white
- **Malandilionath** (Calembel): dark red and white
- **Hýarthulionath** (Linhir): gold and navy
- **Danuhirionath** (Blackroot Vale): white and black

⚠️ **Existing saves keep the old colours.** A clan's colours are written into your save file when the
campaign is created, so this shows up on a **new campaign only**.

Note that a garrison or town militia still fights in the kingdom's grey and black rather than a
house's colours. That is expected: those parties have no lord leading them, so there is no house to
take colours from.

## 🗡️ Armoury

**275 broken item references repaired** after an art cleanup moved files out from under them, so the
items that pointed at the missing art work again.

## 🔧 Under the Hood

Memory diagnostics tooling for tracking down the out-of-memory crash some players hit on long
campaigns. No gameplay change.
