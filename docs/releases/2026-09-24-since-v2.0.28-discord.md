# TAOM v2.0.31: everything since v2.0.28
Covers 12 to 24 September, including what v2.0.29 and v2.0.30 already shipped on the Bannerlord 1.5 beta. **Requires Bannerlord 1.5.3 (Steam beta branch).** It does not run on 1.4.8.

**Engine and install**
• Bannerlord 1.5.3. All four modules ship as one version, and the bundled ButterLib, MCM and UIExtenderEx moved with the engine. Remove any standalone Harmony, ButterLib or MCM: TAOM ships its own.
• One setting to change by hand: Combat Mechanics > Auto-Knockdown Weight Ratio now defaults to 6 (was 8). Existing players keep 8 until they move the slider.
• Party screen, nameplates and the map figure scale slider re-based on the 1.5 layouts; the nameplate error every party threw on 1.5.3 is gone.
• Executing a lord crashed the game on 1.5 (the new cutscene needs a headsman per culture). Every culture has one. Executing an enemy of your side still costs no honour.
• 83 lords would have started with stale skills on 1.5, Sauron, the Witch-king and the Nazgûl among them. Fixed, new campaigns only. (#626)
• Recruitment discounts follow the party leader's perks, as 1.5 does.
• Advanced Starting Options lists the 14 kingdoms of Middle-earth, "United Empire" is gone, and the ERROR text every TAOM kingdom showed there is fixed. (#604)
• Crash bundles name the engine and whether the game shut down cleanly, keep the real throw site instead of a rethrow frame, and a map-load heartbeat says within 30 s whether the campaign map came up.
• Campaign map re-saved on the 1.5.2 and 1.5.3 Modding Kits.

**Creatures**
• War elephant: the howdah crew is back and shooting from a moving elephant, the elite howdah is visible, the elephant is 30% bigger and the howdah rides its spine. A battle ending with crew aboard no longer hangs. Its collision fits its body, so arrows and blows no longer pass through neck, legs and trunk. (#627, #624)
• Mûmakil: eight archers on the war tower (five main deck, two upper, one crow's nest), standing on a navmesh, all confirmed shooting. Shares the collision fix. (#627)
• Thranduil's great elk: the Mirkwood elk_rider career finally has an elk, at twice horse size, with an antler charge (one 60 blunt blow, lethal, credited to the rider). Thranduil, both Mirkwood cavalry troops and the Mirkwood lords ride it (lords on new campaigns). (#636)
• Animalia elk and moose from two Fab packs: reskinned onto the horse skeleton with 97 retargeted clips; Custom Battle test riders for now. (#646)
• Elephant, mûmakil, war ram and elk attack under a player rider too, like the warg. The blow and the kill are the rider's, the combat log names the right damage type, and the false "Crushed through!" is gone. (#643)
• The Nine are race nazghul: six loaded as human and three as uruk. Orc shield-crush, immortal, bald and clean-shaven. Three wraiths were defined twice and dressed at random; fixed for new campaigns. (#644)
• Nazgûl SCREAM: an overhead or side swing rings 6 m, deals 0.3x the hit, knocks back and drains up to 25 morale, 15 s cooldown, with Mike's scream clip. (#645)
• Two new spider mounts, Brown and Pale; the goblin Spider Rider line has three ranks on larger spiders. Spider and chariot hit capsules refit: spider 50% to 97% hittable, chariot 9% to 98%. (#616)
• The war ram's head-butt hits one foe for 40 to 50 through armour. Ram bardings take team colours. (#618)
• Ten creature mounts retuned: wargs 55 to 65 speed, rams 53 to 55, spider 65, elephant 30, mûmakil 20, chariot 70. (#615)
• Cave trolls move and fight with a new 52-clip animation set.
• Mid-battle freezes around wargs and spiders: a dead creature's engine slot was handed to the next horse spawned, so the game asked a horse to play the warg bite. Creature AI moved to the main thread. (#592, #595)
• Battle freeze in large field battles: six engine callbacks arrive off the main thread; every writer is parked or locked, and a freeze sampler (Mod Options, default on) logs the stuck frame. (#634)

**Combat**
• Cavalry knocks men down like vanilla again: a full gallop floors a man from any angle, charge damage differs by culture, and three new sliders apply immediately. Trolls, dwarves and Sauron resist. (#610)
• Culture doctrines, opt-in under Battle Tactics > Culture Doctrine: each kingdom fights field battles its own way. The Dwarven wall and the Elven ring race the enemy foot to the high ground, archers take the flank of the wall, infantry go for the infantry. (#608)
• Sauron's signature strikes: an overhead is a ground slam, a side swing a sweep, each on its own cooldown; toggle and cooldown multiplier under Combat Mechanics. Its mission gate sat in a callback the engine never fires for TAOM behaviours; three other behaviours had the same dead hook, the siege dismount among them. (#605, #606)
• Smart Cavalry AI forms a line, charges, rides through and comes again; a second F3 charges at once. Off by default. (#586)
• Career: cavalry mount bonuses now reach the mount, and typed damage resistances and extra javelins now apply. (#611, #613)
• Battlefield promotions work for every soldier race. Orc, uruk and goblin players never got an offer. (#612)

**Troops, armour and weapons**
• Every kingdom's armour rebuilt on one curve (one chest cap per kingdom at the elite band: Erebor 70, Rivendell 68, Gondor 57, down to Rohan 40); 2,510 items restated. (#581, #583)
• Low-tier troops no longer wear lord armour, and the lord line gets its stats back. (#609)
• Every archer tier has its own bow: damage, accuracy, skill and reach climb per tier and kingdom rank, so tier-2 militia no longer carry a 105-damage Noldor longbow. Hero bows are capped, and crossbows out-hit and out-aim their kingdom's bows at the same tier. (#582, #617)
• Melee ladder: troop tier barely changed melee damage (1.04x from tier 1 to 10 against vanilla's 2.23x over five tiers). Weapons restatted and 243 roster swaps: climb 2.68x, over-armed troops 244 to 0. (#631)
• Black Númenóreans spawn only for the two houses, Sauron and the vassal reward, and their cavalry ride a proper barding ladder, ten items on KEYforce's set. (#584, #602)
• Gondor elites cost Castar from level 41; Black Númenórean upkeep halved again. (#600)
• Level 41+ elites weigh 3.0 in Troop Weight instead of 2.0. (#585)
• Veteran militia stand 15 skill points above basic militia. (#588)
• Named lords field their own party: Faramir raises Ithilien rangers, Sauron a Black Númenórean and Uruk host. (#580)
• 238 empty equipment slots from the 1 Sep Gondor sword rebuild repointed, using Erkam's Armoury fix. (#568)
• Battles against Rhûn never finished loading (three longbows had no collision body), an elf start hung forever on the tournament loading screen after the 11 Sep bow art drop, and a new guard stops any battle or tournament loading forever on a missing collision body. (#633, #599, #601)

**Campaign and world**
• Wanderers refuse to serve a player of the opposite alignment. No more Aragorn in Mordor's service. (#575)
• Settlement nameplates show friend and enemy and are translucent again, with four Mod Options sliders. (#591, #596)
• Supply Lines: a search box on the order screen finds who stocks a good across every market. (#587)
• The encyclopedia troop tree badges troops that cost a special resource. (#590)
• Player starts hand out weaker starter versions of the culture's gear, the six cultures that started in Calradian gear start in their own, and career kits are each culture's lowest troop gear (33 of 40 careers had started in vanilla gear). (#569, #629)
• Four Gondor fiefs recruited the wrong vale. A castle with a household line offers only that line; towns run 60/40 instead of 80/20. (#598)
• Serelond, Methir and Framsburg have villages (3, 3 and 2), and Isengard has nine more. New campaign only. (#597, #562)
• Culture conversion re-mans the garrison and militia with the new culture's troops, like for like. (#632)
• Orcs and goblins overbreed: seven causes fixed (start-of-campaign children, 2x to 3x fertility, the race bonus beating the clan brake, fertile ages). Humans fertile to 60, not 195. (#628)
• Play as a Lord: taking over a non-leader makes you your clan's leader, and a vote already decided no longer freezes the game. (#550)
• Enlisted: no more Order of Battle screen or captaincy (that formation took no orders), and the party is no longer yanked out of a live battle at high fast-forward. (#576, #577)
• The map bar Extra Fast Forward button applies the multiplier. Only the E key did. (#574)
• 24 of TAOM's 36 overridden conversation lines had lost their culture and trait tags and matched everyone.
• Four siege scenes join Custom Battle: Westoft, Western Moria, Dol Amroth, Minas Morgul. (#603)
