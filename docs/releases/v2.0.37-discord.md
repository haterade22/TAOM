# TAOM v2.0.37 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.7

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.7** (Steam **beta** branch; the last note said v1.5.6). You must be on the v1.5.7
beta. The v2.0.36 build does not run on v1.5.7: a new campaign stays on the loading screen and never reaches the map.
This v2.0.37 build does not run on v1.5.6.

- **Not yet played on v1.5.7.** The automated checks pass, but nobody had started a campaign with this build on v1.5.7
  when this note was written. The first thing we need from you: start a new campaign and tell us whether you reach the
  map.
- **Modules:** all four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.37**. Install them
  together, from the same package. A TAOM with an older TAOM_Map can fail on a new campaign. With an older
  LOTRLOME_Armory the six dire wolf rider troops spawn on foot, and the dwarf attacks, the Lothlorien gear and the Snow
  Orc armour are missing.
- **Install into a clean folder.** Remove the old `Modules\TAOM` folder first. This build no longer ships eight screen
  files, and copying the new files over an old install leaves them in place.
- **Co-op:** do not start a co-op session on this build yet. The co-op mod must be rebuilt for v1.5.7 first.

## 💾 Saves

- ⚠️ **Start a new campaign.** The map gained five realms since v2.0.36 (Ered Luin, Anduin Vale, Angáladh, Dorwinion and
  South Rhovanion) with their settlements, and its distance cache was rebuilt with them. A v2.0.36 campaign is expected
  not to load (read from the game's code, not confirmed in game; if you try one, report what happens).
- **Only a new campaign has** the new realms, the Feral Orcs (an older save shows their 23 dens empty), the companion
  Elegost, the Lothlorien lord kits and clan parties, the face paint of Harad's lords, and the new realm colours of
  Lindon and Cardolan.
- **If an older save does load:** a hero with a Gondor career gets every career choice refunded once, back to the root
  (tier unlocks stay, and you see one message). Each named companion you have not recruited tells their backstory once
  on the next talk. The three goblin realms start at 0 War Spoils, and foreign upkeep troops there owe upkeep after one
  tick of grace, then desert. Lindon's new banners show with the old realm colours. The first time v1.5.7 loads the
  save, the base game removes dead heroes, and disabled heroes of other clans, from party and prisoner lists.

## 🗺️ Bannerlord v1.5.7

## ⚔️ Fords of War (new battle mode)

- Fords of War is a new single-player battle mode with its own option on the main menu. It needs no campaign. Pick a
  faction for each side and an AI difficulty (three levels). West is you and East is the AI.
- You play the commander: a free camera and a command panel. The keeps and clock sit on a slim strip at the top, the
  battle board at the bottom left, the buy grid and orders at the bottom centre, and the heroes at the bottom right. The
  top strip names the enemy faction in red.
- Three lanes cross the field. Each side has a chain of towers per lane that must fall in order. When a side loses every
  tower in one lane, its keep can be attacked. Destroy the enemy keep to win. The game returns to the main menu 25 s
  after the result.
- The hand-built Fords of War lane map is the only map, so the setup menu no longer asks for one.
- Thirteen factions: Gondor, Rohan, Mordor, Isengard, Rivendell, Erebor, Harad, Gundabad, Dale, Dol Guldur, the
  Easterlings, Umbar and Mirkwood. Each has its own feats, roster, strengths and weaknesses, shown during the countdown.
- Towers shoot back with volleys of javelins and arrows, and every third volley leads with a hurled rock that has a
  small blast. Towers no longer show bowmen on top, so your archers stop wasting shots on them.
- Towers have 8,000, 11,000 and 18,000 hit points and regenerate 1 hit point a second after 45 s without damage. A tower
  takes at most 50 damage a second from ordinary blows. Heroes and siege units ignore that limit, and it rises with a
  faction's tower damage feat.
- Spear and lance thrusts now damage towers. Before, most thrusts did 0 damage to a tower.
- Walls weaken in a long match. After minute 15, every blow on a tower or keep does more each minute (25% of the base
  per minute). The match feed tells you once.
- At minute 40 the side with more towers standing wins, then the side with the larger share of its hit points left. The
  feed warns five minutes before, and the result panel says time ran out.
- A lane whose towers have all fallen sends no more free waves for its owner. A bought pack still comes, so a comeback
  stays possible.
- Free waves march down each lane on a timer. Buy troop packs with F1 to F8 or the panel buttons. Factions with more
  than eight offers get a third row on F9 to F12. Kills, towers, heroes and held capture points pay gold.
- Every culture can now buy a foot spear pack, and the AI buys it too. Rohan and Rivendell get a new spear troop for
  this.
- Lane orders are Push, Hold, Fall back and Seize (key V). Seize sends a lane to its nearest open camp or capture point,
  holds it, then returns to Push. A lane charges only enemies in its own lane, except at the enemy keep.
- Neutral creature camps spawn on a third team, hostile to both sides, with a 15 m ring on the ground. Clearing a camp
  pays its reward to the killer's side.
- A new Upgrades panel buys three levels on six army tracks: melee damage, missile damage, melee armour, missile armour,
  siegecraft and fortify. Press U to hide it, and Shift+F1 to F6 or a click to buy. Upgrades reach units already on the
  field.
- Each side's next hero takes the field for free every five minutes, cheapest first, in the lane where its side is most
  outnumbered. You can still pay to field a hero early. Heroes deal 3 times the damage and take 0.4 times the damage.
- Every hero arrives with a guard of elite troops of its culture, at no extra cost. The guard waits for room in the
  lane.
- Press P while pointing at one of your soldiers to fight as that soldier. The match runs at 1x and your lanes keep
  their last orders. Press P again to hand the soldier back. If the soldier falls, you return to the commander view.
  Nobody has played this yet.
- Change match speed between 1x, 2x, 4x and 10x with the button in the top left corner or the L key.
- Harad's arrows and bolts poison: 3 hit points a second for 8 seconds. Dol Guldur's venom does 2 a second for 6
  seconds. Another hit refreshes the time but never stacks.
- In a Fords of War match, pierce blows (spears, pikes, arrows, bolts, javelins, couched lances) do double damage to
  riders and mounts. Outside Fords of War nothing changes.
- Lanes now march and close in their culture's formation. For example Isengard, Erebor, Gondor and the Easterlings close
  in a shield wall, Rohan marches in a wedge, and Mordor and Gundabad march loose.
- The AI commander follows each faction's own buy plan, saves for upgrades and beasts, and pulls a badly outmatched lane
  back under its own tower before it pushes again.
- Warg rage is off in Fords of War. Riderless mounts of every kind fade out after 20 seconds, unless a soldier holds or
  has chosen them. Thrown weapons are unlimited. Race ability messages no longer cover the battle board.
- The mode uses its own copies of its troops, so its balance changes never reach your campaign. The mumak costs 1,000
  gold.
- Prices, free waves and faction pros were set by several AI-against-AI round robins. Examples: Mordor's free waves
  bring 25 orcs, Rohan's riders cost 0.7 of the full price and its horse archers 0.55, and Dol Guldur now fields an uruk
  army. These are not final balance. A fifth AI round robin measured the shipped values: Gundabad won 11 of 12 matches
  and Rohan 10 of 12, Mordor won 1, and 40 of 75 matches ended on the time limit. Fixes are proposed but not in this
  build.
- Testers: with cheat mode on, taom.fords_start [west] [east] [aiProfile] [scene] starts a match from the main menu, and
  taom.fords_gold <amount> adds gold during a match.
- A tower or keep takes no more damage once the winner is named, so a keep can no longer fall under the "time ran out"
  panel. A tower's rock can knock down a soldier it does not kill.
- The riderless-mount rule above is the fix for a native crash seen about once in seven long matches. Only more long
  matches can confirm it.
- The Hard AI's income drops from 1.2 to 1.0.

## 🏰 The Long Defeat (new survival mode)

- The Long Defeat is a new mode on the main menu. You hold Helm's Deep against wave after wave of Isengard. Helm's Deep
  is the only fortress.
- A cleared wave pays kill bounties and a bonus. Between waves, the shop (B) sells packs of Rohan troops. The horn (H)
  starts the next wave early for bonus gold.
- You command the defenders with the order bar. If you die, you take over one of your soldiers, and you return at the
  keep when the wave ends. No wave retreats.
- The run ends when no defender stands, or when the attackers hold the keep point long enough. The run also ends as a
  loss ("The garrison has fallen") when the fight stalls with 5 or fewer defenders left and the attackers outnumber
  them. Your best run is saved in Documents.
- Attackers in this mode do not panic on low morale, so each wave fights to its last man.
- Ten waves are authored. After that, each extra wave gives attackers 5% more health and 3% more damage, up to three
  times. Every third extra wave moves each troop one tier up. The damage increase also hits the gate, the walls and
  shields.
- Wave numbers and prices are a first guess, not final balance.
- When a wave is cleared, every defender still standing gets back half of the health they are missing.
- A wave no longer stalls on its last few attackers. When 5 or fewer are left and the defenders outnumber them, those
  attackers fall after 60 s. Felled attackers pay no bounty.
- Speed controls: press M to cycle through 1x, 2x, 4x and 10x, or use the buttons in the shop. When you control your own
  soldier, 10x is a true 10x only at 100 frames per second (about 6x at 60).
- Each run starts with a class: Sword and Board, Two Handed Infantry, Archer or Cavalry. Your class sets your starting
  gear, which comes from your faction's own troops. A Cavalry rider starts mounted in the gear of the East Emnet
  Eorlingas Lancer.
- Every third cleared wave offers a boon: you pick 1 of 3 cards, and it lasts for the rest of the run. Some boons have a
  drawback, shown on the card. You earn one reroll per five cleared waves, and you can bank up to three. There are 34
  boons, including nine troop boons and three boons for each of the four classes.
- From wave 5, each wave gets named enemy powers: one power from wave 5, two from wave 10, three from wave 15. There are
  eight powers, from faster swings and better armour to stronger rams and towers (Engines of Orthanc), morale drain near
  attackers (Crebain from Dunland, never below 35) and poisoned blades (Fouled Blades). Between waves, the HUD names the
  next wave's powers.
- Every fourth wave brings an enemy commander: a captain at wave 4, a lieutenant at wave 8 and a general at wave 12.
  Later commander waves bring the general back, stronger each time. Every commander has a war cry. The lieutenant and
  the general also summon 20 Uruk-hai, plus 5 more each time they return. Killing a commander pays a rank bounty.
- Two fortifications are on sale between waves. Field care heals your standing defenders once per intermission, from the
  second intermission on. Reinforce the gate adds up to three levels of gate hit points. Repair the gate mends a damaged
  or broken gate, priced by how much is missing.
- The shop now has Reinforcement and Fortification tabs. Boon choices appear in a separate panel. A battle bar shows the
  B, H, M and N shortcuts.
- The end panel shows the defenders you lost in cleared waves and the name of the run's report file. That file is meant
  for a future leaderboard, which does not exist yet.
- Not yet played in game: the latest wave table, the shop with real clicks, the 10x speed with a person playing, and
  gate levels and repair. In unattended runs, 10x lost more defenders than 1x, and the cause is unknown. New text in
  this mode is English in all languages until the translation pass.

## 👁️ Seat of Seeing

- New free camera for battles. Take the Seat (default Q, rebindable) lifts the view off your hero. The movement keys,
  the wheel and the mouse fly it, and the same key brings it back. Your hero stands fast while the view is up, and the
  order panel works from the Seat.
- With the order panel open in the Seat, a cursor shows and orders land where it points. Hold the middle mouse button to
  turn the view. End hides the whole interface. Insert keeps the view on the soldier under the pointer, or, with the
  panel open and one company selected, on that company.
- From the Seat you can pause the battle, slow it to a half or a quarter, or speed it up through 2x and 4x, then 10x
  when the Seat is up or your hero has fallen.
- New optional settings, all off by default: your hero fights on while you command, and you take a soldier of your party
  when the hero falls (SeatHeroFightsOn); the view rises while the order panel is open (SeatRiseWithPanel); and Keep the
  order panel open.
- A manned siege engine or a board game refuses the Seat with a message. Fords of War leaves it off. In a co-op battle
  the Seat gives the camera only.
- Known limit: a click on a panel button in the Seat does not press it. It gives a Move order on the ground behind the
  button. Real keys, the mouse and the look of the camera still need a person's check.

## 📣 Marshal's Word

- Queue orders: hold Left Shift while you give an order with the order panel open, and the company does it after its
  current orders. Flags show the path. A normal order clears the queue.
- Several selected companies given one click or drag now move as one body and keep their places. Hold Left Alt to turn
  the group without stretching it. With Arrive Together on, nearer companies walk slower so the group arrives together.
- Volley fire: archers draw and hold until you give the word, or for 14 seconds at most. Massed fire looses by itself
  once most archers are holding. A mark tells a ranged company which enemy company to shoot. It does not steer melee
  soldiers.
- Hold the Line (Period) makes the shielded foot of a company in a shield wall, circle or square stop attacking and keep
  their places. A wounded soldier strikes back for a few seconds, and a charge, a retreat or a second press lets them
  fight.
- Javelin and axe throwers ordered to Advance now halt at throwing distance (24 to 32 m), throw, then charge. Setting:
  Throwers Throw First, on by default. It applies to your own companies in field battles only.
- The order panel gains a Marshal's Word set with the three new orders. Marshal's Word is off in co-op and multiplayer.
  Nobody has checked the panel in game yet, and the automated runs used custom battles only, not a campaign battle or a
  siege.

## 🪜 Staged Siege (off by default)

- Castle assaults on recorded scenes can be fought in stages. Attackers take point A outside the walls, B in the
  gatehouse, C in the courtyard and D near the keep. Each capture brings in the next share of both armies, and every
  casualty counts in the campaign.
- After point D the defenders fall back to the lord's hall. Hold its last room (point E) for about 20 seconds to win the
  hall and the castle.
- The first recorded scene is used by 17 castles, among them Caradhras, Hollin Gate, Krathol and Hammerdeep. Every other
  siege is unchanged. Staged sieges also run in custom battle at Ustokol Castle.
- A panel at the top shows the points and the capture progress, and flags take the colour of the side that holds them.
  AI-led trolls now go with their formation to the points, and break gates in stage B as before.
- Turn it on in the mod options: Staged Siege group under Battle Tactics. Co-op never runs it.
- The layout of the Staged Siege strip is fixed, and it now paints its own background. Nobody has looked at it in game
  yet.

## 🐺 Creatures, mounts and combat

**Creatures and mounts**

- Gundabad and the goblin tree (Goblin-town, Misty Mountain Orcs, Blue Craig) gain a three-step dire wolf rider line.
  Riders from level 26 cost 1 War Spoils to recruit or upgrade, with 0.05 daily upkeep. Goblin-town, the Misty Mountain
  Orcs and Blue Craig now earn War Spoils. Not tested in game.
- The dire wolf is about 25% larger than a warg in battle, and its rider sits in the saddle. Not tested in game.
- Hits no longer knock a rider off a warg, ram, elk, elephant, mumak, chariot or other creature mount. The damage still
  lands. Horses, camels and mules work as before. Not tested in a live battle.
- A rider on any creature mount now dismounts just before death. This guards against a native crash seen when a warg
  bite killed its target. Not tested in a live battle.
- Warg riders get their formation back after a rage in more cases: when they leave the warg mid-rage, when the warg
  dies, when a new rider mounts, or when the rage's target was already dead. Riders who join a battle late keep their
  formation too.
- A warg rage now really ends after 6 s with no attack. Rages end sooner, so this changes warg balance.

**Combat**

- Dwarves on foot (AI and companions) gain a head butt that staggers (18 damage, at most every 12 s) and a drop kick
  that knocks down (25 damage, at most every 20 s). A shield raised toward the dwarf halves the blow and stops the
  knockdown. Your own dwarf never uses them.
- A raised shield now blocks TAOM's scripted blows (troll Brute Force, war ram, elephant, mumak, elk, moose, signature
  strike rings) only when its bearer faces the attacker. Not tested in game.
- Trolls no longer try Brute Force when the Armory is missing its animation.

## 📜 Careers

- Gondor's three careers (Captain of Osgiliath, Ranger of Ithilien, Knight of Belfalas) now use a root and six forks.
  Each fork has two or three options, and you pick one per fork. You get a career point at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20 and
  25.
- The Gondor forks offer new kinds of effects: more damage for one troop class, elite troops that need no daily resource
  upkeep, double the realm's special resource, troops that resist dread and terror, hold their block against
  crush-throughs or resist knockdown, daily XP for one troop class, and a longer, wider career ability.
- Gondor balance numbers are drafts and have not been checked in game.
- The career system gains ten new campaign effects. Gondor's forks use three of them (influence, cheaper recruits, daily
  troop XP). No career uses the other seven yet (prisoner conversion, food use, wound healing, daily clan gold, gold
  from defeated parties, raid loot, trade prices). Co-op behaviour of these effects is not verified.
- The career screen's Active Effects panel now lists the root perk and the passives of keystone picks. The header shows
  the root perk's text. Mount health and inventory capacity bonuses now show as percentages.
- Ered Luin has one career per fighting style. The Warden of Thorin's Halls uses an axe and shield, the new Mattock
  Hewer of Belegost uses a two-handed axe and throwing axes, and the Firebeard Arbalester uses a crossbow. The Hewer's
  ability is Mattock Storm: +15% melee damage and +10% damage reduction for allies within 50 m for 8 s. Not tested in
  game.
- 42 cloned careers now show their ability icon on the career screen, where it was blank before. This still needs a
  check in game.
- If you switch away from a hero, that hero's lord's gear quest is now cancelled, because it could never finish. The
  hero can start that step again on return.
- The career perk tooltip's frame now draws behind its content instead of over it. This still needs a check in game.
- New career text is English in all languages until the translation pass.

## 🏴 Bandits

- New bandit faction: the Feral Orcs, masterless orcs and goblins with 23 dens from the Ered Luin borders to Dale, South
  Rhun and Dorwinion. A new game fills a random seven of them. Their gear is a mix of stolen Mordor, goblin, Gundabad
  and Dol Guldur pieces.
- The Feral Orcs have five troops: goblin snaga and archer, orc raider, Black Uruk brute and a warchief who guards each
  den. Snagas fight with a one-handed mace and a small Mordor shield.
- Feral Orcs never rout only when Culture Doctrine is on in MCM, and it is off by default. Their stolen armour does not
  change the tier of those items in markets, loot or the armoury.
- Fixed a possible crash when a bandit clan and its dens do not load together. A clan with no dens now raids nothing,
  and an empty den with no clan stays empty.
- Co-op: spider brood and troll band spawns now run only on the host or dedicated server, not on every client. Not
  tested in a co-op session.

## 💰 Special resources and the Elite Emissary

- New MCM option Elite Troops Cost Gold Only, in a new Special Resources group, off by default. With it on, no troop
  costs a special resource to upgrade, recruit or keep, and none desert for lack of one. Gold, XP and wages still apply.
- With that option on, the Elite Emissary sells for gold, priced by the new Emissary Gold Per Resource setting (default
  200, range 50 to 1000). It sells only to a buyer whose special resource matches the settlement owner's, and never more
  troops than the party has room for. A full party sees the offer greyed out.
- With that option on, the two career perks for upkeep and upgrade cost do nothing, and elephant and Mumakil upkeep is
  0. In co-op each player has their own setting, but the server charges by its own value.
- All 11 special resources now pay the same flat amounts: 250 per siege victory, 100 per raid and 50 per battle victory
  (base). Before, the amount depended on the faction.
- None of these changes has been played in game. The new text is English in all languages until the translation pass.

## 🎖️ Enlistment, battles and Supply Lines

- Fixed a crash to desktop after an enlisted player won a battle and captured a lord. The battle now closes normally and
  service continues. Not tested in game.
- A battle you enter while enlisted without your commander now pays kill XP, merit and spoils. The service menu no
  longer covers the battle menu during a fight. Not tested in game.
- Meeting a party that has no leader and no men now shows a menu with a Leave option, instead of crashing. That menu
  text is English until the translation pass. Not tested in game, because no known way forces an empty party.
- If your commander's battle cannot be joined and the army merge did not land, an enlisted player already in a battle
  now stays in it. The game no longer leaves the army or parks your party. Not tested in game.
- Meeting a supply caravan on the map now opens a short menu with a Leave option, instead of ending the encounter while
  it is still being built. This does not prove a fix for the reported crash when an Order Supplies delivery arrives. If
  you get that crash, please send a crash bundle.
- Supply caravans now follow the same land routes as lord parties, so they no longer cross mountains, lakes and rivers.
  Two possible supply-route crashes are guarded. The original crash reports were from Bannerlord 1.4.8 and were not
  reproduced on 1.5.6. The in-game checks are still owed.
- Alignment desertion now skips a party or garrison during a battle and resumes on the next day.
- Testers: the console command taom.service_status now also shows the settlement siege state and who the map camera is
  following.

## 🏆 Tournaments

- The default tournament bet cap per round is now 15,000 (it was 0, meaning unlimited). You can raise the MCM slider to
  1,000,000, or set it to 0 for unlimited.

## 🖥️ Screens

**Campaign and encyclopedia screens**

- New themed campaign screens: illustrated campaign navigation, the Two Trees clock, expandable stats, and themed escape
  and campaign options screens. Not yet checked in game, except the escape menu background, which the maintainer
  confirmed.
- The encyclopedia has new lists and detail pages: stone panels, live portrait and banner previews, settlement
  illustrations, paged clan, fief and relationship galleries, and a horizontal family tree. The new labels are English
  in every language until translation. Not yet checked in game.
- The troop upgrade and recruit pop-up shows the tooltip that lists the horses an upgrade uses again. It also gets back
  the game's header layout, list and key-hint placement. TAOM's close button stays.

**Faster screens (Shadowfax Screens)**

- The clan, inventory and trade, party and quest log screens open with less wasted work. Each has its own switch in a
  new "Screen Performance" group on the Battle Load Diagnostics settings page: "Clan Fast Open", "Inventory Fast Open",
  "Party Fast Open" and "Quest Log Fast Open". All four are on by default.
- The clan screen builds its party list once instead of three times. Clan Fast Open is off during a live co-op session.
- The inventory and trade screens skip a name sort that the screen replaces a moment later. The party screen builds each
  troop portrait once per screen.
- The quest log no longer builds every finished quest when it opens. A finished quest builds its stages when you select
  it. If its guard patches fail to attach, the screen falls back to the game's normal build.
- "Log Screen Open Times" (off by default) writes one line per screen open to taom_debug.log. None of these speed-ups
  has been measured or tested in game yet.

## 🛡️ Troops and gear

**Lothlorien**

- Lothlorien no longer recruits Rivendell's troops. It has three trees of its own: Regular Lorien (T4 to T7),
  Marchwardens (T5 to T8) and Galadhrim (T6 to T9), 28 troops in all, plus a tavern mercenary. Its militia, patrols,
  armies, rebels, crown guard (the Mallorn Defender), enlistment rosters and career kits now come from these trees too.
- Lothlorien villagers are their own troop now, and they upgrade into Lorien Scouts. Every Lorien lord wears Lorien
  armour in battle, but civilian outfits still use Rivendell items. Galadriel wears her own pieces, Celeborn fights
  mounted with a glaive and bow, and both have higher skills than the T9 troops.
- Shield troops no longer carry a two-handed sword that the AI would never draw. Seven lord-only armour pieces are no
  longer sold in markets. Lorien bows are now named "[Lorien] Longbow" with a tier numeral.
- Lothlorien has its own armour ladder. Its lord material has a placeholder name and uses Mirkwood's bar until new art
  is made. Lothlorien also joins the Elite Emissary: its key settlement is town_L1, with six offers at 28 and 18 Elven
  Wine.
- The Marchwarden captain Thandirion joins Galadriel and Celeborn in the Lothlorien custom battle commander list. The
  Lorien practice dummy no longer appears in custom battle.
- Needs a full restart and a new campaign. Not tested in game. The new troop and item names are English in every
  language for now.

**Gundabad: Snow Orcs of Mount Gram**

- New Snow Orc troop tree for Gundabad: 13 troops, from the T1 Recruit to the T5 Blackshields, with infantry, spearmen,
  archers and wolf riders, plus a new snow orc kit. Mount Gram and the Grishuk clan recruit them. A Gundabad career
  start keeps the pale uruk kit, not the orc-only snow kit. Not tested in game.

**Arnor elite troops**

- Four new elite troops, from player feedback, all reached only by upgrades. Arthedain: the Knight of Annuminas
  (cavalry, after the Lancer), the Evendim Warden (poleaxe, no shield, after the Warden of Fornost) and the Dunedain
  Steel Bowman (after the Ranger Captain, with a new sixth Arthedain bow). Cardolan: the Tharbad Bridge Guard (pike, no
  shield), a second upgrade of the Veteran Barrow Guard.
- AI Arthedain and Cardolan lords also upgrade into these troops, and volunteers can grow into them. None of them costs
  a special resource. The Arthedain faction screen now shows the King's Guard of Fornost.
- Needs a full restart. Not tested in game. The new names are English in every language for now.

**Troop gear fixes**

- Black Uruk troops carry the mix of weapons they were meant to have again. The Barad-Dur Guard no longer carries two
  copies of the same halberd, and its sets now vary between Halberds IX, VI and XII. The Shield Guard has a spare
  halberd again. Every Grunt set now has a polearm and a sword or axe. Before, about one Grunt in 14 spawned with a
  lance and no sidearm. The Fords of War Barad-Dur Guard gets the same fix. Not tested in game.
- Known limit: the Shield Guard still opens with Gundabad Spear III in all four sets.
- Three Isengard starter boots no longer hide the leg. They show the shin again, the same as the shoes.

**Harad war paint**

- Harad villagers, notables, wanderers, troops and lords now wear war paint by yotthani: 8 designs for men and 7 for
  women. The face editor offers these designs and 12 new tattoo colours to every human player. Umbar stays clean-faced.
- Known limits: the longer colour list changes existing tattoo colours for other cultures, including Rhun troops and 270
  lord entries. The face editor tiles for the new designs show the wrong thumbnails, but the paint on the head is
  correct. Not tested in game.
- Lorien greaves no longer show a bare shin: all eight had the wrong leg cover flag. The same flag is now set for the
  Isengard and Mordor leg pieces. This needs the matching LOTRLOME_Armory package and a full restart. Not tested in
  game.

## 🕷️ Arachnophobia Mode

- New setting "Arachnophobia Mode" in a new Accessibility group, off by default. When you turn it on, spider riders ride
  brown, dark or fell wargs instead. Volunteers, upgrades and the Elite Emissary no longer lead to spider troops, and
  spider troops leave the encyclopedia and the Custom Battle list. Spider broods stop spawning, and broods already on
  the map disband at the next daily tick.
- Spider troops already in your save stay until they die. They ride wargs but keep the name Spider Rider. Fords of War
  still has its Spider Riders, on wargs. Lore text and some scene cobwebs stay. When you turn the mode off, the spiders
  come back at the next load. In co-op, every player needs the same value. Not tested in game.

## ⛓️ Warden's Tally

- A town or castle dungeon menu now shows how full the dungeon is, how many of its prisoners are lords, and each lord's
  daily chance to escape. Where they apply, it breaks the chance down into the base chance, the halving for a dungeon
  your clan holds, each governor perk, and any War Chronicle escape boost. A lord who cannot escape (one waiting for
  execution) is named as such.
- Setting "Show Warden's Tally" (World/Warden's Tally group, on by default) hides it. A co-op client does not see the
  halving or the boost. The new lines are English in every language for now.

## 🤝 Co-op

- Co-op guests no longer change shared world state. A guest who clicks a refuge gets the normal meeting and cannot
  found, upgrade or dismantle it. A guest cannot place Supply Lines orders. A guest joins tournaments the normal way,
  with no prize swap or skill reward. A guest cannot send alliance proposals or make paid career changes, and gets no
  join-kingdom offer after character creation.
- Career quests no longer give a co-op guest the item, renown or influence reward. Tier unlocks still apply. A guest who
  meets the host's supply caravan now sees that a caravan passes on toward its lord.
- In co-op, upgrading a troop into an elite now costs its special resource, as in single player. Only the server
  charges. Known limits: the guest sees the lower balance only at the next join, and a guest can pay full price because
  the server may not know the guest's career discount.
- Known limit: Player Possession is still not restricted for guests. None of these co-op changes has been tested in a
  real co-op session.

## ⚠️ Known limits

- **Nothing here has been played on Bannerlord v1.5.7.** The new features were checked on v1.5.6, most of them by
  automated tests and unattended matches. Where a line above says "not tested in game", no person has seen it work.
- New text since v2.0.36 is English in all 12 languages until the translation pass.
- **New realms:** the Angáladh card on the faction map covers the wrong part of the map, and the careers of Anduin Vale
  and Angáladh show a blank portrait.
- **Helm's Deep:** the scene's inner gate was restored after an editor rework dropped it. No battle has run on the
  restored gate yet. Report any error after the outer gate breaks, in The Long Defeat or in a campaign siege of Helm's
  Deep.
- **The Long Defeat:** at Helm's Deep the Elven Volley and the tower archers stay hidden until their places are
  recorded. Helm's Deep has no sally point yet, so a Cavalry run rides from the start inside the walls, and a lord whose
  horse dies fights on foot. The armoury and the engineer's yard sell nothing yet.
- **Fords of War:** two native crashes seen in test matches have no fix yet: one where the AI reads a target that is
  gone (seen once) and one at the end of a match (seen once in 75 matches).
- **Staged Siege:** when the hall is taken, finish the defenders still standing, or step away from them, before you
  leave the battle. In custom battle there is no lord's hall: taking D wins the battle for the attacker.
- **Arachnophobia Mode:** a brood in a battle, or one that holds a hero prisoner, waits until it can leave. After you
  turn the mode off, broods return at one a day.
- **Creatures:** a rider who survives a dying creature skips the game's fall action, and a creature whose rider dies
  skips its move-away step. How that looks is unknown until a battle is played. A prisoner recruit of a dire wolf rider
  costs no War Spoils. A base-game harness with reins on a dire wolf may crash the game (read from the code, not seen in
  game).
