# :crown: Play as an Existing Lord

Every campaign begins with a nobody. You name a clan nobody has heard of, design a banner nobody flies, and spend forty hours earning the right to matter. **Player Switcher** offers the other opening: pick a lord who already matters, and play the campaign as him.

## :dart: What it does

**1. A panel at the face generator.** Pick your culture and character creation lists that culture's lords in three groups: **the ruling house, the clan leaders, and the wanderers**. Children, notables and townsfolk are not offered, and nobody appears twice.

**2. Click one and you see him.** The model becomes that lord: his race, his face, his own war gear, animated rather than standing in a bind pose. Race and gender controls grey out while a lord is previewed. Click him again and your own character comes straight back, exactly as you left him.

**3. Take over a house.** Choose a ruler or a clan leader and you *are* him on the map. His face, his gear, his skills. His clan, with its family, its renown and its tier already earned. His fiefs. His kingdom, and his throne if he sat on one. The character you built is set aside.

**4. Or adopt a wanderer.** Choose a wanderer and it runs the other way: he joins **your** clan, under the name you chose and the banner you designed, and you lead it. You start independent, and you are asked once whether to bring the clan into your culture's kingdom. Only 20 of 39 cultures have wanderers to offer.

**5. The backstory questions get out of the way.** Pick a lord and creation goes straight from the face generator to the career choice. Those six childhood and youth questions only ever shaped the character being set aside, so you no longer fill in a questionnaire for someone about to be discarded. Your career is the one answer that carries across, and it lands on the lord. Deselect him and the full questionnaire is back.

**6. Yes, you can play Sauron.** He and the Nazgûl sit on their cultures' lists behind a toggle that ships off. Read the caveat below before you flip it.

## :gear: Mod Options :arrow_right: Player Switcher

- **Enable Player Switcher** (on). Off means the panel never loads and character creation is untouched.
- **Include Wanderers** (on).
- **Allow Sauron and the Nazgûl** (off).
- **Carry Over Starting Gold** (off). An established lord is already funded, so stacking a startup package on him is a balance change rather than a fix.

> **Caveats, plainly.**
> Playing a dark lord costs you his capture immunity. TAOM's rule that Sauron and the Nine can never be taken prisoner steps aside for whichever character *you* control, so a player-controlled Witch-king can be beaten, captured and ransomed like any other lord. That is the trade the toggle is asking you to make.
> Pressing **Back** from the career menu walks you back through the auto-answered backstory menus one at a time instead of returning to the picker.
> A clan you take over is holding parties that were sized while the AI still ran it, so a large kingdom can start with far more men under arms than its own limits allow and shed the difference over the following days. Gondor sees this most.
> This runs during character creation only. Existing saves are untouched, and a campaign started this way saves and loads like any other.
