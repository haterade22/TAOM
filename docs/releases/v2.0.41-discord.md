# TAOM v2.0.41

This note covers the changes since v2.0.40.

## ⚠️ Before you install

- **Bannerlord v1.5.7 only** (Steam **beta** branch), the same as v2.0.40. If your game is on another version, TAOM
  now says so in a popup at the main menu and stays switched off, instead of failing with the engine's error box.
- **Modules:** TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM.CoopCompat, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory all read **v2.0.41**. Install
  them together, and take TAOM and TAOM_Map from the same package.
- **Install into a clean folder.** Remove the old `Modules\TAOM` folder first, then copy the new one in.
- **Saves:** nobody has loaded a v2.0.40 campaign on this build yet. Some changes reach new campaigns only: Gimli's new
  axe, the Harad clan tiers and the new lord armour. A running campaign keeps what its heroes and lords already wear.
- **Settings:** the TAOM mod options now set the rules for **new campaigns only**. A new campaign picks its main rules
  on the Advanced Start Options screen and keeps them in the save. A campaign started before this version still
  follows the mod options. No existing setting changed its default.

## ⚔️ Battles

- **Combat stamina (off by default):** every swing, shot, throw, jump, kick, shield bash and blocked blow costs
  stamina, and running drains it. A tired soldier is slower, and an exhausted one cannot attack, block or run until
  he recovers. Each people has its own pool: orcs run out sooner than dwarves. Turn it on under
  **Battle Balance / Stamina / Enable Combat Stamina**.
- **Cleaving Blows:** your two-handed swing with an axe, mace, sword or polearm can cut through one enemy and strike
  the next, for less damage. Thrusts, bashes, horse charges and swings from horseback do not cleave.
- Orc soldiers shout with the Uruk battle voice.
- Tournaments use each culture's own weapons, not Calradian ones.
- When a battle load compiles 50 or more shaders, a counter shows the progress, so the game does not look frozen.
- New cheat options: God mode in the battle cheat list, an "Unlimited party" switch, a searchable list of your
  faction's troops and a screen with every troop. Co-op refuses God mode and the Unlimited party switch.

## 🏰 Aragorn, Legolas and Gimli

- **The Long Defeat:** the shop sells Aragorn (300 gold), Legolas and Gimli (250 each) between waves. Each has
  800 health, timed abilities and heavy blows. Legolas shoots with his ranged kit, steps back from melee and draws
  his sword when cornered.
- Gimli fights with the Erebor two-handed axe.
- **Helm's Deep story mission:** the three heroes now stand on the walls with the garrison. The offer now follows
  your side of the war, so a Mordor or Harad player is no longer asked to defend the Hornburg.
- In the Long Defeat at Helm's Deep, the Cavalry class rides out from the slope outside the Deeping Wall.

## 🗺️ Campaign

- **Campaign rules on the new-game screen:** War of the Ring or Sandbox, the war's start date (Early, Standard,
  Late or Custom), and Middle-earth rules such as Troop Weight, Eye of Sauron hunters and an Alignment preset.
- Free and Shadow realms that are at peace before the War of the Ring no longer act as friends. Each side has a
  switch under **World / Hostile Peace**.
- Tavern mercenaries now follow alignment desertion, the same as volunteers and prisoners.
- Four new Realms options: whether your realm and AI realms take their title's name, and which banner they fly. A
  coronation no longer replaces your clan banner unless you choose the title banner.
- The Make Camp button says why it is blocked.
- Armies stuck in a finished battle march again, a save stuck after a battle-end crash loads again, and parties
  stranded at sea move back to land.

## 🛡️ Troops and balance

- Troop skills now follow a ten-tier curve close to vanilla. A tier 10 troop had 390 in its main weapon; vanilla's
  best noble troop has 260.
- Under-armed troops carry a stronger weapon of the same kind, and elite troops no longer wear recruit armour.
- Lords wear varied armour close to the best troops of their culture, with new lord looks for Mirkwood and Harad.
- Heavy bracers, greaves and pauldrons can no longer roll down to almost no armour.
- Harad's clans start at tier 5 in a new campaign. Dunland refuses calls to war on Gondor. Blue Craig fights only
  its neighbours, and its garrisons need no gold or food.

## 🐘 Mûmakil and elephants

- The war elephant and the mûmak each walk with their own gait, and their feet keep pace with the ground.
- The archers on a mûmak or an elephant howdah stay on their seats and shoot while the beast moves. Nobody has
  checked this in a played battle yet.

## 🎨 Banners and screens

- New culture selection texts for 19 cultures.
- New banners for Arthedain, Nos Peredhel, Greenwood and its neighbours, the Anduin Vale houses and Haradwaith, and
  10 new banner icons. Four icons that an earlier build lost are back, and old saves show them again.
- Clans and lords of eight realms, among them Arthedain, Cardolan, Dale and Dorwinion, take Realms in Exile names.
- Realm names on the map have a size setting.
- For players who type Chinese, Japanese or Korean, map and battle hotkeys no longer open the input method window.
  Option: **Map UI / Text Input**.

## 🤝 Co-op

- A Long Defeat run can be hosted and joined in a BannerlordCoop session, when the installed Coop has the session
  support it needs.
- A guest can buy from the Elite Emissary and improve gear at a town armoury.
- Nobody has played these with two clients on this build yet.

## 🌐 Translations

- Simplified and Traditional Chinese now use the Chinese team's own text, so the separate TAOM_CN module is no
  longer needed.
- The TAOM pages of the Mod Options menu, and many battle, siege and screen texts, can now be translated. Their new
  rows are in English in the other languages until the next translation pass. The renamed Realms in Exile names are
  also in English there until then.
