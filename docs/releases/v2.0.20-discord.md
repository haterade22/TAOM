# TAOM v2.0.20

## ⚔️ Enlist: take a lord's coin and serve in the ranks

Every campaign has started the same way: you are nobody, and the only ladder out is *lead your own warband*. There has never been a way to simply **soldier**. Now there is. Find a lord in the field, swear the oath, and you serve in his column as a common soldier.

- **Your term runs 365 days.** Your own troops and companions don't disband; they march with the column out of sight, and come straight back to you the moment service ends.
- **Paid from his purse, not thin air.** 5 / 8 / 14 / 22 gold a day by rank. If his coffers run dry the pay defers into arrears and he settles up when he's flush again. Desert and you forfeit every copper of it.
- **Four ranks:** Recruit, Soldier, Veteran, Sergeant. Promotion is checked at the end of the day *and* right after a battle, so the fight that earns your last stripe promotes you on the spot.
- **Pick your place in the line:** Infantry, Archer, Cavalry or Support. You now stand in formation with the rest of the company instead of off on your own, and your assignment decides your training and which duties you're offered.
- **Battles are scored on how you actually fought,** not just that you turned up: kills (capped, so farming stragglers won't carry you), whether you held formation, and whether you fought the way your assignment asks.
- **27 duties and camp incidents.** 13 field duties, 11 in-camp jobs resolved on a skill check, and 3 incidents where the pay is late or the rations are short and you decide how to handle it. What you get offered depends on what the column is actually doing.
- **Draw your kit from the quartermaster,** once per rank, and it's *your faction's* kit rather than Calradian hand-me-downs. 84 authored sets: every culture at all four ranks, plus a neutral fallback.
- **The company looks after its own.** You eat from the commander's stores, share the column's morale, and get seen by its surgeon.
- **Losing your commander is a moment.** If he falls or is taken you're told properly, and you get a grace period to find your feet rather than being cut loose mid-march.
- Off switch in **Mod Options → Enlistment**. Turning it off mid-service discharges you honourably instead of stranding you.

## 🎖️ Battlefield Promotions

The axeman who has carried your line through six battles is, mechanically, identical to the one you recruited yesterday. Now he can be raised into a **named companion**, keeping the gear he fought in and a skill sheet that fits his level, so you get a credible veteran rather than an instant superhero.

- Merit is earned **only in fights you won without massively outnumbering** the enemy, tracked per troop type.
- Declining costs you nothing. The merit stays banked and you'll be asked again later.
- Human, dwarf and elf only. No promoting a cave troll into your clan.
- Full controls in **Mod Options → Battlefield Promotions**.

## 🏰 Cultures & Kingdoms

- **Blue Craig and Lindon are real cultures now.** Both shipped as kingdoms running on somebody else's culture, so picking Blue Craig actually started you in Goblin-town and picking Lindon started you in Rivendell, with borrowed troops, names, banners and feats. Both now have their own troop trees, lords, equipment, wanderers, careers and starting settlements. Lindon is Círdan's Falathrim of the Grey Havens, not a Rivendell reskin.
- **Nine kingdoms were quietly fielding Calradian troops.** Imperial infantry patrolled Dunland and Vlandian crossbowmen patrolled Rohan. The same gap ran through villagers, militia, rebels and caravans, not just patrols.
- **Every culture's lords were being dressed as Dunlendings,** and an Uruk-hai lord could turn up wearing a Calradian face.
- **Rohan recruits were issued Gondor gloves.**
- **Khand's Variags** held the region on the map and none of its culture.

## 🏹 Troops & Gear

- **Dale's spearmen never actually used their spears.** All four Dale spears counted as strictly two-handed, so the 18 troops carrying one alongside a shield drew their sidearm the instant combat started. The spear stayed visible right up until it mattered.
- **16 more troop kits had the same problem,** pikes and halberds paired with shields. They now carry weapons the AI will actually fight with.
- **Settlement militia was being deleted back down to about 20 every day** while the overlay cheerfully reported +5/day. Both numbers were honest, which is why it went unnoticed.
- **Armour tiers respect modifiers now,** so a legendary low-tier piece stops out-armouring a plain high-tier one.

## 🧙 Character Creation & Careers

- **The two orc cultures were selectable and then handed you nothing:** no backstory, no starting gear, no career. Their 96 backstory lines had never been wired up.
- **A Gundabad career you could see, pick, and get nothing from,** plus the last two cultures with no careers at all. Every selectable culture now has its own.
- **New career screen.** A diamond layout with an icon on every choice and an Active Effects panel showing exactly what you're running. Tiers read as bronze, silver and gold. Centred properly on ultrawide monitors.
- **Career balance pass.** Troop-damage bonuses came down from 3-20% to a 2-8% band (at the old values your own troops mostly just burned villages faster), and max-health bonuses from 25-100 HP down to 5-10. The health bonus also only ever worked in battle; it now shows and applies on the campaign map too.

## 💥 Crashes & Bugs Fixed

Female dwarves crashing the game on sight · a kingdom that owns no settlements crashing the daily tick · the tournament winner panel · warg bites · a completed hunt duty killing the process · characters rendering lying flat in every menu portrait · the career screen collapsing when you level up · Isengard Uruk-hai showing as black silhouettes · named companions with nowhere to call home · a one-race host turning every hero in the world human.

## ⚡ Performance

- **Roughly 4.8 GB of memory freed** by sizing art to what the screen actually shows. Banner sigils were held from launch to exit at far higher resolution than any panel displays them (2,624 MB down to 128 MB), and faction culture art was the same story (2,474 MB down to 78 MB). Quality goes **up**, not down: the game was shrinking those images on the fly before.
- Log spam cut hard across battles, tournaments and boot.

## 🌐 Co-op & Dedicated Servers

- **TAOM now recognises BannerlordCoop and stands down** instead of fighting it. Before this, TAOM was stripping the co-op mod's own changes out from under it.
- **Dedicated servers crashed on boot** from 168 stray entries in the Armory module. Fixed, and servers now receive TAOM's files without a hand-copy.
- ⚠️ **Co-op is still not verified end to end.** These are the fixes that stop TAOM breaking it. They are not a promise that it works.

## 🌍 Localisation

- **317 lines of text that no translator could reach** are now registered and translated into all 12 languages. 258 of them were the cultural feats shown on the culture screen and in the encyclopedia, for every culture in the game. They rendered fine in English, which is exactly why nobody spotted them.
- Enlistment, plus the orc and goblin backstories, translated across all 12 languages.
- Four shipped strings had garbled characters. Fixed.

## 🎮 Compatibility

- **Bannerlord v1.4.8 supported.** Nothing in TAOM needed changing for it.
- The bundled dependency module moved to **v2.0.6**, and a mismatched pair is now blocked at the launcher instead of surfacing later as characters stuck in a T-pose.

## 🔨 In Progress

- **Lond Cirion,** Gondor's harbour city. Walls, gatehouse, barracks and harbour ships are built in the kit, not yet in a playable build.
- **Install size.** A packaged build can drop around 93 GB of editor-only data (147 GB down to 55 GB in testing). Measured, not shipped yet.
