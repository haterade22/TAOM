# TAOM v2.0.43

This note covers the changes since v2.0.42.

## ⚠️ Before you install

- **Bannerlord v1.5.7 only** (Steam **beta** branch), the same as v2.0.42.
- **Modules:** TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory all read **v2.0.43**. Install them together.
- **Co-op:** every player and the server must run the same TAOM and TAOM.CoopCompat, both v2.0.43. An older build is
  refused at join.
- **Saves:** a v2.0.42 campaign loads. Isengard's higher clan tiers reach new campaigns only.
- **Settings:** no existing setting changed its default. The new switches below start at their defaults.

## 🏰 The Long Defeat

- **Minas Tirith** is the second fortress. You defend the first circle as Imrahil, with Gondor against Mordor's
  waves. The boons there carry Gondor names, and the Cavalry class reads as a knight of Dol Amroth.
- **Normal or Hard:** after you pick the fortress, choose a difficulty. Hard sends a quarter more attackers in every
  wave (138 in wave 1 instead of 110), the endless waves grow stronger faster, and the attackers get their first power
  two waves sooner. Each difficulty keeps its own best run. Every leaderboard starts fresh.
- When your lord falls, he now rises at the keep in his own body at the next cleared wave. The soldier you took over
  goes back to the AI.
- Gear you buy at the armoury goes onto your lord where he stands, and it refills his arrows and mends his shield.
- Legolas speaks one of his lines when he joins the defence.

## 📜 Story missions

- **The Breaking of the Fellowship:** with "Offer Story Missions" on, the War Chronicle offers the fight at Parth
  Galen for 10 days. Stand with the Fellowship and lose the moment Boromir falls, or lead 20 Uruk-hai and win the
  moment he falls. Free players defend, evil players hunt, neutral players choose.
- **Helm's Deep:** the Hornburg offer now has both battles. Evil players can storm the walls, with a host of 350
  Uruk-hai and men of Isengard. Nobody fights their own realm.

## ⚔️ Training Grounds

- In English the town arena is now the **Training Grounds**, and its keeper is the **Training Master**.
- **Wave fights:** wager on yourself and fight up to eight waves of the town's fighters with practice weapons. A
  cleared wave pays 1.5 times the wager at first and 2 times from the sixth wave. Fall or leave, and that wave's
  wager stays with the town. Each town runs the waves once a day. Your hero is never wounded or killed. MCM switch:
  Wave Fights at the Training Grounds (on).
- **Team practice:** in towns of the Free Peoples the practice fight is a team fight, you and two allies against four.
  Towns of the Shadow keep the brawl. MCM switch: Team Practice Fights (on).

## ⛏️ Erebor war chariots

The Ironpass ram line has a second branch. A Ram Vanguard can become an **Ironpass War Chariot** (level 41), two
armoured war rams and a dwarf with spear, shield and axe. It upgrades to the **Ironpass Steel Chariot** (level 46),
which flies two pennants in your colours. The upgrades cost 5 and 7 Gems, and both troops take 3 party slots. The
Erebor emissary sells them. The two horses of the Rhûn Wainrider chariot are now the same size.

## 🗣️ Voices and dialogue

- Male elves fight, shout orders and die in their own elf voice, recorded by TierOneTemplar.
- Sauron, Saruman and the Nine greet you, demand surrender, yield and take leave in their own words.
- The ruler of each realm speaks in the realm's own voice when you ask for release from homage, end a mercenary
  contract, or take a vassal or mercenary offer.

## ⚖️ Balance

- **Isengard** fields bigger parties and armies. Its level 31 Uruk veterans, berserkers, Orthanc guards, warg riders
  and twin-blades count as one man against the party limit, not two. Uruk-hai Legions gives +10% party size, up from
  +5%. Ten Isengard clans go up one tier (new campaigns only).
- Elite Emissary prices no longer cost 20 times too much for the income of small fights.

## ✨ New and changed

- **Battle Gear Loot** (MCM, off by default): when you win, beaten enemies drop the gear they fought in, each piece at
  the chance you set (default 10%). Heavy, elite and lord armour, named weapons and creature mounts never drop.
- The faction screen shows every faction's character in 3D, and the model changes to the hero, lord or wanderer you
  click.
- The army panel stands above the campaign stats plate in the TAOM theme, no longer hidden under it.
- "Enable Birth and Aging" is on the new-campaign options screen (on by default), so you no longer need a separate
  module for it.
- Caravan troops, militia and named characters show their culture in the name, such as [Gondor] Caravan Guard.
- The giant spider walks, trots, charges, takes hits and dies on its own animations, and it no longer slides in a
  charge.
- A new Lórien item, the Lord's Crown A.

## 🤝 Co-op

- Your TAOM career reaches the co-op server and stays in its save, so it is there when you join again and other
  players see it.
- Co-op players find their culture's lord's materials after a won battle, and the armoury lists and climbs the lord's
  gear ladder.
- A player whose mods load in another order than the host's gets a warning at join. MCM switch in the new Co-op group
  (on).
- Guests keep TAOM's banners on join, and the themed map bar and Camp icon show in co-op.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- Clicking a battle that had half ended no longer crashes the game.
- Dwarf attacks no longer hang a battle.
- Clanless wives no longer cause an error on the daily tick (thanks to MinimalCash63).
- Promoted companions keep the gear you gave them.
- Each character creation choice gives its own skills.
- TAOM popups pause the campaign map.
- Unlimited party also stops upkeep desertion.
- Wargs and dire wolves keep their legs in pace at every gait, and the dire wolf steps backward when it backs up.
- Orc villagers always greet you as Orcs.
- Goblin, orc and Gundabad wanderers wear the gear of their own race, Mordor's three human wanderers no longer wear
  orc gear, and non-human troops no longer borrow human civilian clothes.
- A bribed or bought-off hunter of the Eye stops the chase for the paid time.
- Ability effects stay out of the training yard and tournaments.
- Many name and text fixes: Tolkien spellings, Lothlórien's accent, troop names, career keystones.

## 🌐 Translations

Some new text (the Long Defeat difficulty and Minas Tirith lines, the new dialogue, Battle Gear Loot and other new
settings) shows in English in every language until the next translation pass.
