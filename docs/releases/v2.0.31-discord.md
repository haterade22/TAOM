# TAOM v2.0.31 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.3 and a new campaign required

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam **beta** branch). All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies,
TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.31**. **Start a new campaign:** a Ringlo Vale troop was removed from
Gondor's roster, so a save that holds one can break, and the new spider broods exist only in a campaign started
with this version.

The tester checklist, posts 1 to 5: [v2.0.31-tester-checklist.md](v2.0.31-tester-checklist.md).

## 🕷️ Spider broods of Mirkwood (new)

Up to four spider broods, led by a pale broodmother, roam Mirkwood as bandits. Meeting one goes straight to
attack or leave. In battle each spider fights with no rider, hunting, biting and pouncing, and your troops target
and kill it like any enemy. Spiders are never recruited or taken prisoner; the ridden spider is unchanged.
The new MCM group **Creature Bandits** tunes them (**Spawn Spider Broods** is on; off stops new broods). Their
four names are English in every language for now.

## 🧌 Hill trolls and Brute Force

Mordor fields a new **Hill Troll**, far bigger than the cave troll, on its own skeleton. Both trolls have 200 HP,
cost 50 war spoils to recruit, stand wide apart in formation, never carry a banner, and fight with **Brute
Force**: a two-handed smash that knocks back and floors every enemy in a ring ahead, unless they block with a
shield.

## 👻 The Nine

All nine Nazgûl are now the **Nazgûl race**, and three of them no longer draw a random Mordor lord kit. The Nine
have a signature of their own, the **Scream**: a shriek that pushes back everyone around the wraith and shakes
their morale.

## 🐘 Beasts

- The **war elephant** is 30% bigger, and its howdah deck now rides the elephant's back as it walks.
- The **Mûmakil** carries archers on its war tower, crow's nest included, and they shoot from it.
- **Mirkwood** rides into battle on elk: Béleglas on the great elk with its antler charge, Rôchenlas on the new
  elk, and Thranduil and his lords on the moose.
- Spiders and chariots can be hit almost anywhere on the body.

## 🏰 Gondor and Umbar

The Lamedon and Ringlo Vale lines are re-kitted, and the Ringlo Vale Guardsman, Spearman and Warden fight with a
two-handed poleaxe. Lossarnach's noble line is now its axemen, the Blackroot Vale bowmen are renamed, and Gondor
has new heavy and light banner spears. Gondor's noble lines carry better kit than regular troops of their level.
Several Umbar troops carry new swords.

## ⚔️ Troops and starts

- Higher-tier melee troops now hit clearly harder than recruits.
- Crossbows out-hit and out-aim the bows of their kingdom at the same tier.
- A career start gives you the lowest troop gear of your culture.

## 🗺️ Campaign

- When a fief converts culture, its **garrison and militia** now convert too, man for man (three new settings
  under **Culture Conversion**, all on).
- The Order of Battle **Assign Heroes** button now places your companions as captains.
- Orc and goblin births are held back by the clan population brake.
- A second campaign in one session no longer inherits the first one's special resources or enlistment state.

## 🛡️ Art

Every Armory mesh now has six levels of detail, which should make big battles lighter. Gondor's large town
buildings and the Isildur statue on the campaign map are updated.

## 🔧 Stability

- A battle freeze caused by engine callbacks arriving off the main thread is guarded, and a new freeze sampler
  (on by default) logs what was stuck.
- One broken patch no longer stops the whole mod from loading; a red on-screen line names it instead.
- Crash reports now carry the exact build, and both crash capture toggles work without a restart.
- Shader pre-compilation is switched off again, and its settings are gone.
