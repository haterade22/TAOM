# TAOM v2.0.33 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.3 and a new campaign required

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam **beta** branch). All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies,
TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.33**. **Start a new campaign:** the wild troll bands exist only in a
campaign started with this version, and taking over a lord happens at character creation. If you installed
Kysaro's separate **TAOM_FactionUI** module, remove it: its screens are now part of TAOM, and both patch the same
places.

Everything in the v2.0.32 tester build is still here ([v2.0.32 notes](v2.0.32-discord.md)). What is new since then:

## ⚔️ Race and culture abilities (new)

AI soldiers now fight with a battle ability of their race, or for men, of their culture. A soldier waits for the
moment (wounded in melee, a kill, cavalry closing, a target in bow range, kin falling), fires the ability for a few
seconds, and kin nearby whose ability is ready join in, so a whole line braces or a pack goes berserk at once. Each
ability comes round every one to two minutes. Elite troops hit harder and pay more; lords most of all.

- **Races:** berserkers go berserk (every swing breaks a block, no guard, then spent), Uruk-hai fall into
  bloodlust (kills heal and lengthen it), dwarves stand fast (nothing crushes through them), elves quicken (draw,
  aim and feet), orcs swarm (stronger the bigger the mob), goblins scurry, Mordor's uruks hold with iron
  discipline, Gundabad's run their prey down and Dol Guldur's spread dread.
- **Cultures:** Gondor closes ranks, Rohan's riders spur on, Dale aims true, Dunland rages, Harad's arrows bite,
  Rhûn locks its wall, Umbar's corsairs press, Khand's Variags fight savage and the Shadow's men frighten.
- **Seen across the field:** while a fury, guard or dread ability is active, the soldier wears a coloured outline
  (red for fury, steel blue for a guard, violet for dread), and sparks burst on the soldier who fires it. Only the
  40 soldiers nearest the camera are outlined, and Hide Battle UI hides it.
- Works in campaign battles and in Custom Battle. MCM **Battle Tactics > Race Abilities** has the switches: the
  abilities, war cries, a message line for big waves, the glow and sparks, and a debug log.

## 🏆 Tournaments

- At **Join** you choose one of three prizes, then the combat skill the tournament trains: every round you win
  gives experience in it, and winning gives more.
- A winner's renown grows with the lords and heroes in the field, a win in a town of the winner's own kingdom
  earns influence, and both scale with the winner's culture.
- Bets are no longer capped at 150 a round: MCM **Tournaments > Max Bet Per Round** (0, the default, is unlimited).
- Prizes are light, medium or heavy gear and nothing above: a tournament with fewer than four lords awards light
  or medium kit, a bigger one heavy armour or a Tier 4 weapon.
- Troll weapons and armour can no longer be bought, won as a prize or forged at the smithy.

## 👑 A new front door, and play as the lord you pick

Kysaro's themed front end is now part of TAOM: the splash, an animated main menu with its own video and music,
painted loading screens, his skill icons and character-creation screens, and his faction and hero picker. The
first loading screen shows TAOM's own background, and the galloping horse in the corner of every loading screen
is now the One Ring's inscription turning in gold.

Picking a lord or lady of your faction on the faction screen now makes you that character: you go to the career
choice and into the campaign in their own clan and kingdom, with their gear, skills, fiefs and treasury. Legends,
wanderers, troops, Aragorn and Gimli, and Haldir and Bolg are still copied instead, and Sauron and the Nine need
Player Switcher's "Allow Sauron and the Nazgul". Keep the default start: the other Advanced Starting Options
starts still replace a taken-over lord's gold, and some their gear or kingdom.

## 🗺️ Realm borders (new)

Every kingdom's border is now drawn on the campaign map and moves with the war: a captured castle, a clan changing
sides or a rebellion redraws it within a frame or two. Borders follow rivers, coasts and mountain ranges, each
realm's land is tinted in its colour, and realm names are lettered across their lands. Zoom all the way out and a
parchment map of Middle-earth fades in with the borders drawn on it.

- **G** switches between the realms, the Free Peoples against the Shadow, and your allies and enemies; **M** hides
  and shows the borders (both rebindable under Keybindings > Campaign Map).
- A message tells you when your party rides into another realm.
- MCM **Realm Borders** has every look control, a colour for each realm and one for **Your Realm**.
- Nothing is saved, so existing campaigns get the borders too. With the Kingdom Borders mod loaded, TAOM's
  borders stand aside.

## 🛡️ Armour and the town armoury (new)

Heavy, elite and lord armour is no longer on every stall. Markets and battle loot still hand out light and medium
armour, but a town sells heavy pieces only at armoury (Barracks) level 1 or more, elite at 2 and lord kit at 3.

- **Visit the armoury** in a town menu upgrades a piece you carry into the next class of its kingdom's kit, for
  gold and metals; lord kit also costs the kingdom's special resource.
- **The lord's gear ladder:** a town armoury lists six rungs (hands, legs, shoulders, head, body, weapon). Each
  rung opens a quest done by your hero's own deeds, or is done at once by handing over your culture's lord's
  materials, and a done rung is claimed at a level 3 armoury. The weapon rung awards your culture's named weapons
  where it has them.
- **Lord's materials,** one for each of thirteen cultures, turn up after battles won and from a new village quest,
  "The Deep Seam".
- Twenty-seven hero weapons and shields are named, Andúril, Glamdring, Galadriel's sword and Tuor's axes among
  them: never sold, never looted.
- A master armourer sometimes visits a town for a week and raises its armoury a level.
- The numbers are first guesses: tell us what feels off.

## 🧌 Creatures

- **Wild troll bands:** two to four hill trolls roam as bandits, about one band per kingdom. They wear no armour
  but have 300 hit points and take 70% of every hit. Meeting a band goes straight to attack or leave, and a troll
  is never taken prisoner. MCM **Creature Bandits > Spawn Troll Bands** (on).
- **Spider broods** now reach twenty and spread over every town, castle and village of Mirkwood and Dol Guldur.
- **KEYforce's new art:** a new war elephant with three plain armours and three howdahs (every howdah carries its
  platform and two archers), and new giant spiders. The Brown Spider is now the **Great Spider**.
- **Goblin spider riders:** Goblin-town, the Misty Mountain Orcs and Blue Craig field a new Spider Rider and
  Spider Lord on mountain spiders, a second upgrade from the Lurker.

## 🏹 Custom Battle

- **Sieges load again.** Since v2.0.30 a Custom Battle siege assault never started with Siege Dismount on its
  default setting.
- No more crash on Start when a side picks Nord, Vakken or Darshi: the faction list shows the 22 TAOM factions.
- Mordor, Gondor and Rohan field their full commanders: Sauron, the Witch-king and the Nazgûl, Boromir, Faramir,
  Imrahil, Théoden and his captains. Khamûl heads Dol Guldur's list.
- Each formation defaults to the culture's own troop where one fits (Rohan's infantry used to be the Vlandian
  Swordsman).
- **[Rohan] Edoras** joins the siege scenes.

## 🏰 Campaign and troops

- Field battles load terrain that fits where you fight: plains in the green kingdoms, desert in Harad, steppe in
  Rhûn and Khand, swamp in the Dead Marshes. TAOM's own field scenes, Mordor's and Rohan's among them, are back.
- **Saruman the White** now heads Isengard, and Saruman and Sauron have their own faces.
- The **Mouth of Sauron** wears his own helm and gear.
- The Uruk-hai **Champion** and **Berserker** now wear the Berserker Skirt as body armour: about 90 more armour
  each, and slower for its weight.
- A fief converting culture no longer turns its garrison's cavalry into spider riders.
- **Battle corpses** fade 60 seconds after a body settles, at most 25 at a time, in field battles, sieges and
  sally-outs (Mod Options > TAOM > Performance > Battle Corpses). This is aimed at the mid-battle freezes some of
  you reported. On the first main menu TAOM offers to lower Number of Ragdolls to 5 and Number of Corpses to Low
  if yours are higher; nothing changes unless you click Apply.
- The Load Game screen's hero preview no longer shows the rider lying flat.

## 🌍 Translations

- About 4,600 more names and texts (characters, heroes, cultures, careers and battle scenes) are now translated
  into all 12 languages.
- In a language other than English, 313 strings that were already translated no longer show in English, the
  Abanissa and Shaghana culture names, clans and notables among them.
- Still English in every language for now: the race ability names and wave messages, and the tournament prize
  and training dialogs.

## ⚡ Performance

- Loading a campaign or a Custom Battle should be faster: TAOM now merges the modules' XML in one pass. That merge
  took about 28 of the 50 seconds of a new campaign's loading screen on the dev PC; tell us your load times.
- Battles and the campaign map do less work per blow, per frame and per party.
- A slow battle frame now writes a [Hitch] line to taom_debug.log saying where the time went: send the log with
  any stutter report.
