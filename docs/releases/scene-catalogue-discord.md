# :european_castle: What Middle-earth actually looks like right now

The map ships **45 scene folders**. Here is the part that is wired and live: **94 settlements** load a hand-built scene, and **8 field-battle terrains** sit under the campaign map. Everything below is reachable in a normal campaign today.

## :classical_building: Cities and strongholds

Seven settlements have their own centre scene. This is what loads when you walk in, and what a siege is fought over.

- **Bar Melui**
- **East Osgiliath**
- **Helm's Deep**
- **Minas Morgul**
- **Minas Tirith**
- **Orthanc**
- **West Osgiliath**

## :shield: Castles

Three castle scenes carry **16 fiefs** between them.

- **Barad Faen** (6): Amonost, Caras Tolfalas, Edhellond, Harlond, Linhir, Morlad
- **Bergraw** (5): Bar-en-Siril, Cair Andros, Erethir, Methrast, Méreharn
- **Melgobas** (5): Barad Harn, Glanhir, Hyarpendë, Min-Rimmon, Tumladen

## :ear_of_rice: Villages

Four village scenes cover **66 villages** across Gondor, from the Pelennor out to Anfalas and the Ringló vale.

- **Gondor Village A** (17 villages)
- **Camloth** (17 villages)
- **Dinadab** (16 villages)
- **Mereham** (16 villages)

## :crossed_swords: Arenas and taverns

- **Mordor arena**, shared by 5 towns: Barad Dûr, Durthang, Naerband, Seregost, Thaurband
- **Minas Morgul** has its own arena *and* its own tavern, the only settlement with a bespoke interior on both

## :fire: Field battles

Fight in the open in the right place and you get a purpose-built map instead of a Calradian one. Eight terrains are keyed to the campaign map:

- **Mordor Plains I, II, III and IV**, swamp, covering the whole ash plain
- **Black Gate**, swamp, on the two cells at the Morannon
- **Dead Marshes**, swamp
- **Rohan Plains**, plain
- **Fords of Isen**, plain

These are added to the vanilla terrain pool rather than replacing it, so a battle on those cells rolls between the TAOM scene and the stock one.

## :video_game: Custom Battle

**22 TAOM scenes** are pickable from the Custom Battle menu without starting a campaign: **10 sieges** and **12 field maps**. That includes **Goblin Town**, which is the one scene you can only reach this way. It has no settlement on the campaign map yet.
