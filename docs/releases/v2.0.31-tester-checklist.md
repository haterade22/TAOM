## Post 1 of 5

````markdown
# 🧪 TAOM v2.0.31 Tester Build: what to test (1/5)
This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam beta). All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map, LOTRLOME_Armory) must read **v2.0.31** in the launcher. If one reads v2.0.30, reinstall it before testing.

Posts 1 to 5 are a checklist. Pick a section, work through it, report what fails. You do not need to do all of it.

## ⚠️ Before you start
☐ **Start a new campaign.** A Gondor troop (Ringlo Vale) was removed, so older saves can break. Old saves are not supported in this build.
☐ The Shader Precompilation settings are gone from MCM. That is expected: the feature is parked.
☐ Culture Conversion has three new MCM settings, all ON by default (see post 4).

## 📋 How to report
- One report per problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened. Say your culture, and whether it was Custom Battle or campaign.
- **Crash:** send the whole TAOM crash report folder. It now records the exact build, so we can match it to the code.
- **Freeze** (game stops, no crash window): wait a full minute before you kill it, then send taom_debug.log. A new freeze sampler writes down what was stuck at 10, 20 and 40 seconds.
- Screenshots for anything visual: wrong armour, floating, clipping, missing or pink textures.

## 🔥 Priority 1: stability
☐ Start a campaign as a Free Peoples culture and as an evil one. You reach the map, can save, and can reload that save.
☐ Start a **second** new campaign without quitting the game. Your special resources (Castar, war spoils and so on) start fresh, and nothing from the first campaign's enlistment carries over.
☐ Fight long field battles (200+ troops) with creatures on the field (trolls, elephants, spiders, wargs). Report any mid-battle freeze.
☐ Watch the screen on game start and campaign load for a **red message saying a patch failed**. Screenshot it: it names the broken part, and the rest of the mod keeps running.
☐ A battle or tournament that **never finishes loading** is critical. Note your culture and the location.
☐ Note campaign map load time and whether menus feel sluggish.
````

# 🐉 Creatures (2/5)
Custom Battle is the fastest place to test most of this.

## 🧌 Trolls (new: Hill Troll)
☐ **Hill Troll** (Mordor, level 51, troll mace) spawns, walks, fights and dies without a crash. It is far bigger than the cave troll and has its own skeleton.
☐ **Armored Troll** (cave troll) and Hill Troll both have **200 HP**.
☐ **Brute Force:** with enemies close in front, a troll does a two-handed smash. Enemies in a ring ahead take a blunt hit, are knocked back and knocked down unless they block with a shield. It must never hit the troll's own side.
☐ Trolls in formation stand wide apart and do not clip into each other.
☐ Trolls never carry a banner, in campaign or Custom Battle.
☐ Hill troll idle, cheer, inventory and conversation poses (report stiff or jumping loops).
☐ Campaign: the Mordor clans Bolgrukig, Zarunik and Brughash field a few of each troll. Recruiting one costs 50 war spoils.
☐ **A crash to desktop with trolls in battle is top priority.** Send the crash folder and taom_debug.log.

## 👻 The Nine
☐ All nine Nazgûl (the Witch-King, Khamûl, the Knight of Umbar and the rest) use the **Nazgûl race**: taller, own model, not a man or an orc.
☐ The Tainted, the Shadow of the Northmen and the Shadow of Umbar always wear Nazgûl kit, never a random Mordor lord kit.
☐ Still a Nazgûl after save and reload. Still cannot be taken prisoner.
☐ **Scream:** an overhead or side swing sets off a shriek you can hear, pushing back everyone within about 6 m and hitting their morale. Knock-back only, no knockdown, once per 15 s. A thrust does nothing.
☐ Nazgûl portraits, encyclopedia and Load Game thumbnail look right (not lying flat, not blank).

## 🐘 War elephant and Mûmakil
☐ The **Elephant Rider**'s elephant is 30% bigger. Howdah archers sit on the deck (not sunk into it, not floating) and keep shooting while it walks.
☐ **Mumakil Rider**: the archers on every deck of the tower, crow's nest included, stay on it and shoot. None fall to the ground.
☐ Blows and arrows hit almost anywhere on both beasts.

## 🦌 Elk and moose (new)
☐ **Mirkwood Béleglas** rides the great elk. Its antler charge hits and hurts enemies.
☐ **Mirkwood Rôchenlas** rides the new elk; Thranduil and the Mirkwood lords ride the moose. Check walk, trot, gallop, idle, rearing, hit reactions and death.
☐ The Mirkwood elk rider career start gives you an elk.

## 🕷️ Spider broods (new, new campaign only)
☐ Broods (a pale broodmother and her spiders) appear around Mirkwood within a few days and patrol there, never more than four.
☐ Meeting a brood goes straight to attack or leave, with no conversation. On the map it shows as a spider alone.
☐ In battle each spider hunts, bites, pounces and swipes, with no rider. Your soldiers and archers target and kill it.
☐ With 20 or more troops you get the deployment screen: the spiders stand still until you start the battle.
☐ The battle ends normally, the scoreboard lists the spiders and their kills, and no spider becomes your prisoner.
☐ MCM **Creature Bandits**: turning **Spawn Spider Broods** off stops new broods.

## 🕷️ Spiders and chariots
☐ Spider Rider, Brown Spider Rider, Pale Spider Rider: each rank rides a bigger spider.
☐ Arrows and blows hit spiders and chariots almost anywhere on the body.
````

# 🗺️ Campaign systems (4/5)

## 🏳️ Culture conversion: garrisons change too (new)
When a fief converts to its owner's culture, its **garrison** and **militia** are now replaced with the new culture's troops of a similar tier and role.
☐ Look at a garrison before and after a conversion. The headcount stays the same: no free troops, no lost troops, wounded stay wounded.
☐ Troops come from what that culture can recruit (a Lothlórien fief gets the Rivendell line). Report any practice dummy, arena fighter or guard showing up in a garrison.
☐ Prisoners and heroes in the fief are left alone.
☐ Your own fiefs convert too, unless you switch off **Replace Garrison In Your Own Fiefs**.
☐ Switching off **Replace Garrison On Conversion** or **Replace Militia On Conversion** stops that part.

## 🎖️ Order of Battle: Assign Heroes (new)
☐ As army leader, before a battle, press **Assign Heroes**. Your companions become captains of formations that suit them, and a message says how many were placed.
☐ It never assigns you, a hero already leading a formation, or an empty formation.

## 💰 Special resources
☐ Balances show and spend correctly. Trolls cost 50 war spoils to recruit, and upkeep is charged.
☐ Gondor elites cost Castar from level 41.

## 👶 Orc and goblin populations
☐ After 20 or more in-game years, orc and goblin clans should not balloon in size. Report a clan that looks absurdly large.

## 🪖 Enlistment
☐ Enlist, serve, get discharged, then start a new campaign in the same session: no leftover commander, offers or timers.
☐ Enlisted service survives save and reload.

## 👑 Lords and the Player Switcher
☐ Taking over a lord still works, and you lead your clan.
☐ Take over a Nazgûl: still a Nazgûl after reload.
☐ Kingdom votes open and close normally, including one vote straight after another.
````

# 🎨 Art, map and translations (5/5)

## 🛡️ Armory: new levels of detail
Every Armory mesh now has six levels of detail. Big battles should run smoother. A bad level shows up as:

☐ Items invisible up close, or pink or missing textures.
☐ Beards and hair that look wrong at any distance.
☐ The Dol Guldur Uruk chest armour (freshly re-exported).
☐ Note your battle FPS in a 500+ troop battle compared with v2.0.30, if you remember it. A rough number helps.

## 🗺️ Campaign map
☐ Gondor's large town buildings and the Isildur statue look right on the map.
☐ Rock cliffs are textured, not flat grey.
☐ Settlement names show in your language.

Play a while in your own language and report:
☐ Any English text, "ERROR" text, or a name that belongs to a different troop.
