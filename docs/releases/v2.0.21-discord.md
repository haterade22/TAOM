# TAOM v2.0.21

## 👁️ Aura of Unnatural Dread

The Ringwraiths have fought like heavy infantry in a black cloak. Their weapon was a sword, when it should have been fear. Now it is.

- **Stand in front of a Nazgûl and your line breaks.** Morale drains from every enemy near him, and when the first men go, vanilla's own panic spreads it outward through the ranks.
- **It takes real exposure, not a glance.** A line infantryman holds about half a minute of contact. A veteran holds nearly a minute. You can walk a formation out of it.
- **Elves and dwarves resist.** Elves take 40% of the dread, dwarves 50%.
- **An elven hero never breaks at all.** Not a special case in the code, and not a hand-placed exception: elven resistance plus hero resistance drops the drain below the rate the game regenerates morale, so it simply never wins. Send Glorfindel.
- **Veterans hold better than levies**, using the same resistance curve the game already applies to morale from kills, so a wraith is no more arbitrary than an ordinary rout.
- **Sauron carries it too**, further and harder than the Nine.
- **You are immune.** The player never breaks, and never could: the engine gives player-controlled characters no morale to drain.
- Off switch and tuning in **Mod Options → Aura of Dread**: radius, drain rate, and whether it touches your own troops. The sliders take effect immediately, on wraiths already on the field.

Battle only. Wraiths do not project dread on the campaign map, and hideouts, arenas and tournaments are untouched.

## 🔧 Under the hood

- The dread feature ships with 161 tests, including a pinned balance table: if someone retunes the drain, the tests that assert "a line infantryman breaks in about 28 seconds" go red and force the doc to move with it.
- Three defects were caught and fixed in review before this shipped. The worst: toggling the aura off and back on mid-battle would have delivered the entire off-period as one hit and routed every enemy in range instantly.
- Documentation counts that two different co-op docs quoted are now derived by reflection and asserted, so they can no longer drift apart.
