# TAOM v2.0.30

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.3 required

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam **beta** branch). On v1.5.3, v2.0.29 threw an error on every
party nameplate on the campaign map: the engine's new ship-banner widgets were missing from TAOM's
copies of the nameplate layouts. Both layouts are rebuilt on the v1.5.3 files, with TAOM's changes
re-applied. All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.30**.

## ⚠️ One setting to change by hand

**Combat Mechanics → Auto-Knockdown Weight Ratio** now defaults to **6** (it was 8), so a horse and rider
at full gallop knock a man down from any angle, as vanilla does. MCM keeps the value you already have,
so **existing players stay on 8** until they move the slider to 6.

## 🐎 Cavalry charges like vanilla again

Charges knock men down the way vanilla does: a rider never pushes through a line worse than vanilla
allows, and a full gallop floors a man from any angle. Charge damage now differs by culture, through
each culture's horses and bardings. Trolls are hard to knock over, dwarves and
Sauron still resist, and three new charge sliders under Combat Mechanics take effect immediately.

## 🛡️ Culture doctrines (opt-in, off by default)

Each kingdom can fight field battles its own way: its own tactics, formation behaviours, morale and
readiness to rout. The Dwarven shield wall and the Elven ring race the enemy foot to the high ground,
archers take the flanks of the wall, and infantry go for the enemy's infantry. It is **off by default**
while it is tested. Turn it on under **Battle Tactics → Culture Doctrine**.

## 🔥 Sauron's signature strikes

Sauron fights with a ground slam and a sweeping strike, each with its own cooldown. The **Signature
Strikes** toggle and a cooldown multiplier (0.5x to 5x) live under Combat Mechanics.

## 🏹 Every archer tier has its own bow

Archers and crossbowmen now carry bows made for their tier and kingdom. Damage, accuracy and reach
climb tier by tier, the stronger archer kingdoms rank above the weaker ones at the same tier, and each
troop's Bow skill matches its bow. The bows lords and companions carry are capped at a hero ceiling.

## 🕷️ Spiders

Two new spider mounts, the **Brown Spider** and the **Pale Spider**. The goblin Spider Rider line now
has three ranks, each on a larger spider.

## 🐏 War ram and 🐘 war elephant

- The Dwarven **war ram** has a head-butt of its own. It hits **one** enemy for 40 to 50 damage, and
  armour does not stop it.
- **Ram bardings** take your team's colours.
- The **war elephant**'s collision now fits its body. Its neck, legs, trunk and haunches were so thin
  to weapons that blows and arrows passed through them; now nearly all of the elephant can be hit, and
  soldiers bump into its whole body instead of passing through its head and rump. The Mûmakil shares its
  skeleton and gets the same hit fix.
- Ten **creature mounts** get retuned speed, handling and charge.

## 🧌 Cave trolls

Cave trolls move and fight with a new animation set.

## 🏰 Custom Battle

Four siege scenes join the Custom Battle picker: **Westoft**, **Western Moria**, **Dol Amroth** and
**Minas Morgul**.

## ⚙️ Armour and troops

- Low-tier troops no longer wear lord armour, and the lord line gets its armour values back.
- **Black Númenórean** cavalry ride on a proper ladder of bardings.
- Gondor's elite troops cost Castar from level 41, and Black Númenórean upkeep is halved again.
- Promoting a soldier to companion works for every soldier race.
- Career cavalry bonuses now apply to your mount, and career damage resistances and extra javelins
  now apply.

## 🌍 Translations

Troop, lord, clan and kingdom names are translated into all twelve languages, and the Advanced
Starting Options descriptions are back in every language.

## 🔧 Fixes

- An **elf start** loads again after the recent elven bow art update.
- A guard stops a battle or tournament from loading forever when an item names a collision body the
  art no longer ships.
- **Taking over a lord** with the Player Switcher makes you your clan's leader, and a kingdom vote that
  was already decided no longer freezes the game.
