# TAOM v2.0.32 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.3 and a new campaign required

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.3** (Steam **beta** branch). All four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies,
TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.32**. **Start a new campaign:** the new spider broods exist only in a
campaign started with this version.

Everything in the v2.0.31 tester build is still here ([v2.0.31 notes](v2.0.31-discord.md)). What is new since then:

## 🕷️ Spider broods of Mirkwood (new)

Up to four spider broods, led by a pale broodmother, roam Mirkwood as bandits. Meeting one goes straight to
attack or leave, and on the map a brood shows as a spider. In battle each spider fights with no rider, hunting,
biting and pouncing, and your troops target and kill it like any enemy. Spiders count on the battle scoreboard,
are never recruited and are never taken prisoner. The ridden spider mount is unchanged.

The new MCM group **Creature Bandits** tunes them: **Spawn Spider Broods** (on; off stops new broods, and broods
already on the map stay), hit points, how many soldiers each bite, pounce and swipe can hit, damage, crit-only
knockdown, cooldowns, and the damage spiders take. Their four names are English in every language for now.

## 🧌 Troops

- The **Hill Troll** now swings its own war hammer instead of the cave troll's mace.
- The **[Test] Animalia Elk Rider** and **Moose Rider** are gone from the Custom Battle troop list. Rôchenlas
  still rides the elk, and Thranduil and the Mirkwood lords the moose.
