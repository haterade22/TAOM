# TAOM v2.0.38

This note covers only the changes since v2.0.37. Everything from 28 October to v2.0.37 (Fords of War, The Long
Defeat, the new realms, the Bannerlord v1.5.7 update and more) is in the separate "everything since 28 October"
post.

## ⚠️ Before you install

- **Bannerlord v1.5.7 only** (Steam **beta** branch), the same as v2.0.37. This build does not run on v1.5.6.
- **Modules:** all four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.38**. Install
  them together, and take TAOM and TAOM_Map from the same package.
- **Install into a clean folder.** Remove the old `Modules\TAOM` folder first, then copy the new one in.
- **Saves:** a v2.0.37 campaign should load. One change below (new-campaign friendships) applies to new campaigns
  only.
- **Settings:** one new MCM switch, **World / Kingdom Allegiance**, is on by default. No existing setting changed its
  default, so nothing needs a reset.
- **Co-op:** do not start a co-op session on this build yet. The co-op mod still needs its v1.5.7 rebuild.

## ⚔️ Battle HUD

- The base game's health bars are gone. A compact circular HUD replaces them: your health, plus a horse ring and a
  shield ring that show only while you have a horse or a shield.
- The centre of the HUD shows your career ability's charge, its icon and the key that triggers it.
- The weapon previews are hidden. Your ammo count now sits in a small capsule with a quiver or javelin icon.
- Not yet checked in game: the ammo count while you switch between ranged weapons, and the low-ammo alert.

## 🛡️ Kingdom Allegiance

- Orc lords no longer turn up serving Gondor. An AI clan of the Free Peoples no longer joins, defects to or hires on
  as mercenaries with an Evil realm, and an Evil clan no longer does so with a Free realm.
- Neutral clans and realms still deal with either side.
- A new campaign starts with no friendships between Free and Evil lords. Each one becomes an enmity of the same
  strength.
- Your own clan, and any realm you rule, keep the base game's freedom. Nothing stops you.
- Clans that already serve a realm of the other side in an existing save stay where they are. The rule only stops new
  joins.
- You can turn it off in MCM: **World / Kingdom Allegiance**. No restart needed.

## 🤝 Sworn Anew

- A captured lord is now judged by their own people, not by the realm they serve.
- An orc lord who served Gondor can now swear to an Evil realm, and no longer to a Free one.
- A Free lord who served a Neutral realm can no longer swear to Mordor.
- A Neutral lord, such as a Khand clan serving Mordor, can now be asked by any player.
- Lords whose clan and realm are on the same side keep their old answer.

## 🔧 Behind the scenes

- Fixes to the 3D retopology tools we use to prepare models, and a packaged copy of that toolkit. These do not
  change the game.
- Test and documentation updates.

## 🌍 Translations

The new Kingdom Allegiance settings text is in English in all 12 languages until the next translation pass.
