# TAOM v2.0.42

This note covers the changes since v2.0.41. Public players who installed the v2.0.41 hotfix of
21 November already have these changes; v2.0.42 gives that build its own version number.

## ⚠️ Before you install

- **Bannerlord v1.5.7 only** (Steam **beta** branch), the same as v2.0.41.
- **Modules:** TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory all read **v2.0.42**. Install them together.
- **Saves:** a v2.0.41 campaign loads. A save that holds stranded parties of eliminated clans releases them when it
  loads.
- **Settings:** no setting changed.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- Fixed a crash after a clan was eliminated: "X has been eliminated" showed, then the game crashed, and the clan's
  leftover parties stood still on the map and could not be attacked. Those parties are now released, also in a
  save where they already exist.
- An enlisted soldier's commander now stays in his siege after a sally-out or relief battle, so you are no longer
  pulled out of the siege with him.

## 💰 Caravans

- Caravan guards now earn a regular wage, not the 1.5x mercenary wage. A Gondor escort of 70 men costs about
  488 gold a day instead of 732. This applies to every caravan, AI caravans included.
- Caravans of your clan move 15% faster ("Caravan pack train" in the speed tooltip). In co-op, players' caravans do
  not get this yet.

## 🌐 Translations

The "Caravan pack train" label is in English in every language until the next translation pass.

Thanks to REDACTEDSTUFF for all three changes.
