# :crown: Realms: the titles of Middle-earth

In Bannerlord a castle belongs to whoever took it last, and nobody remembers whose it was. Denethor is a faction leader with nothing to his name. **Realms** gives every fief and every realm a title: a holder by right, a line of heirs, and claims from everyone who wants it.

## :dart: What it does

**1. A title over all the land.** Lordships, Baronies and Counties over the villages, castles and towns. **111 Duchies** drawn from the regions of the map. The Kingdoms, and above them **Arnor, Durin's Folk, Rhovanion and the Misty Mountains**. Gondor holds Fiefs, Dol Amroth is a **Princedom**, elves hold Refuges and dwarves hold Holds.

**2. Conquest wins the stones, not the right.** Storm a castle and you gain a **claim**, while its holder keeps the title. A grant, gift or sale moves the title with the fief. When a lord dies, his realm's succession law picks the heir, and his children inherit his claims.

**3. The Return of the King.** Gondor's throne stands empty, and Denethor rules as **Steward Denethor of Gondor**. Serve Gondor with Aragorn in your clan, and his claim of blood lets you **restore the crown** for 2500 influence. He founds the **House of Telcontar** in Minas Tirith, Denethor stays Steward under the King, and an event window closes on *"Long live the King!"*

**4. Lords are called by what they hold.** **King Aragorn**, **Prince Imrahil of Dol Amroth**, **Marshal Éomer**. Elves are **Aran** and **Hîr**, dwarf lords are **Uzbad**, and orcs keep their plain names. On party names, in conversations and across the encyclopedia.

**5. Claims, bought or pressed.**
- **Buy** a claim near your lands with your people's resource (Castar, Marks, Gems...): 40 for a Barony up to 500 for a Kingdom.
- **Usurp** a claimed title for influence: 10 for a Lordship up to 400 for a Kingdom. A duchy or higher falls to you once most of what lies beneath it is on your side.
- **Form** a vacant title the same way. Hold Gondor and Arnor and forge the **Reunited Kingdom**.
- **Your vassals count.** Lands held by your clan, your vassals and theirs stand behind you, fiefs you hand out included. A king counts every lord of his realm.

AI lords press their own claims each week.

**6. Rule your vassals.** **Grant** a title to a lord of your realm and win his favour, or **revoke** one and earn his resentment. **Appoint** the Steward of Gondor, the Marshals of the Mark or the Lieutenant of Barad-dûr. Offices pay influence every week.

**7. A weekly reckoning.** Vassals pay their liege **tribute**, 50 gold for a Barony up to 300 for a Kingdom, grow closer to him and answer his call to arms for half the influence. Another clan holding your title's town or castle sours your relations and its loyalty.

**8. Sons and daughters get lands.** AI lords hand lesser titles to their grown children. **Boromir** holds West Osgiliath, and **Faramir** the Duchy of Ithilien.

## :books: Where to find it

- A **page for every title** in the encyclopedia: holder, seat, liege and vassals, claims, laws, past holders, and every action open to you with its price.
- A **Titles** list on the encyclopedia home, filtered to yours, your claims or your realm.
- **Titles** sections on settlement, hero and kingdom pages.
- A **title tree** for each realm, from the crown down to the castles. Click a title to open its page.

> **Caveats, plainly.**
> Claims never start wars. Realms adds ambition, not paperwork.
> An existing save gets its titles one in-game hour after loading.
> Turn off the standalone **TAOM.Realms** module: Realms is part of TAOM now.
> Placeholder art, and English only for now.
> In co-op the host keeps the titles; clients can look but not act.
