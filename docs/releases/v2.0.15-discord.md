# TAOM v2.0.15

## ⚔️ Gondor Overhaul
**106 new noble armour pieces** — the Dol-Amroth, Linhir, Blackroot Vale, Arndir and Lond-Galen lines now wear their own regional kit instead of shared mid-tier gear. **~111 Gondor lords redressed** across 15 regional lord-tier sets, battle and civilian. Shields and weapons standardised by fief. Gondor now **out-armors Mordor at every tier**.

## 🏳️ Banner Bearers
Formations raise their faction standard, carried by one of their own soldiers — orc lines get an orc bearer, dwarf lines a dwarf. Infantry only, with denser banner spacing.

## 🏹 Troops
- **73 troops** had skills corrected to match their actual weapons (crossbowmen carrying Bow skill, two-handed cavalry carrying Polearm)
- **Erebor:** 200 equipment swaps so rosters stop repeating the same helmet and axe; Iron Hills noble crossbows buffed to 175 / 225 / 275
- Taverns sell **local mercenaries** — Minas Morgul no longer offers you a Calradian "Hired Pike"
- Every kingdom hands out **its own troops** for the vassal-oath reward
- Eastern dwarf bandits renamed "Erebor Warriors" → **Blacklocks**
- Cave trolls no longer stand around as town guards
- No morale lost recruiting prisoners from your own side

## 🔥 War of the Ring
Phase 1 now fires on **Day 30**, Phase 2 on **Day 44** (was Day 2 / Day 14) — a month of peace to build, then a two-week Rohan/Isengard window before the map ignites.
⚠️ **Existing players keep the old days.** MCM saves your settings — move the sliders yourself or delete `Configs/ModSettings/Global/TAOM/`.

## 💥 Crashes & Hangs Fixed
Siege CTD on banner-bearer spawn (two separate causes) · siege load hanging forever on Dunland troops · age-8 child education CTD for Lothlórien and 3 other cultures · CTD accepting notable quests · Nan Angren raid defence stuck on the loading screen · first-siege CTD from cavalry AI and mixed formations running in sieges.

## 🔧 Also Fixed
Female elves rendering on the male elf body · rulers introducing themselves as "king of the Vlandians" · **[Gondor] Riding Caparison** silently refusing to equip · every mace shipping a broken 0-damage thrust · 9 careers labelling post-battle survival chance as "troop regeneration" · heroes announced dead a day before they died · a Gondor recruitment pool summing to 120% · debug logs piling up forever (now keeps the 10 most recent).

## 🌍 Localisation
**Polish first pass** — 7,946 previously-English strings translated (wanderer backstories, careers, character creation, quests). New liege titles translated across all 12 languages.

## 📦 Dependencies
Bundled BUTR stack updated: ButterLib 2.11.0, MCM 5.12.1, UIExtenderEx 2.13.2.

## 🔨 In Progress
**Lond Cirion**, Gondor's harbour city — walls, gatehouse, barracks and harbour ships are built in the kit, not yet in a playable build.
