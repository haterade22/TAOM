# Lord Face Variety

## Overview

When a new campaign is created, one pass gives every campaign hero a face of their own. Heroes that start on the minimum of a face range are rolled inside that range, heroes that still share an explicit face key with someone of the same race and sex keep it for one anchor and are rolled close to their own face, and heroes generated while the campaign is built (initial children cloned from a lord) are rolled close to their own face too. Existing saves are not touched.

## Why This Exists

- **Vanilla behavior:** `Hero.Deserialize` (not `SetCharacterObject`) copies `CharacterObject.GetBodyPropertiesMin(returnBaseValue: true).StaticProperties` into `Hero.StaticBodyProperties` (`Hero.cs:1880`), and the minimum's weight and build into `Hero.Weight` and `Hero.Build`. A hero authored with a `face_key_template` therefore always gets the template's minimum, and a hero authored with one explicit `BodyProperties` key gets exactly that key. Only a hero born from a template is rolled (`DefaultHeroCreationModel.cs:197-202`).
- **TAOM requirement:** the Haradrim lords all looked alike. 68 Harad women (36 Shaghana, 32 Abanissa) and four Umbar lords carried one explicit key, and the 107 Harad men on `BodyProperty.fighter_haradrim` all started on its minimum. The same shape showed up across the other cultures, so the pass covers every hero (issue #952, widened by the maintainer).
- **Without this feature:** whole houses render as one face.

## Architecture

### Design Challenge

The face is decided by data, not by a model hook, and a hero's face is saved with the campaign. A fix has to rewrite `Hero.StaticBodyProperties` once, at creation, and leave the hero's age alone.

### Solution Approach

A campaign behavior listens to `CampaignEvents.OnNewGameCreatedPartialFollowUpEndEvent` only, never to a load event (the maintainer's decision: new campaigns only). That event fires once, on a new game only, after the plain `OnNewGameCreatedEvent` and the 100 follow-up passes (`CampaignEvents.cs:2098-2106`), so heroes other behaviors create in their follow-ups exist when it runs. It runs `LordFaceVarietyService` once. No Harmony patch, no save data of its own.

- **Candidates:** every hero, alive or dead, that is not the player and has a string id (`IsCandidate`). Rules A and B take only original characters (`CharacterObject.IsOriginalCharacter`); rule C takes the others.
- **Rule A:** the character's base range has spread (minimum and maximum static keys differ) and the hero still holds the minimum. The hero is rolled between minimum and maximum with `BodyProperties.GetRandomBodyProperties`, variation 0, using the character's own hair, beard and tattoo tags, as `DefaultHeroCreationModel` does. Weight and build are rolled between the range's minimum and maximum with `CharacterHelper.GetDynamicBodyPropertiesBetweenMinMaxRange`, the helper `HeroInitializationArgs` uses for a hero born from a template.
- **Rule B:** the remaining original candidates are grouped by (race, sex, static key). In each group larger than one, every member except the anchor is rolled with the minimum and maximum both set to the hero's own current face and variation `0.2`. The roll scales each face morph by a random factor between `1 - v` and `1 + v` of its own value (a morph at 0 stays 0), and draws a new hairstyle, and a beard where beard tags exist, from the hero's tags. The anchor is the first canon hero present (`LordFaceVarietyService.CanonAnchorIds`), then the clan leader, then the first id in ordinal order. A hero whose rule A roll failed falls into this rule.
- **Rule C:** every non-original candidate whose range is flat is rolled around its own face as in rule B. These are heroes the campaign generated by cloning an original character, such as the initial children `TaomInitialChildGenerationBehavior` makes with `HeroCreator.CreateChild` from an explicit-key lord: the child holds its parent's face and has the parent's flat range. A generated hero on a range with spread was already rolled by the engine and is left alone. Generated heroes never enter a rule B group, so a clone (its id `CharacterObject_<n>` sorts first) can never take a lord's place as the anchor.
- **Roll age:** rules B and C roll at `LordFaceVarietyService.RollAge = 30`, not at the hero's current age. The age picks the hair and beard tables, and a young hero rolled at his own age was reset to Bald and Cleanshaven.
- **Twins:** Elladan and Elrohir (`lord_R1_3`, `lord_R1_4`) are identical twins by design. A group containing either keeps both and rolls the rest (`SharedByDesignIds`).
- **Seed:** FNV-1a 32-bit of the hero's `StringId`, masked to 31 bits so it is never negative; the native roll treats -1 as return the minimum. Stable across runs and machines, so a face is fixed by its hero id. Weight and build in rule A come from the engine's random and differ between campaigns.
- **Self-check:** after the rolls the service regroups every candidate and logs `[LordFaceVariety] varied {A}+{B}+{C}, {K} still shared: ...` through the TAOM logger.
- **Throw safety:** a failed roll for one hero is logged and counted, and the pass goes on. The behavior also catches anything `_service.Run()` throws, so a campaign always starts.

### Component Diagram

```
CampaignEvents.OnNewGameCreatedPartialFollowUpEndEvent
        |
LordFaceVarietyBehavior (thin, catches)
        |
LordFaceVarietyService (rules A, B and C, anchors, seeds, self-check)
        |
IHeroFaceAdapter / HeroFaceAdapter -> Hero.StaticBodyProperties (public setter)
```

### The write

`Hero.StaticBodyProperties`, `Hero.Weight` and `Hero.Build` have public setters, so `HeroFaceAdapter` assigns the roll directly. Rule A writes the static face, weight and build; rules B and C write the static face only. The adapter holds no state: each `HeroFaceSnapshot` carries its engine hero in an internal `Handle`, and a snapshot that did not come from the adapter makes a roll return false. Rules B and C build the hero's own `BodyProperties` at the roll age from his current weight and build. No reflection is needed.

## Configuration

None. The constants live in the service: `OwnFaceVariation = 0.2f`, `RollAge = 30f`, the canon anchor list and the twins allowlist. Every canon anchor and twin id must be a hero with an explicit face key (a data test enforces it), since a hero on a template is rolled by rule A and never kept.

**Tuning:** `0.2` is a first guess. It keeps a rolled hero recognisably the same person while changing the face enough to tell two heroes apart. Raise it if lords still look alike in game, lower it if a rolled hero stops looking like his house. The value is read at campaign creation, so a rebuild and a new campaign are needed to see a change.

## Data change

In `Main/_Module/ModuleData/characters/lords.xml` the shared explicit `<BodyProperties>` key of these hero groups is replaced by `<face_key_template value="BodyProperty.<template>" />`, keeping their hair and beard tags, so rule A rolls them:

| Group | Heroes | Template |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Harad women (Shaghana, Abanissa) and `lord_U1_1`, `lord_U1_11`, `lord_U1_12`, `lord_U1_13` (Umbar) | 72 | `fighter_haradrim` |
| Misty Mountain orcs (race `orc`) | 90 men, 60 women | `fighter_gundabad` |
| Gundabad `pale_uruk` | 70 men, 30 women | `fighter_gundabad` |
| Goblins (Goblin and Bluecraig clans) | 60 men, 20 women | `fighter_gundabad` |
| Dol Guldur `dg_uruk` | 71 men, 55 women | `fighter_dolguldur` |
| Mordor `uruk` men | 24 | `fighter_uruk_mordor` |

Each template is the one that culture's own troops already use (checked with `TAOM_bodyproperties.xml`: minimum and maximum differ in all four). `fighter_goblin` was not used because its minimum equals its maximum. Canon and named heroes were left on their authored faces.

A hero takes only the template's static minimum, so a swapped hero now also gets a rolled weight and build between the template's, where he used to start from the authored values (the Harad women from 0.5 and 0.5, for example). Faces are fixed by the hero id, but weight and build use the engine's random and differ between campaigns. `HeroFaceVarietyDataTests` prints the predicted rule A and rule B counts and holds a ceiling on rule B, so a new shared explicit key shows up.

## Tests

- `TAOM.Tests/Features/LordFaceVariety/LordFaceVarietyServiceTests.cs`: rules A, B and C, anchor order, roll age, one seed per hero, twins, skips (player, empty id, non-original on a spread range, unique faces), seed, self-check, throw and refuse in rules A and B, a rule A failure falling into rule B.
- `TAOM.Tests/Features/LordFaceVariety/LordFaceVarietyWiringTests.cs`: module listed once, behavior declared, exactly one listener on `OnNewGameCreatedPartialFollowUpEndEvent` and no load event, the catch scoped to `_service.Run()`.
- `TAOM.Tests/Core/FemaleLordHairDataTests.cs`: every human lady on a face template carries her own hair tags, because rule A rolls with the template's tags and the human templates hold men's styles (a lady with none starts bald half the time; `docs/modding/body-properties.md` Gotchas).
- `TAOM.Tests/Core/HeroFaceVarietyDataTests.cs`: runs the real service over the repo's heroes with a roller that always gives a unique face. Only the twins may remain shared, every canon id and twin must be an explicit-key hero, no Harad hero may keep a shared explicit key, and rule B may not exceed a ceiling. The test prints the rule A and rule B counts.

## Known limitations

- **Custom Battle commanders are not covered.** The pass runs in a campaign only.
- **Vanilla lords retagged by TAOM are covered at runtime but not by the data gate.** The gate reads this repo's ModuleData, and vanilla lords are not in it.
- **UNVERIFIED in game.** No new campaign has been started and looked at yet: the log line, the Harad houses, the creature houses and `OwnFaceVariation` are owed a look.

## How to keep two heroes on one face

Add the pair to `LordFaceVarietyService.SharedByDesignIds` and to the data gate's expectation. A hero who should keep his authored face when others share it goes into `CanonAnchorIds`.

## Co-op

The pass runs only where a campaign is created and has no load path. See [coop-interop.md](coop-interop.md) for the client and dedicated server cases.
