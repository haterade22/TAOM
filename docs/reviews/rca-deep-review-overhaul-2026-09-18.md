# RCA: /deep-review overhaul and the XSD gate (deep review, 2026-09-18)

## Top-line

The work (#621) rebuilt `/deep-review` around a read-only senior `deep-reviewer` agent, a Design & Elegance lens whose proposals Step 4 applies, an XML lens, and a new XSD gate, `tools/validate_xml_schemas.py`. Its own deep review ran on the new agents in two waves (engine claims, tool correctness, cross-document data flow and design, then standards plus completeness). Every finding below was re-read in source or reproduced before it was fixed; the tool fixes each went RED then GREEN in `tools/tests/test_validate_xml_schemas.py` (23 tests became 47). Three findings sit in pre-existing data or code outside the change and were filed instead of fixed (#619, #620, #622), per the changed-code-only rule.

The dominant pattern: **a change to a contract was checked against the shapes its author had in mind, not against every consumer and every input.** The launch table, the trigger sweep, the tool's input boundary and the first docstring's glob rule all failed that way.

## Root Cause Pattern: a contract checked against its author's shapes

F1, F2, F3, F5 and F7 share one mechanism. Each change defined or described a contract (which lenses run, when `/deep-review` triggers, which files a gate reads, what a flag does, which files a glob matches) and was checked only against the cases its author was thinking about. The consumers and inputs outside that picture (a harness-only changeset, a sibling doc with an old count, a nonexistent path, a mixed file list, a volume without short names) were never exercised. The fix is the same each time: enumerate the consumers or inputs mechanically (grep every consumer, test every input class, measure on the target volume) instead of reasoning from the design.

## Why the lenses missed or caught these

This was the work's first review, so "missed" here means what each lens did not see.

- **Engine claims (Lens 2, engine-claims mode):** caught F7 and the "mirrors exactly" overstatement, and settled the open question by reading `rgl_log`: the engine does validate on a campaign load. Not scoped to tool inputs (F3) or to docs consistency (F2).
- **Tool correctness (tooling plus Lens 3):** caught F3, F4, F12 and the Agent 7 trap. Did not see F13: `validate_moduledata.py` was not in its scope.
- **Data flow (Lens 5, harness mode improvised for this review):** caught F1, F2, F5, F6 and F8's mechanism, the widest yield, as in every earlier review. It did not check YAML validity (F10 came later, during the fixes).
- **Design (Lens 6):** proposed the lens files, the waves, the predicates, the `SCHEMA_INVALID` pass and module inference, all applied.
- **Standards plus completeness (Lenses 1 and 4, adapted):** caught F9's lesson link, F11, F14, the missing catalog rows and the missing tests.
- **F13** was found by the builder during the fixes, outside every lens's scope; **F10** was introduced during the fixes and caught by `tools/test_hooks.sh` before the convergence pass.
