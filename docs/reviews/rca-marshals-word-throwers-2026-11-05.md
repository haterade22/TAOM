# RCA: Marshal's Word, section 9.4, throwers on an advance (build checker, two review rounds, Codex and two game runs, 2026-11-05)

**Top line:** the build checker, then a first review of 29 lens findings, a critic and Codex, and then a second wave of a checker and three lenses, found one HIGH and a run of MEDIUM and LOW items across the feature. The HIGH is the hand-back to the base game's Advance. The stand lets vanilla run underneath and only changes the answer it returns, and the contract said vanilla's melee point therefore stays current. It does not: `MovementOrder.cs:1185` replaces the cached engage point only while the new point lies within `MovementSpeedMaximum^2 * 9 * Depth` of the company (about 29 m at speed 4 and depth 6, against a stand gap of 24 to 32 m), so an enemy that drew off during the stand left the cache behind and the release walked the company back to it. Codex reproduced it by compiling the vanilla cache block in memory beside the production `ThrowerRun` class (no game run, no full build). Every finding that changed behaviour is fixed in the worktree on top of `6d2c51de9` (uncommitted when the fix and close reports were written), and the final suite fails only the known `BannerlordRefAsmVersion_PinnedGameVersion_IsTheSameGameBuild`. Two game runs on the fixed build (`6d2c51de9.dirty`, built 20261106-033959 UTC) passed: `20261105-214011` (`advance_throwers`) and `20261105-214249` (`advance_throwers_vanilla`). They are in [marshals-word.md](../features/marshals-word.md).

How to read the table: C1 to C15 are the decision ids of the first review. Lens items are numbered from 1 in the order each lens listed them: Standards (S), Tooling (T), Engine compatibility (E) and Data flow (D). The critic is the Completeness pass; its two items are Critic a and b. The Deviations rows are in [build-spec.md](../features/marshals-word/build-spec.md), rows 52 to 57.

## Root-cause pattern

**Row 4 is the main cause: the code trusted vanilla's state to stay current.** The postfix design lets vanilla run first, so that vanilla's cache, pull-back and Deployment yield stay vanilla's, and the contract concluded that the hand-back is therefore free. It is free only while vanilla's own refresh rule keeps up, and `MovementOrder.cs:1185` says it stops keeping up past about 29 m, which is inside the range of a 24 to 32 m stand. The hand-back must make vanilla recompute, not rely on it.

I grepped `docs/reviews/rca-*.md` (569 files) for hand-back, stale cache, sticky and `_engageTargetPositionCache`. No earlier RCA names a vanilla cache that outlives an override. The nearest earlier shippings are the same family: handing control back to vanilla while dropping or assuming one of its responsibilities. `rca-kingdom-vote-deadlock-2026-09-06.md` finding 1 (Seam B closed a window without the listener clear that vanilla's `ExecuteDone` does) and `rca-fords-of-war-2026-11-05.md` row 2 (a soldier handed back to vanilla would have kept a flat morale unless `CommonAIComponent.Initialize()` ran). Their lessons ("Substituting for a vanilla method inherits every one of its responsibilities; deferring to one inherits every one of its branches", and the trigger lesson after it) tell a builder to list a method's branches and side effects. They do not say to list the state vanilla keeps between calls. The new lesson in `harmony-il.md` says that.

**Rows 5, 6, 10 and 11 share a second cause: a check that judges a window or a signal wider than its claim.** `throwers-throw` and the trial's window (both row 5) counted the approach, `throwers-stand` counted position without stopping, `walked-in` and `closed` passed a company that never stood, and `throwers-melee` passed on blows an enemy also produces. This is a repeat. `rca-marshals-word-m3-massed-m5-m6-2026-11-04.md` rows 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10 are verdicts that cannot fail or pass on less than the row claims, and `rca-marshals-word-m1-m2-2026-11-04.md` rows 9 and 11 are verdicts a broken feature also passes. The lessons "Each trial verdict has a test that makes it fail" and "A log verdict needs a number for the broken build and for the working one" were in force when these steps were written. They cover the fail path and the dead build; neither says to count over the window the claim names, from facts the run froze. The first game run shows what that costs: `run_shots=60` against `shots=39`, so 21 of the 60 throws came on the approach, before the stand, and the old window would have counted all 60. The new lesson in `testing-qa.md` says that.

**Rows 9, 13 and 14 and the second review's rows 21, 22, 26 and 29 share a third cause: a rule changed or read in one place and not in its twin.** The team change cleared the records and not the table (row 9); `TryChoose` lower-cased and `PlayerFor` only trimmed (row 14); the timer's postfix dropped a non-Advance call but its prefix still read the clock for it (row 26); the fix for row 14 left two summaries on `NameOf` (row 21). This is also a repeat: rows 13 to 15 of `rca-marshals-word-m3-massed-m5-m6-2026-11-04.md`, and the lesson "A correction to one reader of a rule searches its other readers (Marshal's Word, 2026-11-05)", written the same day. No new lesson: one root cause is one entry, and that entry already says what to do.

**Rows 16, 18, 19 and 28 share a fourth cause: contract text written from the plan or a partial engine read.** The throw mechanism (row 18), the setting row, the skein hint and the shot count all stated more than the code or the engine did. This repeats rows 1 and 11 of the same RCA, and the lesson "A doc that states behaviour is checked against the final code, not the plan" covers it.

## The game runs

Both runs: `battle_terrain_a`, 101 defenders with `lord_1_9` against 150 attackers, one battle per step, build `6d2c51de9.dirty` built 20261106-033959 UTC (the BuildStamp pair read OK). The company is 40 `gondor_har_javelineer` (`units=40 armed0=39 ammo0=390 range=80.0 stand_gap=32.0`). The wrapper printed a PASS line for each: one step with a PASS line, no FAIL line, no CrashReport block, no failed Marshal's Word check, no Marshal's Word `[ERROR]` line, a clean module unload, `swallowed=0 shield_swallowed=0 errors=0`. **Run 1, `20261105-214011`, `advance_throwers`, PASS** (98.8 live seconds):

- The company approached at 2.1 to 3.3 m/s from `gap0=330.5`. The first throws came before the stand: the last approach line reads `gap=31.0 speed=1.4 ammo=369 shots=0 run_shots=21`.
- It stood at `gap_at_stand=30.2` (stand gap 32.0, `overshoot=0.8`), at `speed=0.3`, then 0.4 and 0.5 at the next lines. It stood for `stood_s=13.9`, and `shots` rose to 27, 38 and 39 at gaps of 22.1, 17.0 and 11.5 as the enemy came in.
- It released `why=enemy-close` at `gap=11.5` with `run_shots=60`, `ammo=330` and `ammo_spent=60` (so the count and the ammunition agree), `armed_at_release=39`.
- The checks: `throwers-stand` pass; `throwers-throw` pass at `shots=39 stood_s=13.9`; `throwers-cost` pass at `calls=5246 mean_us=3.6 max_us=497.6` (the check fails a mean over 50 us or a call over 5 ms); `throwers-melee` pass at `blows=269 melee_s=26.9 advanced_m=3.4`.
- The cost line: `pub_calls=5246 adv_calls=0 replaced=0 published=67 postfix=5246`, step `faulted=0`, session counter `taom.marshals-word.throwers-postfix pass=5246 fail=0`.

**Run 2, `20261105-214249`, `advance_throwers_vanilla`, PASS** (55.5 live seconds), the control with the rule off: `gap0=279.1 gap_end=7.8 published=0 armed_at_close=39 walked_m=10.8 slowest=1.7 faulted=0`, session counter `throwers-postfix pass=0`.

**What only the game proved**

- The soldiers throw while they stand: 39 shots over 13.9 s, with the ammunition falling from 369 to 330. Before this the throw was a decompile read.
- `OnAgentShootMissile` reports a thrown javelin: the shot count (`run_shots=60`) equals the ammunition spent (`ammo_spent=60`).
- The postfix runs and is not inlined: `postfix=5246` and `pub_calls=5246` in the run with the rule on, 0 in the control.
- The cost is far inside its limits on this battle: mean 3.6 us, longest call 497.6 us, against 50 us and 5 ms. The postfix and the write faulted nothing (`faulted=0`). The engage-point bind did not latch a fault.
- A standing company reads 1 m/s or less (`speed=0.3` to 0.5), so the stand is read. The control's company walked past the stand zone at a slowest speed of 1.7 m/s, above the 1 m/s limit, so `stand-blind` reads false there.
- The company goes in after the release: `advanced_m=3.4` against the 3 m minimum, with 269 blows.
- The window of row 5 mattered: the old count would have read 60 where the stand read 39.

**What stays unproved**

- Which AI path makes the throws. The run throws on the approach (between the gap 56.4 and gap 31.0 lines, where is not recorded) and at the stand; the `0x6960D0` scoring and the ranged-mode flags were not read, so `GoToPos` against Ranged is still a decompile read.
- The row 4 case. The enemy in run 1 came toward the company (the gap fell from 30.2 to 11.5), so the retreating-enemy hand-back is covered only by the model test. Whether the write in `MarshalEngagePoint.Forget` reaches the formation's own order through `ArrangementOrder.cs:236` is still [Likely], not shown.
- Release by `spent`, `stand-time` or `run-time`: only `enemy-close` has been seen in the game.
- That 3 m is the right melee minimum. One run read 3.4 m, a margin of 0.4 m.
