## Root-cause pattern

Findings 1 to 3 share one cause. The rule, its doc and its checklist were written outward from the cases named in the request and from the first engine lines read, not from the whole of `Hero.CharacterStates`: what sets each state, what ends it and how long that takes. The `default: return false` in `CanReceive` then decided three states that nobody had listed (Fugitive, Dead and Disabled; the prisoner was put to the maintainer). The same family is on file: "A fix keyed on an outcome enumerates the paths that produce it" (campaign-mechanics, 2026-10-02) and "A config's default/fallback value applies to the WHOLE set" (data-content-cultures). Because it repeats, the prevention is a row in the campaign trap sheet, which loads with the campaign files, and not only a lesson.
