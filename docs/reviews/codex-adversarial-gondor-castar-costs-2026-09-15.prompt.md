FEATURE. Special Resources gives every kingdom a secondary currency (Gondor: Castar, resource id stays `caster`). `Main/_Module/ModuleData/special_resources/troop_resource_costs.xml` prices troops: `upgrade_cost` (charged on the party-screen upgrade, Patch26), `recruit_cost` (Patch51 greys the Done button on the recruit screen when unaffordable; the charge fires on `CampaignEvents.OnUnitRecruitedEvent`, one call per unit), `daily_upkeep` (daily tick, desertion at zero balance), `merchant_cost` (the Elite Emissary's one-time price). Until this change Gondor's only rows were eight merchant-only emissary prices. Now every Gondor troop at level 41 and above (16 ids) carries `upgrade_cost` and `daily_upkeep` at the Mordor band (L41 4 / 0.2, L46 5 / 0.3, L51 6 / 0.4), the two that a notable can hand out directly (`gondor_ithilien_ranger`, `gondor_mt_fountain_guard`) also carry a `recruit_cost` equal to their emissary price (45, 28), and the 13 Black Numenorean rows' `daily_upkeep` is halved to 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.08 / 0.1 / 0.15. The upkeep is charged in the PLAYER's resolved resource whatever the troop's faction.
