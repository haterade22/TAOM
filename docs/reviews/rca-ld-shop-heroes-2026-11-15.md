# RCA: the Long Defeat shop sells Aragorn, Legolas and Gimli (#1337, #1335)

Findings from the tier 1 review of Phases 2 and 3 of #1325, on the uncommitted worktree `feat/hero-companions` over aa67e8ed4. The first wave ran four lenses (5 Data flow, 2 Engine compatibility, 7 XML, Tooling). It found a HIGH, so a second wave ran the other four (1 Standards, 3 Efficiency, 4 Completeness, 6 Design) with a data-flow and an engine re-review of the fix, and that wave found a second HIGH in the fix itself. The second fix got its own data-flow and engine re-review, which found no HIGH. Four unattended game runs sit between the reviews; two of the findings were first seen in a run. The Codex pass did not run: the maintainer said to skip it while the account has no usage.

Not applied:

- `LdRemoval.TroopId` carries a shop hero's id for a hero removal (lens 1). A rename touches every removal path; the enum's doc states the meaning.
- `RunEffects` builds one more empty list per tick (lens 3). The class has seven others of the same kind.
- A rule in the judge that every hero must have a kill (data-flow re-review). A kill count is the outcome of a fight, and a hard rule on it would make an unattended run fail at random. The position rule of finding 3 fails on the state the idle run was in; the kills are printed for a person to read.
- The opening-split section of the feature doc says "the engine does" for three hand-backs of which two are TAOM calls. It is outside this change.
- A forced-use defender engine would take crew from the heroes' group (engine re-review). Defender engines are disabled at boot today.

## Proof

Run 20261115-210056, on the committed build a5f607f41, is the first run after the second fix. `python tools/ingame_verdict.py --issue 1337` reads PASS for `hero`, `hero-fall` and `hunters`. At the three wave clears the standing heroes were in formation 7 with nobody else in it and near the lane (`near` equal to `standing`: 3, 2, 1). Legolas had 24 kills at the second clear and Gimli 20 at the third; in the run before, the two melee heroes had none. Aragorn fell twice and Legolas once, and Aragorn was bought again between waves, so the fall and the buy-back are now seen in game. The lord's fall handed the group back once, and the hold retook it with nobody moved. The run reached no stalemate charge and no membership repair; findings 4 and 6 rest on their pins. The wrapper's exit is still FAIL on the muster line of #1142, as in every run.

## Root-cause pattern

Findings 1, 2, 4 and 5 are one failure four times: a design step that named an engine call and its expected effect without reading the body that produces the effect, in an area where a sibling file had already recorded the trap. `Posts.cs` held finding 1's answer, `Split.cs` held half of finding 4's, and `Split.Order` sets the three orders finding 5 left out. The plan read those files for their spawn recipe and not for their comments.

Finding 3 made the first two expensive. Each fix shipped with a line that proved the fix's mechanism and not the player's symptom, so the second HIGH passed its own check in the game.

## Why each reviewer missed these

- **The plan's design pass** and its refutation review (before the build) accepted "formation 7 with the not-splittable flag" as a spike to measure. The spike was folded into the first game run, which had no line for it.
- **First wave, lens 5 and lens 2** found finding 1 after the second run had already shown it, and found 7 and 10. Lens 2 named a parked group under the flag as a risk; nobody had yet asked what order an off-AI group holds in a calm, because that design did not exist.
- **The main session** designed the charge of finding 2 and did not read the order's applicability. The builder of that fix quoted the order bar's call, which is correct and beside the point.
- **Second wave, lens 5 and lens 2** found 2, 3 and 4 from the engine source before the third run ended; that run then agreed.
- **The re-review of the second fix** found 5 and 6 and the last LOW items. No lens found anything in the shop rules, the refund or the report: the pure half had its tests first.
