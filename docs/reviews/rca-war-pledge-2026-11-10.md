# RCA: War Pledge, Slice 1 (deep-review, 2026-11-10)

## Top-line

`/deep-review` tier 1 ran on the uncommitted #1113 change in a sibling worktree (branch `feat/war-pledge`, base `8841aac99`): Lens 5 (data flow), Lens 2 (engine compatibility), Lens 7 (XML) and the Tooling lens, plus one Codex pass at `ultra`. Every engine claim was checked against the installed v1.5.7 DLLs. No reviewer found a CRITICAL or HIGH defect, so no second wave ran. Three MEDIUM and ten LOW findings were confirmed against the source and fixed in the same change. Lens 3, Lens 4 and Lens 6 were skipped by the tier, so Step 4 (improvements) did not apply.

One defect was found before the review, by a gate. The pledge store first used a plain dictionary. `UnlookedForAidWiringTests.TheAlignmentReadPath_OnlyReadsItsMap` failed in the first full run, because `AlignmentService` is read on the party AI worker. The store became copy-on-write behind a volatile reference before any reviewer saw it.

The findings that changed behaviour: a kingdom that has a side through its culture can no longer pledge (1); the pledged realm declares its own wars, so vanilla charges a broken alliance to it (5); and the AI's wait starts when the pledge is stored, also when a listener throws after it (6).

Trunk moved nine commits during the work (to `335d6a65d`, with Kingdom Allegiance #1111 at `85e8788dd`). The reviewers read that commit: a pledged realm reads its new side through `ResolveSide` in the join veto, so AI clans of the other side do not join it, and clans that already serve stay. The rebase is owed, with the pinned settings counts at 530, 495 and 338.

## Fix re-review

Findings 1, 5 and 6 changed runtime behaviour in a tier 1 area, so the fix was reviewed again: Lens 2 and Lens 5 at `high` on the fix, and one Codex pass at `high`. None found a CRITICAL, HIGH or MEDIUM defect. All three confirmed against the v1.5.7 DLLs that the declarations pass TAOM's `DeclareWarAction` prefix, that the join's guard (`HasDeclaredWar`) agrees with the adapter's, and that none of the ten lean realms is refused by the new culture check. Applied from it:

- **Tests that could not fail** (Lens 5 F1 and F6, Codex F2): the test fake answered `AreAtWar` and `HasDeclaredWar` from one set, so a guard on the model-aware check would have passed; it now models constant war separately. New tests pin that a refused candidate starts no wait, that the peace-block switch decides the owed wars, and that the join declares while the model already holds the pair at war.
- **A lost diagnostic** (Lens 5 F3): the join's reason and log line name each realm whose war was not declared.
- **Text** (Lens 2 F1 to F4, Lens 5 F4 and F5, Codex F1): a broken trade agreement follows the same first-faction rule (`TradeAgreementsCampaignBehavior.cs:186-203`); the session-launch retry needs `autoWarBetweenHostileTiers` and declares in list order; a join that throws leaves own-side wars open; the comment on the declaration said it only stores the stance; the status printed "courts no realm now" beside "the Free side courts".

Not applied from it: a pledged-realm-first order in `WarOfTheRingService.DeclareHostileTierWars` for the retry (Codex F1, Lens 2 F1). It matters only after a declaration failed during a join, which needs a listener to throw, and none is known; the feature doc states the limit. A shipped-data test that a lean realm's culture is Neutral (Lens 5 F2) guards a future data edit and is the maintainer's call.

## Not applied

- **Hostility and peace by culture** (the rest of Codex F1). Codex asked that a pledged realm also be Hostile to, and at peace with, a kingdom sided only by its culture. The Full War rules read kingdom ids only (`DiplomacyService.IsWarAllowed`, `diplomacy.json` has no row for a player-founded realm), so no native member is forced into a war with one. A pledged realm follows the same rule; `TryJoin_Joined_MakesNoWarAndNoPeaceWithAKingdomSidedOnlyByItsCulture` pins it and the feature doc lists it as a limit.
- **An explicit repaint of the Alignment map** (Codex F2). A join that changes no stance leaves the paint stale until the next war, peace, alliance or fief event anywhere on the map. The fix is a dependency on `RealmBorderService` for a state that needs a realm already at war with every realm of the other side, and that heals at the next event. Simplicity criterion: a tiny win for a new coupling. The doc states the limit.
- **Escaping `=` and `;` in the save string** (Codex suspect 11). Kingdom ids are identifiers; reachability is unverified and nothing in the data has such an id.
- **A production-order pin for the container** (Lens 5 T4). `IoC.cs` resolves nothing before the modules register today; a pin would guard a future edit.

## Maintainer decisions owed
