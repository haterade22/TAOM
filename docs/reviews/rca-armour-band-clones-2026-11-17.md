# RCA: top-tier armour priced by low troops (armour band clones, 2026-11-17)

## Top-line

The roster-first armour restat prices an Armory piece at the band of its lowest battle wearer
(`rebalance_armor._roster_first_plan`, `worn_band`). Since the 2026-11-14 restat, a piece that a
level 36+ troop shares with a level 30 or lower troop carries the low troop's numbers: `mirkwood_body`
dropped from body 25 to 8 under the level 51 palace guard because two level 11 militia wear it, and
three level 36 goblins wear `sk_md_orc_inf_chest_med_b` at body 13. On trunk 642b1c482 the rule covers
101 pieces (validator code `TOP_TIER_ARMOUR_WORN_LOW`, 101 before, 0 after the apply in the worktree).

The fix keeps the pricing rule and changes who wears what. An elite piece (treatment A) gives each low
band a clone (`taom_band_<lt|md|hv>_<source>`), so the source's lowest wearer becomes elite; a low piece
(treatment B) moves its level 36+ wearers to a stronger piece of the same line and slot. The plan is
`tools/armour_band_clones.json`, written by `tools/generate_armour_band_clones.py`; the whole-file troop
generators apply the same swap. The Armory restat itself is not applied: its dry run is the evidence. Until it runs, the `armour-restat`
data gate (`rebalance_armor.py --check`) fails on the same 34 items (33 before the review round); it passes
on trunk.

No `/deep-review` ran (the builder was a subagent that cannot invoke skills); it is owed before the
commit, with the restat apply and an in-game check.

## Root-cause pattern

One number, the lowest wearer's band, stood for a piece that several bands share. The 2026-11-14
restat was right for each piece by its own rule and wrong for the troops: no gate looked at a piece from
the side of its best wearer. F2 to F4 are the same shape at a smaller scale: a picker that judged a
troop or a piece alone missed the troops it is tied to (its other sets, its upgrade targets, its
colour twins).
