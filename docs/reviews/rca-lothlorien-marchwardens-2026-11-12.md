# RCA: Haldir, Rumil and Orophin move from Mirkwood to Lothlorien, 2026-11-12

## Summary

Commit `d55b15905` changed three elf lords (`lord_M3_3`, `lord_M4_3`, `lord_M4_5`) from culture
`mirkwood` to `lothlorien` and put them in `clan_lothlorien_3`. The review ran at tier 2 after the
push: Agent 5 (Data flow) and Agent 7 (XML), both at `effort: "high"`, no Codex (Mike declined the tier 2
pass on 2026-11-12). Neither found a CRITICAL or HIGH defect, so there was no second wave. Agent 5 traced 9
flows with no gap. Agent 7 ran 9 gates and found the references, bytes and line endings clean.

Seven items were confirmed: one MEDIUM that is a decision still owed, five LOW items about text that
still described the old data, and one LOW item that predates the commit. One more LOW claim was
disproved (below). The text items repeat a lesson already on file:
[misc.md](lessons/misc.md) "Sweep docs for the facts a change alters, not for the feature's name"
(#889, 2026-10-26).

## Disproved

Agent 7 reported that Mirkwood's generated-lord kit pool loses `mirkwood_bat_template_medium_a`, because
its donors drop from 2 to 1. Counting the rosters in `lords.xml` after the move shows 3 Mirkwood lords on
it (`lord_M2_4`, `lord_M3_4`, `lord_M6_1`) and 5 on `medium_d`. The pool keeps both kits. Not applied.

## Root-cause pattern

Findings 2 to 4 are one pattern: the change altered a fact (a lord's culture, a deleted comment) and the
sweep looked for the feature's name, not for every place that states or cites the fact. Finding 3 adds a
second step the sweep must take: ask whether a file is generated before editing it. Finding 1 is new: a
culture is also a key. Moving a hero changes which kits a culture's generated lords can roll.

## Why each agent missed these

The builder's own pass ran `data_gates.py` (7 PASS) and the full C# suite. No gate reads comment
citations, generated-doc provenance or donor counts, so all three were invisible to them. Agent 5
found items 2, 3, 4 and 6. Agent 7 found items 1, 2 and 5, and also reported the pool-loss claim that did
not hold. Lenses 1, 3, 4 and 6 were skipped by tier. Their usual yield (citations, comments) is what items
2 and 4 are, and the two lenses that ran caught both.
