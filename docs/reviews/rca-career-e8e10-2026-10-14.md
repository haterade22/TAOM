# RCA: career forks engine, steps E8 to E10 and E13 (#769), 2026-10-14

## Top-line

The second engine change of the career redesign lets a career option carry two effects (E8), filters
troop effects by troop class (E9), adds three counter effects for the leader's troops (E10: fear,
knockdown and crush-through) and describes them in the diagnostics (E13). `/deep-review` ran eight
lenses in two waves of four: standards, engine, data flow and XML; then efficiency, completeness,
design, and one expansion lens on the new `CareerTroopHooks` and its six callers. Two refuters then
tried to disprove each MEDIUM defect. Nothing was CRITICAL or HIGH. Five findings were MEDIUM: the
four defects went to the refuters, who kept the per-call container lookup at MEDIUM and rated the
forced-knockdown gap and the class-blind diagnostics as LOW; the fifth, the fragment keys (XML7-F1),
was filed as a proposal and was not refuted. The other 61 findings were LOW, nits or proposals, and
several lenses often found one defect, so the table groups the 66 into rows 1 to 15. A fix round, a
documentation sweep with a critic pass, and a four-reviewer convergence review followed; rows C1 to
C9 are what the convergence review found.

**Seven rows repeat a lesson that was already on file** (rows 1 to 6 and 8). The lessons existed; the
builder brief did not name them and the builder did not read them. The E4 to E7 RCA's first pattern
was briefs that described only the happy path; this is the same root, a brief that leaves out what
the orchestrator already knows. So this RCA gives the builder template a lessons slot instead of
adding an eighth lesson to read.

## Root-cause patterns

1. **The brief did not carry the subsystem's lessons** (rows 1 to 5 and 8). Each of these defects
   matches a lesson already in `docs/reviews/lessons/`. The orchestrator's brief named files, steps
   and seams; it did not name the lesson files for the subsystems it touched, and the builder did not
   read them. An eighth lesson would join seven that went unread. **Prevent:** the Feature Developer
   template in `docs/ai-includes/agent-teams.md` has a `Lessons:` slot that the lead fills with the
   applicable lesson headings by name and file, and the builder's report says what it did about each.
2. **One predicate made to answer a second question** (rows 4 and 5). `ClassFiltered` was asked
   "does this type honour a mask?", and `ApplyFactor` was asked "what does a reader with no class
   receive?". Each needed its own answer (`MaskGated`, army-wide reads).
3. **Evidence designed after the code** (row 3). The plan's in-game steps name what Mike will see;
   the change did not emit it. Second occurrence of the `testing-qa.md` lesson inside one week.
4. **A fix or a doc written from the finding's text instead of the code** (C1, C7). The forced-knockdown
   list copied the review's examples, and a doc writer rewrote a correct page from the method body
   without the registration rule. Both were caught only because the convergence review checked claims,
   not presence.

## Why each lens caught or missed what it did

- **Standards:** caught 1, 3 (STD-4), 4, 6, 9, 10 and the cheat's size. It does not trace what a reader
  receives, so it missed 5.
- **Engine:** caught 1 (ENG-6), the dismount route (2), the thread inventory and summary text (7), the
  tripwire (8) and the two declined items (15). It verified the engine's knockdown readers but not TAOM's own
  synthetic blows, which data flow found.
- **Efficiency:** caught 1 and the unparseable-mask half of 4.
- **Completeness:** caught 3, 4, 6 (C4-13), the docs in 7, most of 8, 13 (C4-12) and the harvest blank
  line.
- **Data flow:** caught 1 (F8), 2 (the forced knockdowns), 3, 4, 5, 7 (F4), 9, 10, 11, 13 and both older
  gaps (14).
  It is again the lens with the most distinct findings.
- **Design:** caught 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 13.
- **XML:** caught 6 (fragment keys and English labels), 13 and the harvest blank line.
- **Cross-feature hooks (expansion):** caught 1 (and judged the reload reason valid for a never-reset
  static cache), plus 3, 4, the thread inventory and hero claim in 7, the `RequiresGame` pins in 8, and the
  Race Abilities tally in 12.

- **Convergence review** (four reviewers: fix verification, regressions, engine and threading, docs and
  RCA accuracy): 59 of the 66 findings resolved, 6 partly and 1 not (F5); rows C1 to C6 close them.
  The engine reviewer verified every engine claim the docs make on the installed v1.5.5 DLLs and found
  the crush hold's reads and locks safe off the main thread; it left the concurrent use of the shared
  random generator UNVERIFIED, and Codex then proved it unsafe (X1). The docs reviewer caught C7, which the critic pass
  (staleness and links) could not.
