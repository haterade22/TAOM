# RCA: Black Uruk duplicate halberd and lance-only Grunts, 2026-11-07

**Summary:** A player reported that set 1 of the Black Uruk Barad-Dur Guard showed two halberds while the other sets showed one, and that almost every Black Uruk Grunt kit (the hired copy too) carried the same Black Numenorean lance. Both came from the melee ladder fix applied in `ea0592eb9` (2026-09-21): `tools/fix_melee_ladder.py` replaced each out-of-band weapon with the single best in-band weapon of its class, so two different weapons in one troop became one id. The Guard's spear and spare halberd both became Uruk Halberd IX, the Shield Guard got two Gundabad spears, and the Grunt's Orc Polearms II, III and IV all became the lance. Grunt set 1 also held only a sword in Item0 and nothing in Item1; because the engine draws each slot from an independent set, about one Grunt in 14 spawned with a lance and no sidearm. Mike waived the GitHub issue.

The repair: every Guard set now carries one halberd (IX, VI, XII, IX), in the campaign troop and its Fords of War copy; the Shield Guard's duplicate spear became a spare Halberd III, the pre-fixer shape; the Grunt and the hired Grunt rotate the lance, Orc Polearm II, the lance and Orc Polearm III over their 14 sets, and set 1 carries a polearm and a sword. The fixer now refuses to give a troop a weapon it already keeps or was already given.

The review was tier 2 (repo troop data and a data repair tool with its tests; no patch, model, save data or live install file). Lenses 5 (data flow), 7 (XML) and tooling ran at `high`. Codex was not run: Mike chose to skip it (CODEX: NOT AUTHORIZED). No CRITICAL or HIGH was found, so no second wave ran. The C# suite passed (37608 passed, 0 failed, 9 skipped) and the Python tool tests passed (8384 passed, 12 skipped) before the review fixes; the final runs are in the commit's `/verify`.

## Root-cause pattern

F1, F2 and the original defect share one cause: a weapon change was judged one slot at a time. The fixer judged each swap alone and never looked at what else the troop held; my first repair fixed the duplicate slot and did not look at the troop's other slots or other sets. A troop's battle sets are one menu the engine draws from slot by slot (`.claude/rules/troops.md`), so a weapon change is reviewed as a table of every set and every weapon slot, before and after.

## Why each reviewer caught or missed these

- **Lens 7 (XML):** caught F1 and F2 by reading the before and after of every set against `git show ea0592eb9`, and confirmed no cross-set shield conflict.
- **Lens 5 (data flow):** found F7 and F8, confirmed every downstream consumer (career kits, enlistment kits, equipment sets, C# tests) stays connected, and rated F6 LOW.
- **Tooling lens:** found F3, F4 and F5 with a mutation run of the new tests, and raised F6 at MEDIUM on a count that included civilian-only swaps.
- **Lenses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6:** skipped by tier. **Codex:** not authorized.

## What went wrong in the process

1. I described the first repair to the maintainer (a spare Halberd III in Guard set 1) before checking it against his question, which was about two halberds in one set. The review caught it (F2).
2. My first Grunt rotation put Orc Polearm II first. `test_generate_career_kits.py` failed: the Mordor cavalry and infantry career kits take the lowest-level, then most-carried, Mordor polearm, and Orc Polearm II is `requires_no_shield` beside those kits' shield. The rotation now keeps the lance in 7 of 14 sets. The gate worked; the lesson is to run `generate_career_kits.py --verify` before choosing a rotation for a low-tier troop.
