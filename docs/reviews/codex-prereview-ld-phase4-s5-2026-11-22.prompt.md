## What the change does

- `Main/Adapters/LongDefeatBattlefieldAdapter.Armoury.cs` (new): `SwapGear(LdLoadout)` puts a kit on the lord's own live body: `BuildKit`, `Agent.UpdateSpawnEquipmentAndRefreshVisuals(kit)`, then `MissionGameModels.Current.AgentStatCalculateModel.InitializeAgentStats(lord, lord.SpawnEquipment, lord.AgentDrivenProperties, null)`, then `lord.UpdateAgentProperties()`; it reads armour, health, mount and the wielded item before and after. `StartErrand`, `TeleportErrand`, `EndErrand` walk the AI-driven main agent to a place with `Agent.SetScriptedPosition`, teleport him, and release the walk with `DisableScriptedMovement`, holding the body by reference. `ReadLordArmour` reads the main agent's driven armour.
- `Main/Features/LongDefeat/LongDefeatDirector.Armoury.cs` (new): serves `RunEffects.SwapGear` and `RunEffects.Errand` in `Apply`, releases an open walk at the run's end, and dispatches the bot's place actions (`PlayPlace`).
- Every spawn of the lord (start, rise, sally remount) now builds `SurvivalRun.EffectiveLoadout` (the class kit with the owned gear sets laid over it) in place of `_plan.Loadout`.
- `LocationOffer.Offered` becomes `(true, false, false)`: the build can sell the armoury. Nothing ships: no fortress records an `armoury` point and no shop lists a set.
- `fort-points` gains an armoury leg, and `SurvivalRun.DropPlace` hides a place whose recorded point is unusable.
- The wrapper `tools/run_long_defeat.py`: `LOCATION_OFFERED`, `recorded_fort_points`; `tools/ingame_checks/1079.toml` comments.

Plan and design: `docs/features/long-defeat/phase4-plan.md` (the "Re-baseline, 2026-11-21" section wins over the text below it; D36, D44, D46 and the Slice 5 row), and the new section "The live gear swap and the bot's errand" in `docs/features/long-defeat.md`.
