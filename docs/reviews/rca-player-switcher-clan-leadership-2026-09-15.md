## Why each agent missed finding 1

- **Standards:** out of scope; the guard is a rule question, not an architecture one.
- **Compatibility:** verified `Hero.IsAlive` exists and read its body, but was asked to verify members, not to judge whether the member was the right one for the guard.
- **Efficiency:** out of scope.
- **Completeness:** confirmed one test per state-table row, which is true; the table itself was one row short of the doc comment's claim, and a coverage check cannot see a row that was never enumerated.
- **Data flow:** found it. The agent was asked to enumerate every entity state the mutation could meet, did so from `CharacterStates` rather than from the table, and compared the two.

The general shape: a state table written by the author is only as complete as the author's enumeration. The data-flow agent's instruction to enumerate from the engine's state enum, independently of the code, is what caught it, and the same instruction (rule 5, "Lifecycle Completeness") should be read as "enumerate from the source of truth, then diff against the code", not "check the code's own list".

## Codex review 113: suspect disposition

Seven suspects were handed over; Codex disputed six with decompiled lines and confirmed one as a pre-existing vanilla hazard. The dispositions worth keeping: the promotion's ordering is coherent in both party shapes (a hero riding in the old king's party is re-headed together with `LordPartyComponent.Owner`, `PartyComponent.cs:79-85`, `LordPartyComponent.cs:158-166`); a nested `ShowInquiry` from an inquiry callback is the same thing vanilla's single-clan branch does; `ExecuteDone` reads nothing that a selection would have set; the session-launch message survives because `GauntletChatLogView` subscribes before the initial module screen and the queue is not cleared on a game-object change (`MPChatVM.cs:537-547, 684-689, 702-714`); `Kingdom.Leader` is computed and every UI consumer checked rebuilds after session launch; and `KingdomManagementVM.ForceDecideDecision` replacing a live `CurrentDecision` never finalizes the old item (vanilla, untouched by this change, noted in the registry as a known hazard). Codex also corrected the reflection-site catalogue's "private readonly" on `_decision` to "protected readonly".

## Feedback memories to codify

None new. The lesson is a small one and belongs in the adapters category file, not in a standing memory: `Hero.IsAlive` is `!IsDead`, and any guard that means "this hero can be acted on" must say which of `Active`, `Prisoner`, `Fugitive`, `Released`, `Disabled`, `NotSpawned` it admits.
