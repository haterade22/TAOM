# RCA: the three heroes free in the Helm's Deep defence (#1366)

Findings from the tier 1 review of #1366, Phase 6 of #1325, on the uncommitted worktree `feat/hero-companions` over 317413337. Before any builder started, a second architect tried to refute the pinned plan, and a spike proved that a new campaign loads the mission heroes and the extra arrow. The first review wave ran four lenses (5 Data flow, 2 Engine compatibility, 7 XML and ModuleData, Tooling) and found one HIGH and five MEDIUM. Because of the HIGH, a second wave (1 Standards, 3 Efficiency, 4 Completeness, 6 Design) read the corrected tree. The Codex pass did not run: the maintainer said to skip it while the account has no usage.

The story mission itself was opened later the same day by the driver of #1368 (run 20261116-075342, build 4baa2f53a): `python tools/ingame_verdict.py --issue 1366` reads PASS for all seven rows, with the three hero lines at `is_hero=0 campaign=1`, their own weapons and `health_factor=3.00`, and Aragorn's aura on 102 soldiers. That proves the spawn kit and the aura source. The take-over (findings 3 and 4) and the faces (finding 5) are not exercised by an unattended run: they rest on unit tests and source pins, and a person's look is owed.

That run also shows a defect no reviewer found: `moved_out=1517` on the hunters line. About 20 garrison soldiers a second were put into the heroes' formation and moved out again by the repair, for the whole mission; the Long Defeat never shows it. It has its own issue (#1370).

Correction, 2026-11-16: "for the whole mission" was wrong. A second run with one temporary log line (`E:/Temp/scenario-runs/20261116-081024`) shows that about once a second during the preparation before the first wave (24 repairs in 21 seconds, the last in the second the first wave started, none after it) the whole Infantry formation is moved into the heroes' formation and out again by the repair. The mover is the engine's castle tactic, which before the first wave has one lane and two melee formations and consolidates the garrison's AI-controlled Infantry formation into the formation that is off AI control (`TacticComponent.cs:187-210`, `TacticDefendCastle.cs:1005-1016`). Not known: why the Long Defeat, which has the same heroes, the same tactic and a muster of the same kind, never shows it; its split starts only after its muster, so the split is not the reason. No fix is in the tree; #1370 is open.

One thing the Long Defeat run shows that no check asked for: Legolas had 25 kills at the end of wave 1 and 41 at the end of wave 2, and shot 91 arrows before he fell in wave 3. In the run before this change his first body had 10 and 26 kills at those points and shot 55 arrows before he fell in wave 3. (The hunters line counts a body's kills for its whole life.) `level="36"` is the one change to him between the two (finding 7). Whether the level or chance moved the numbers is not known: one run each, with no control.

## Root-cause pattern

Findings 3, 4 and 5 have one cause. The three characters are heroes to the engine in a custom game and plain characters in a campaign, and every rule the engine keys on `IsHero` flips between the two: the take-over, the fixed equipment, the face cache, the battle tier. The plan found the ones in the mission code. The ones it missed sat behind a rule the other mode had closed (the take-over) or in a layer it did not read (the views). Findings 1 and 2 are the second pattern of this work: a proof line is written from one more place, and the manifests that read it are not asked again.

## Why each reviewer missed these

- **The architect's pinned plan** listed the `IsHero` sites it found and accepted the take-over with one consequence. It named the face cache flag and did not read the view behind it.
- **The refuting architect** corrected the plan's data and checks and did not follow the take-over into the director.
- **The builders** wrote that the upgrade filter is not reached, from the plan's table, without reading its second caller. **Lens 1** read the caller.
- **Lens 5** followed the take-over through `AdoptIfNew` to the health write and the place test. **The tooling lens** proved finding 1 by running the real manifest on a made-up mixed session. **Lens 7** found the face cache from the view list. **The fix builder** found the unbooked death while it traced a side note.
