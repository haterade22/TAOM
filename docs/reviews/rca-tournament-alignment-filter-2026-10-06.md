## Root-cause pattern: a table lookup with a fallback is only as right as the pair you hand it

Findings 1 and 2 share a shape. `alignment.json` is keyed on ids that do not say what they are (`battania` is Khand, `empire` is Dunland), and `ResolveSide(id, culture)` silently changes meaning when the id misses: unlisted and Neutral both fall through to whatever culture the caller supplies. Both bugs came from reasoning about the table from memory rather than from its data: what an id means, and what the fallback receives. Findings 3 and 4 are the same mistake one level down: assuming a culture's troops share its culture.

## Why each agent missed what it missed

- **Standards** found the duplicate test, the nullable gap, the stale comments and the doc How-To; side semantics are outside its checklist.
- **Engine compatibility** verified all 39 engine reads and found findings 1, 3 (as F5) and the wrong caller list; it did not separate basic from elite troop cultures.
- **Data flow** found 1, 2 and 3, and its fix direction (basic and filler cultures) would have covered Umbar; it did not single out Umbar's basic-vs-elite split as its own case.
- **Efficiency** found the per-slot filler mapping; semantics are outside its scope.
- **Completeness** found the INDEX, catalogue, default-test and issue-drift gaps.
- **Design** proposed the cleanest shapes (`MapFaction` for the host, filler-first ordering), but narrowed the exemption to the filler's culture, which is finding 4.
