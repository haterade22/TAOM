# RCA: Co-op audit, gate client-side world writes (#1086), 2026-11-06

Findings from the review of the #1086 change: an audit of 57 TAOM campaign behaviors for writes to shared world
state on a BannerlordCoop client, with gates in six behaviors and five player actions. The reviews were a Codex
pre-review (`ultra`), the Engine, Data flow and XML lenses, then, after a HIGH, the Standards, Efficiency,
Completeness and Design lenses, a Codex pass on the fixes (`high`) and a Data flow re-review. Each finding was
re-read against the code before it was acted on.

Not applied: `PlayerPossessionBehavior` stays ungated as a known limit, by Mike's decision. `Found`, `Upgrade` and
`Dismantle` keep no service gate: they are reached only from menus closed to a guest (the doc says so). The
`CoopPresence.IsActive` lock cache (Efficiency lens) was rejected under the simplicity criterion.

Follow-ups, not in this change: `SupplyCaravanEncounterPatch` ends a guest's meeting with the host's caravan
(rationale corrected only); final guards in `TryPlaceOrder` and the Refuge actions; `KingdomJoinOffer` after Coop
packages the hero; ungated career quest rewards; the `_exSpouses` reflection-site rows; a Patch96 double Join when
vanilla throws inside the reflected call.

Not covered by any of this: an in-game BannerlordCoop session. Every claim about Coop runtime state is from its
source, not a run.
