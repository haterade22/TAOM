# RCA: Seat of Seeing, stages S2 and S3 (deep review, 2026-11-04)

**Top line:** the tier 1 `/deep-review` of the screen work (S2) and of keeping to a target (S3) found two defects that no run or test could reach because the setting behind them defaults off and the trial only plays one kind of battle: keep-open faults the Seat in every mission with no order handler, and it cancels the Transfer order. Tooling found that the judge passed a run with the Seat's own patch category dead and one with every hot key dead, because it scanned only for the line the contract named. The fifth field run found the sixth kind of defect by itself: the trial's own kill threw inside vanilla's morale model, so the company never emptied. The first wave (lens 7, Tooling, lens 5, lens 2) and Codex at `high` gave 3 HIGH and a set of MEDIUM and LOW items; fixes R1 to R28 applied them. Tooling's HIGH findings called the second wave (standards, efficiency, completeness and design at `high`), which found one call on every frame, untested conjuncts and text that had outlived its stage; fixes W1 to W28 applied those and 18 of its 20 design proposals. A re-review of those two rounds (the convergence lens, Tooling, Efficiency and Codex at `high`) found no HIGH, two MEDIUM and a set of LOW items. Fixes X1 to X8 applied them, and tests prove that round with no further re-review, because nothing in it is above MEDIUM. S3's company let-go and the real keys, axes and mouse have not yet been proved in the game.
