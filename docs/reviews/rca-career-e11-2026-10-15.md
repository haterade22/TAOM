# RCA: career forks engine, step E11 and E11b campaign passives (#769), 2026-10-15

## Top-line

E11 adds ten campaign career passives, each read by a TAOM GameModel override: InfluenceGain,
RecruitCost, PrisonerConversion, FoodConsumption, WoundRecovery, DailyGold, LootGold, RaidLoot, and two
new model slots, TroopTraining (`TaomPartyTrainingModel`) and TradePrices (`TaomPartyTradeModel`). The
builder's brief carried, for the first time, a `Lessons:` list of named lesson headings (the preventive
action of the E8 to E10 RCA), and the review found none of those lessons repeated.

`/deep-review` ran six lenses (data flow, engine, standards, completeness, design, XML; efficiency is
folded into design for this change, which adds no hot path). Nothing was CRITICAL or HIGH. Two findings
were MEDIUM: `ScaleByPassive` dropped a whole bonus past -100% (a cliff where four texts promised a floor),
and the healing model crossed the 150-line entry-point ceiling. The data-flow lens traced all ten types end
to end and found every single-player path connected; the engine lens verified all eleven override
signatures on the installed v1.5.5 DLLs.

## Root-cause patterns

1. **A contract stated twice in one summary** (1). The text that the test pinned and the text the callers
   read disagreed. A summary that states an edge case states it once.
2. **A key or reach chosen by analogy** (3). "Like HeroHealing" carried an owner-first reach the sibling
   types had rejected. The brief now asks who receives each effect.

## What the Lessons slot changed

The brief named ten lesson headings. The review found no repeat of any of them: every model body is a
boundary conversion plus one delegate, every cost is applied before its vanilla clamp, every flat term
comes last, every new dependency is injected, and each in-game check names its evidence. The E8 to E10
review, without the slot, repeated seven lessons.
