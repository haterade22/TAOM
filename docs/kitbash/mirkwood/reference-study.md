# Mirkwood reference study

Nine reference screenshots supplied on 2026-11-26 are stored in the Mirkwood work folder
under `refs/owner_2026-11-26`. The local manifest records the original filenames and hashes.
The design studies use our own geometry and materials.

| Image | What to carry into the architecture |
| --- | --- |
| `ref_01` | A threshold in shade. Slim bundled stone piers flare at the base and head. Deep door recesses sit behind them and give the stone a strong silhouette. |
| `ref_02` | Timber ribs branch overhead around the routes below. Light comes from separated amber points. The quiet spans between lights keep the depth visible. |
| `ref_03` | A freestanding stone screen sits in front of a recessed threshold. The rock supports the composition, and layered sills lead down to the approach. |
| `ref_04` | A bridge crosses a dark vertical space. The underside carries branching structure and the top reads as a continuous route. |
| `ref_05` | A short stone span and tall entry belong to one approach. The light from the ravine makes the span visible before the doorway. |
| `ref_06` | The scene sequence moves through giant roots, narrow crossings, compressed passages and wider lit rooms. Use it for the placement of built members around natural terrain. |
| `ref_07` | Columns split into overhead vault ribs. Slender vertical members and the shadow between them establish the scale of the room. |
| `ref_08` | Deep, repeated arched recesses cut into a continuous stone face. A warm wash touches the lower jambs, and the upper mass stays cool. |
| `ref_09` | The route passes over a low branching span below an upper timber gallery. Warm pools mark turns and stairs. The ravine and taller stone behind the route supply the scale. |
