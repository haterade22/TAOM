## 1. AI Army system rework (v1.4.0 → v1.4.3 iteration)

### v1.4.0 (initial rework)
- Minimum army-size requirement based on kingdom's overall mobile military strength.
- AI clan minimum influence to create army raised: **50 → 100**.
- Increased influence AI nobles spend forming armies.
- Defensive activities removed as valid army-creation / maintenance condition. Armies disband after 2 days if too weak to besiege.
- "Your army will now be disbanded if you kick out all the army members."

### v1.4.3 (replacement logic)
- **Minimum total strength of 1000** to form an army.
- ~30% of a faction's parties always remain outside any army.
- Multiple armies allowed when party pool supports it.
- This replaces the 1.4.0 system that penalized armies based on faction-wide army count (which was pushing factions toward single-army-only).

### Mid-formation step-out (v1.4.3)
- Units in tight formations (e.g. shield wall) briefly step out to land an attack, then return.

### Auto-resolve siege duration
- Doubled (v1.4.0).

### TAOM impact
- 🔥 **`TaomArmyManagementModel`** — base class signature likely changed.
- 🔥 **`TaomTargetScoreModel`** — besieger army targeting; `Patch22_ArmyTargeting` border-proximity floor.
- 🟡 Any TAOM logic that assumed the old 50-influence world is now off-balance.
- 🟡 `RevoltTuning` feature may interact unexpectedly.

## 2. Diplomacy / Alliance / Trade rewrite (v1.4.0)

### War / Peace
- Removed second-degree neighbor exposure from diplomatic considerations. **Kingdoms without shared bordering settlements no longer declare war on each other** and are more likely to declare peace.

### Alliance Mechanics
- **Alliance Renewal decision** — once an alliance ends (cancellation or completion), both parties are prompted to renew. Other diplomatic options (war declarations) are deferred until the renewal is resolved.
- Alliance scoring now factors in:
  - Inter-ruler clan + ruler-to-any-clan marriages (the latter weighted less)
  - Owning settlements of another faction's culture = treated as hostile occupation by original owners (Empire factions especially)
  - Being in a trade agreement with each other
- Penalty added to forming a 2nd alliance while already in one.
- **Honor + relationship penalties for declaring war on an allied kingdom**.

### Call to War
- Penalties for denying.
- Relationship bonus for accepting.
- Call-to-War duration extends alliance if it would otherwise end mid-conflict.
- Kingdoms called to war won't consider peace for that war until the Call-to-War duration ends.

### Trade Agreements
- **Limited to neighboring kingdoms** only.
- Port-only kingdoms can't trade with no-port kingdoms (WS).
- Scoring factors in: marriages, prosperity, town security, ally relationship.
- **Caravans (and naval convoys in WS) now generate gold for the owning kingdom on each settlement visit**; income distributed daily among vassals.

### Other diplomacy changes
- Initial AI-noble / AI-notable relationships less clustered at neutral; traits play stronger role.
- Enemy threshold raised to **-50**.
- Faction-leader starting relationships now check for clashing traits.
- Support level display now descriptive (Low / Slight / Good / Strong) instead of percentages.
- War progress normalized one-side-zero (e.g. 60-0 instead of 80-20).
- **Abstain option when voting on kingdom decisions as the ruler**.
- Allied settlements + parties get blue highlight on map.

### TAOM impact
- 🔥 **`TaomAllianceModel`** — April-2026 fix area; re-verify signature.
- 🔥 **`TaomDiplomacyModel`** — race-based enmity may need re-stating against new base.
- 🔥 **`TaomKingdomDecisionPermissionModel`** — abstain option likely adds a new method.
- 🟡 **`TaomExecutionRelationModel`** — relationship math shifted, deltas may need re-tuning.
- 🟡 **`Patch12_WarOfTheRing`** + **`Patch24_BannerDriftGuard`** — kingdom state transitions adjacent to new alliance machinery.

## 3. Battle & Combat changes (v1.4.0)

### Survival chance reverted from v1.3.3 nerf
- **Blunt damage probability** in player battles: 10% → **30%**.
- **Casualty survival chance per Medicine level**: +0.0025% → **+0.01%**.
- **"Doctor's Oath" perk** ("Your medicine skill partially applies to enemy casualties, increasing potential prisoners"): 0.1% → **1%**.

### Auto-resolve
- Auto-resolved siege duration **doubled**.
- **Default cavalry bonus REMOVED** from auto-resolve. Terrain-specific effects still apply. Big balance shift for cav-heavy factions.

### Combat AI
- Melee units commit to attacks more reliably and face targets correctly.
- Spear infantry aim better at mounts.
- Cavalry better at swing timing + direction choice.
- Disabled staggering of archers readying when approached by mounts.
- Reduced accuracy penalty for throwing weapons when moving toward the throw direction.
- AI uses throwing weapons while running (was: slow to walk).
- Sling accuracy now depends on Throwing skill.
- Added shaky-camera near AoE missile impacts.

### Mechanics changes
- **Knockdown functionality added to all two-handed maces** (stats adjusted accordingly).
- **One-handed bash reach unified** with two-handed bash reach.
- Pikemen in mixed formations now move to front to brace when shielded troops are in front.

### Spawning
- **Team spawning distance in land battles based on initial deployment count only** (not reinforcements). Reduces approach times.
- Multi-team deployment respects spawn paths + undeployable zones.

### Siege ranged AI
- Ranged siege weapons factor in terrain + line-of-sight; no firing at obstructed targets.

### TAOM impact
- 🔥 **`TaomPartyHealingModel`** — Medicine constant restored to 1.3.3 baseline; culture survival multipliers need re-tuning.
- 🔥 **`TaomMilitaryPowerModel`** — auto-resolve no longer cavalry-biased; T7-T10 troop power values may need rebalance.
- 🔥 **`TaomCombatSimulationModel`** — auto-resolve duration doubled; blunt/cut ratios per battle type need re-verify.
- 🟡 **`TaomBattleRewardModel`** — April-2026 fix area.
- 🟡 **`MixedFormations`** — interacts with new bracing + step-out behavior.
- 🟡 **`CompanionTactics`** — interacts with new commit-to-attack behavior.
- 🟡 **`Patch19_CustomBattles`** — spawning logic changed; custom-battle commander spawning may need verification.

## 4. Stealth & Disguise / Hideouts (v1.4.0)

### Stealth missions (urban / disguise)
- Vision cones + uphill visibility refined.
- Reduced handheld torch impact on visibility.
- Adjusted cautiousness indicator behavior.
- Improved detection responsiveness at close range.
- Throwing-knife sneak-attack effectiveness adjusted.
- Prison-break: must eliminate guards within countdown if spotted, otherwise mission fail.
- **Smuggler Connection perk now allows trade in hostile towns after setting up contact**.
- Disguise: cannot access Port while disguised.

### Hideouts
- **Sentry-elimination requirement removed** before campfire interaction.
- Roguery XP bonus for reaching campfire without killing/alerting.
- **Skill-based success chance for "send troops to clear"** (Tactics + Roguery skills); on success, no loot, no XP.
- Assault clears: gives only battle loot.
- Stealth clears: gives additional loot (hideout stash / trade goods).
- Bandit count in assault scenes: **20-29** (raised).
- Hideout icon now indicates bandit-party count.

### TAOM impact
- 🟢 **Low.** TAOM doesn't override hideout / stealth missions. Spider feature (currently disabled) and any custom bandit lair logic would inherit base behavior silently — probably fine.

## 5. Clan & Party (v1.4.0)

### Campaign map
- **Party visibility now depends on party size + terrain**. Larger parties spotted from farther; bandits/villagers must get closer. Forest reduces detection.

### Party Roles
- 🔥 **Each clan member can now hold up to two party roles simultaneously**. Reduces companion-limit pressure.
- Role assignment + dialogue updated.

### Clan tiers
- **Reduced initial clan tiers of several Vlandian clans**.
- **Raised initial clan tiers of select Battanian clans**.

### Other
- "Cries in the Mist" random event: "ignore superstitions, press on" now grants Calculating/Daring/Closefisted trait points, reduces party morale, may injure troops.
- Player sibling generation: can get any hairstyle from the player's culture.

- 🟡 **`TaomHeroCreationModel`** — sibling hairstyle now culture-aware.
- 🟡 **`TaomPartySpeedModel`** — terrain visibility partly intersects; verify forest-speed feats still compose correctly.

## 7. Equipment System Overhaul (v1.4.3) — **biggest single change**

See dedicated doc: [v1.4.x-equipment-overhaul.md](v1.4.x-equipment-overhaul.md).

Summary:
- `EquipmentFlags` enum reduced. 13 flags removed; 5 new flags introduced.
- `civilian="true"` on `<EquipmentSet>` deprecated → use `equipmentType="Civilian"` (enum: Battle / Civilian / Stealth).
- `CharacterObject.civilianTemplate` + `battleTemplate` deprecated.
- `EquipmentSelectionModel` return types changed: `MBList<MBEquipmentRoster>` → single `Equipment`.
- New `Stealth` equipment type (third value alongside Battle / Civilian).
- Every main culture must provide at least: `IsLordTemplate`, `IsLordTemplate|IsChildEquipmentTemplate`, `IsLordTemplate|IsTeenagerEquipmentTemplate`, `IsKingdomRulerTemplate`, plus female variants.

### TAOM impact
- 🔥 **3,372 occurrences of `civilian="true"` across 51 files** require migration.
- 🔥 **`Main/Adapters/ChildCreatorAdapter.cs:40`** API break — return type changed.
- 🔥 **~40 new equipment rosters needed across 10 custom cultures**.
- 🟡 **`taom_child_equipment_templates.xml`** — uses old `EquipmentFlags` names.

## 8. Heroes & Equipment (v1.4.3)

- When kingdom ruler changes: new ruler auto-switches to ruler-tier equipment; previous ruler reverts to standard lord equipment.
- Companions promoted to lord status get culture-appropriate lord equipment (battle + civilian).
- Married couples + children display correct cultural equipment in marriage cutscenes + aging events.
- Heroes coming of age / reaching teen age / being born — receive correct equipment set (previously: "naked" appearance bug).
- Heirs and newly-promoted clan leaders no longer wear wrong-culture / fallback equipment.
- Fixed male heroes wearing female clothes; fixed some nobles spawning without weapons.

### ⚠️ Silent behavioral change

**`OnRulingClanChanged` parameter semantics flipped**: the `Clan` argument used to mean **new ruler**; it now means **old ruler**.

TAOM doesn't subscribe directly (grep verified 0 occurrences). BUT:
- `Patch24_BannerDriftGuard` patches `Clan.UpdateBannerColorsAccordingToKingdom` / `UpdateBannerColor` — adjacent territory.
- `Patch12_WarOfTheRing` handles kingdom state transitions.

Audit both during S4 (Harmony patches phase).

### TAOM impact
- 🔥 **`Patch24_BannerDriftGuard`** + **`Patch12_WarOfTheRing`** — verify they don't read ruling-clan state at wrong moment relative to vanilla's new ruler-equipment-swap logic.
- 🟡 **`NamedCompanions`** + **`CharacterCreation`** + **`Patch29_CCBodyProperties`** — depend on equipment rosters being correctly tagged; will surface naked-NPC bugs if our cultures lack the mandatory rosters (see §7).
- 🟡 **`Patch20_NarrativeHorseGuard`** — suppresses CC narrative horse crashes for no-mount cultures; may be triggered or unblocked by new equipment template rules.

## N. Scenes — renames/removals (TAOM impact: HIGH — crashes) — added 2026-05-28

v1.4.5 **renamed and removed scenes** that TAOM's data referenced by the old names. A `scene_name` (or `sp_battle_scenes.xml` Scene id) that no longer resolves crashes the game when that scene loads — entering a settlement/house or fighting a field battle on the affected map cell. Surfaces as "battles near a specific place crash," far from the stale value.

Confirmed v1.4.5 changes hit:
- **House-interior scenes renamed.** `<culture>_house_a_interior_house` → culture-specific new names (`battania_town_house_b_interior_b_house`, `khuzait_house_c_interior_a_house`, `sturgia_town_house_d1_interior_b_house`, `vlandia_city_house_a_interior_house`). TAOM towns' `house_1/2/3` locations still used the old names (61 stale refs); the `tavern` locations had already been updated.
- **`sp_battle_scenes.xml`** — TAOM's catch-all `battle_terrain_extended` (map indices 158–255) referenced a scene that doesn't exist → field-battle crash on those cells. Repointed to `battle_terrain_r`.

### Post-version-bump scene audit (RUN THIS after every Bannerlord bump)

```bash
python tools/audit_scene_names.py     # settlement scene_name refs vs on-disk SceneObj
python tools/audit_battle_scenes.py   # sp_battle_scenes.xml Scene ids vs vanilla + SceneObj; 0-255 coverage
python tools/remap_stale_scene_names.py --dry-run   # then --apply --backup
```

Full how-to + findings: [`docs/reference/scene-reference-audit.md`](../reference/scene-reference-audit.md). Auto-loaded rule when editing the relevant XML: [`.claude/rules/vanilla-data-comparison.md`](../../.claude/rules/vanilla-data-comparison.md). This is one instance of the general discipline: **diff TAOM data against the currently installed vanilla version before relying on it** — vanilla scenes, item ids, culture/clan ids, and XML schemas all drift between versions.
