# Bannerlord v1.5.2: impact on TAOM

**Date:** 2026-09-14 · **Branch:** `bannerlord-1.5.x` (cut from trunk `bannerlord-1.4.5` at
v2.0.28, `6bab10ae`) · **Engine:** client v1.5.2 buildid 25055496, Modding Kit v1.5.2 buildid
25055627, Steam beta branch · **Predecessor:** the v1.5.0 port of 2026-08-20, preserved as tag
`archive/bannerlord-1.5.0-port` (`3bb4669f`), whose impact write-up lives on that tag at
`docs/migration/v1.5.0-impact.md`.

## Bottom line

- **The port is a re-port, not a merge.** Trunk took 240 commits of v1.4.8 work after the v1.5.0
  fork; the old branch's 183 changed files hit 75 real conflicts against it. Every v1.5.0 change
  was re-derived on top of current trunk against the v1.5.2 engine, file by file, nothing
  cherry-picked whole.
- **The Modding Kit moved with the client this time.** That is what the v1.5.0 port was parked
  on: terrain baked by pre-1.5 tools never satisfied `SceneView.CheckSceneReadyToRender()`, and
  the Kit was still v1.4.8 so the map could not be re-baked. It can be now. Re-saving `TAOM_Map`
  in the v1.5.2 editor and reaching the campaign map is the one test that gates everything below;
  nothing offline settles it.
- **Signatures were the small half.** Nine compile-level fixes carried the build; the member-level
  body diff (355 rows) then found what no compile or test gate could: one full-replacement
  GameModel override still carrying the v1.4.8 shape of a body that moved, and one patch target
  that resolved but had gone abstract. Both fixed. Every additive override inherited the
  engine's `BattleEnvironment` perk rework and the new personality-trait system for free.
- **Data moved under the engine's feet.** Cultures need an `executioner`; kingdom naming is
  culture-keyed now (44 faction-keyed overrides went dead, and were removed); the
  character-creation event fires ten times; `BasicCharacterObject` now materialises a
  `MBCharacterSkills` per `<skills>` block.
- **Gates at the last commit (`bc5b5d71`):** `Main` and `TAOM.Tests` 0 errors, full suite
  **9,173 passed / 2 skipped / 0 failed** of 9,175 (trunk's last green was 9,045; the difference
  is new gates), `BindingVerification` 304 at the compile commit and every later addition green
  inside the full run, API snapshot **46 GameModels / 233 patches** at the compile commit,
  action-set parity 0 gaps, mount parity clean, `lint_docs` zero long dashes in new prose.

## The change-set oracle

The Steam update overwrote the vanilla `ModuleData`, GUI XML and `XmlSchemas` in place. What
survives is mtimes: everything the update wrote is stamped `2026-09-14 12:12` and nothing else
is. `docs/migration/v1.5.2-changeset.txt` (`79c612b2`) records 4,599 files under `Modules/`, `bin/`
and `XmlSchemas/` with size and stamp, captured before any regeneration or build touched the
install. **A Steam "verify integrity" destroys this signal**; do not run one before the next
bump's manifest is captured. Among the rows: `SandBox/ModuleData/lords.xml` (`heroes.xml` did not
move), the vanilla `SceneObj` rewrites and a `TileSets` tree of the new terrain pipeline, which is
the physical form of the map re-bake requirement.

## The body diff (the deliverable of this bump)

[v1.5.2-diff-ranked.md](v1.5.2-diff-ranked.md) has every row. The shape: 355 bound members,
51 with a changed body, six review agents in batches with the diff and the TAOM side.

- **One drift.** The recruitment-cost perk discounts, re-implemented in a full-replacement
  override, resolved on the buyer; v1.5.2 resolves them on the buyer's party leader. Fixed.
- **One broken target.** `ClanPartyItemVM.UpdateProperties`, abstract, resolved by name and
  would have thrown at apply time, taking the rest of `Patch17_TroopWeight` with it. Fixed.
- **Everything additive was fine.** 28 GameModel rows call `base.` first and layer TAOM on top;
  all inherited the engine's changes. That is the design argument for the additive shape, in
  numbers.
- **The engine themes** (`BattleEnvironment` on every perk helper, `TraitEffectHelper`, helpers
  owning the `HasPerk` checks, two new starting options, forest -20% and the dropped
  `Objective` bonus in target scoring) are catalogued in the ranked doc.
- **Batch E (ten patch targets) all OK**, including the two big rewrites: `PlayerEncounter.FinalizeBattle`
  became an eleven-way `MapEventComponent` dispatch that still fires `MapEventEnded` synchronously
  before `Finish()`'s next statement (what `Patch85` relies on), and the party-icon literal split
  that `Patch53` already carries two transpilers for.
- **Batch F: five constructor patches OK** (all five constructors byte-identical across the bump),
  three loaders OK, and one **DATA-RISK found and fixed**: `BasicCharacterObject.Deserialize` now
  applies an inline `<skills>` block even when `skill_template` resolved, so 64 lords in
  `characters/lords.xml` and 19 vanilla-id lords written by `lords.xslt` (Sauron, the Witch-king,
  the Nazgûl, the Black Númenóreans) whose documentation-mirror block had drifted from their
  SkillSet would have changed stats silently. `tools/sync_lord_inline_skills.py` re-synced the
  1,138 and 329 values and `LordInlineSkillParityTests` gates both files. That is the second
  finding of the pass, and like the first it hid behind an unchanged signature; the stylesheet
  half was found by the fix's own review, the first draft having read only `*.xml`.

## Data and resources

- **Faction naming is culture-keyed.** The engine reads `str_adjective_for_culture`,
  `str_faction_formal_name_for_culture`, `str_faction_informal_name_for_culture` and
  `str_neutral_term_for_culture`; TAOM covers all four for its 22 main cultures. Vanilla defines
  none for its own bandit cultures, and TAOM's eight hideout-only bandit sub-cultures follow that.
  `str_adjective_for_faction.*` / `str_short_term_for_faction.*` have no reader on v1.5.2 and are
  gone from TAOM.
- **Kingdom loader:** `title` is the formal name; a `str_kingdom_formal_name` text would trip a
  `FailedAssert` (TAOM has none). `Settlement` gained an optional `ferry_target`.
- **A module's stylesheet never sees its own XML.** `MBObjectManager.CreateMergedXmlFile` applies
  module i's XSLT to the merged document of modules 0..i-1 before merging module i's file.
  `XsltTemplateCoverageTests` (BindingVerification) now fails any template that selects nothing in
  the union of the vanilla inputs; 8 of 8 in-repo stylesheets are covered, `TAOM_Map`'s are not.
- **GUI resource scan against v1.5.2:** nine brushes named by TAOM prefabs are not defined by
  TAOM or vanilla (`Popup.Text.Small`, `CharacterDeveloper.DescriptionText`,
  `CharacterDeveloper.SkillNameText`, `Clan.TextInput`, `Header.Frame`,
  `SPCharacterDeveloper.Statistics.Fill`, `TextBox.Background`, `UI.SummaryScreen.Text`,
  `ButtonBrush1.Text`); whether v1.5.x removed them or they were always missing is not established.
  `Map\anchor_stroked` exists only in NavalDLC's sprite data (a hidden `PortIconWidget`,
  harmless). Six prefabs have no C# reference (`ROTsprite`, `EventXml`, `PoppyHUD`, `mainmenuHUD`,
  `CompositionSlider`, `ArmyComposition`).
- Action-set parity 0 gaps (`audit_action_set_parity.py`); mount parity clean
  (`audit_mount_parity.py`).

## BLOCKER carried over: the campaign map must be re-baked

Unchanged from v1.5.0 in mechanism, changed in remedy: open `TAOM_Map` in the **v1.5.2** Modding
Kit and save (the terrain re-bake the new `TileSets` pipeline needs), then in game rebuild the
distance cache (built 2026-08-26, navmesh re-saved 2026-09-14). **Do not delete
`TAOM_Map/RuntimeDataCache`** (21 GB): reported elsewhere, that turns the hang into a
`create_texture_array` crash. The `[MapLoad]` heartbeat (`Patch89`) is the instrument.

## Findings outstanding

- The nine undefined brushes and six unreferenced prefabs above (no crash path known; a brush
  that does not resolve renders with defaults).
- `CLAUDE.md` is over its 46,000 B budget (46,6xx); pre-existing, trimmed slightly here.
- `TaomPartyWageModel.cs` is 199 lines against the 150-line entry-point ceiling; pre-existing.
- `RoleTooltipDecorator` (CompanionTactics) resolves `PartyCharacterVM.TypeIconData` by reflection
  and never reads or writes the handle; its two doc comments describe a write that does not exist,
  and the `ReflectionSiteBindingTests` row pins a binding nothing depends on. Dead code from an
  earlier design, found by the body diff's reflection pass; pre-existing.
- `Patch85_EnlistedDetachDeferral` relies on `PlayerEncounter.Finish()` calling `FinalizeBattle()`
  immediately before `FinishEncounterInternal()`. v1.5.2 rewrote `FinalizeBattle` into an
  eleven-way `MapEventComponent` dispatch and kept the adjacency; a structural test pinning it
  (IL of `Finish`) would turn the next rewrite into a red test instead of a silent regression.
- No GitHub issue tracks the v1.5.2 bump (#463 was the v1.5.0 one, closed; #480 is the in-game
  checklist). Whether to open one is the maintainer's call; nothing here creates it.
