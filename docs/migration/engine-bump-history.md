# Engine bump history

Dated stories and calibrations behind the steps in [`/engine-bump`](../../.claude/skills/engine-bump/SKILL.md). The skill keeps the step and a one-sentence reason; this file keeps the incident, so the skill body stays small enough to survive a `/compact`.

## wEditor jump of 2026-08-10

`Win64_Shipping_wEditor` (the Modding Kit build) updates on its own Steam schedule and can sit at a different engine version than the shipping client. On 2026-08-10 it jumped `v1.4.5.114928` to `v1.4.8.119303`, three engine versions in one update, while the client moved from v1.4.7 to v1.4.8 (`public const string GameVersion` in `TaleWorlds.Library.cs`, `_editor_build_v1.4.5/` against `_editor_build/`). Every native offset derived from that binary before the jump was against a v1.4.5 image, and nothing signalled the change. Even at a matching version string the builds differ (the v1.4.5 client is `.115026`, the v1.4.5 editor `.114928`).

The rule that came out of it, kept in `/native-crash-triage` Phase 1 item 6: read `<game>/bin/Win64_Shipping_wEditor/Version.xml` and compare it with the crash report's `BannerlordVersion` before believing an RVA from that binary.

## Same-version hotfix of 2026-11-04

Steam can replace every engine DLL and keep the version string: changeset 125053 became 125270 on v1.5.6, with the Kit 41 minutes after the client. The drift banner stayed silent, and `taom-src` went on serving the old sources. The first sign was the red `BannerlordRefAsmVersion` test. Detail: [v1.5.6-impact.md](v1.5.6-impact.md), "Hotfix: changeset 125270".

## Partial baseline of 1.4.7

The 1.4.7 to 1.4.8 bump lost its baseline for the module-bin satellites. `_modules_build` did not exist yet, and the two `Win64_Shipping_*` folders the decompile script walked never held `SandBox.View`, `SandBox.ViewModelCollection`, `SandBox.GauntletUI` (plus its two `AutoGenerated` assemblies), `TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade.View`, `TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade.GauntletUI` (plus its two `AutoGenerated`), `TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade.Platform.PC`, or the StoryMode, Multiplayer, CustomBattle, NavalDLC, BirthAndDeath and FastMode satellites. TAOM patches into several of them (`AgentVisuals`, `CharacterTableau`, `MobilePartyVisual`, `SPInventoryVM`, the tournament controllers), so that bump's assembly diff was partial. Recovery came only from the per-type decompiles that `~/.taom-src/v1.4.7/` had cached: 42 of them from module DLLs, all identical against 1.4.8. Anything not cached had no 1.4.7 form left to compare. The 1.4.5 baseline lives at `E:\Decompiled_Bannerlord\_shipping_build_v1.4.5`.

## Per-bump file-diff calibration

- 1.4.6 left the combat assembly (`TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade`) and the interop assembly (`AutoGenerated`) byte-identical; only CampaignSystem and UI moved, so all gameplay change was native-internal.
- 1.4.8 moved 8 of 56 base-bin assemblies, 3 of those by build number alone.
- On 1.4.7 to 1.4.8 the `ilspycmd` nag line was `10.1.0.8386` in the baseline and `10.1.1.8388` in the fresh run, so a raw MD5 or diff loop reported all 56 shipping files changed. The skill's `grep -v` idiom exists for that.
- 1.5.0 rewrote 56 of 56 assemblies, which is why the member-level diff exists.

## v1.5.2 member diff

51 bodies changed. One was real drift: `TaomPartyWageModel.GetTroopRecruitmentCost`, a full replacement still carrying the v1.4.8 perk shape. One abstract-new patch target had passed `HarmonyPatchBindingTests` because an abstract method resolves by name.

## Orphaned tavern actions

`audit_action_set_parity.py` flags a root-level `<action>` (parented by `<action_sets>` instead of an `<action_set>`). Build 1.4.7.117484 tolerates these; the dedicated-server build 117131 throws `KeyNotFoundException` in `MBObjectManager.MergeElements` at `/action_sets/action` and dies on boot. The byte-identical file one build accepts, the next can reject, with no change of ours between them, and no single-player run reproduces it, so re-check the merge path per bump. 1.4.8 moved nothing here: `TaleWorlds.ObjectSystem`, which holds `MergeElements`, was byte-identical from 1.4.7 to 1.4.8 at 62,632 B. The audit found 168 stray elements live: twelve `as_<race>_female_villager_in_aserai_tavern` sets authored self-closing, orphaning the 14 female-conversation overrides that belong nested inside each. `tools/oneoff/fix_orphaned_tavern_conversation_actions.py --apply` fixes them.

## Dwarf action parity

The 1.3 to 1.4.6 dwarf water CTD came from 423 action types that had drifted between `as_human_warrior` and a standalone dwarf set. The audit after that confirmed `as_dwarf_warrior` was the only humanoid gap (1110 humanoid sets, 0 others). Creature mounts (spider, elephant, chariot) use a separate surface, checked by `audit_mount_parity.py`.
