feat(perf): v2.0.32 - per-mission perf rows and A/B compare from logs

tools/perf_runs.py turns any number of taom_debug logs into one row
per mission and compares two groups of runs, so a frame-time or memory
change is judged by numbers rather than by feel. Until now the
[MissionPerf] heartbeat had no reader, and triage_battle_load.py looks
at the last mission of a log only.

Each row carries the load buckets (through triage_battle_load.py's own
parser), the spawn window kept apart from the steady state by its
position, steady medians of fps and of average and p95 frame time, the
worst frame, GC collections per minute and, when the mission tick
profiler's lines are present, per-behaviour cost and hitch counts by
phase. Flags say when the numbers may not mean what they seem
(FRAME_CAP, MEMORY_PRESSURE, DIAG_ON, DIRTY_BUILD,
BUILD_PAIR_MISMATCH), and each names its evidence.

    python tools/perf_runs.py <logs>
    python tools/perf_runs.py compare --a <logs> --b <logs> --scene <id>

--json prints everything. compare refuses to mix Debug and Release
builds or texture settings unless --allow-mixed is given, and counts a
row with no readable texture setting as unchecked.

Nothing is skipped in silence. The report opens with one line per log
(path, size, lines, missions, lines of a known tag it could not parse)
and prints the first five unparsed lines verbatim. Every other
"[Tag] key=value" line inside a mission is kept on the row as extra
tags: a value runs to the next space-led key= outside brackets, and a
continuation line without a prefix takes the timestamp of the entry it
continues. The [MissionPerf] line is pinned by the same literal in the
C# and Python tests, and the heartbeat doc now says the first line
lands at about +6 s in battles, not +5 s.

Limits to know when reading the numbers: hitch counts come from
[Hitch] lines, which the tick profiler writes for the first 100 slow
frames of a mission only; summary lines whose text starts with a word
([AnimMem] summary:, [LoadXml] summary, [XmlMerge] summary) do not
reach the extra tags yet. Lift any external frame cap before an fps
A/B, or every row carries FRAME_CAP.
