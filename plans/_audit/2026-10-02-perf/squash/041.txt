feat(mission-perf): v2.0.32 - hitch probe; spawn, script, clip timing

A hitch probe now runs for every player by default, so a slow battle
frame in any player's log says where its time went. "Enable Hitch
Probe" (MCM, Battle Load Diagnostics, Mission Performance) is on by
default; it is installed once at game start, so turning it on needs a
restart, and turning it off stops measuring from the next mission.

Each frame slower than the hitch threshold (default 250 ms) writes a
[Hitch] line and right after it a [HitchDetail] line: the wait for the
agent tick, the mission tick, agent spawning, scene scripts, and
whether an animation clip was loading from disk. Every 5 s window adds
[TickProfile], [ScriptProfile], [AnimLoad] and, while agents spawn,
[SpawnProfile] lines; each mission ends with [TickSummary] and
[TickSummaryExtra]. Full [Hitch] lines stop after the first 100 of a
mission; later ones are counted.

The probe brackets five whole engine methods (Mission.OnPreTick,
Mission.WaitTickCompletion, Mission.OnTick,
ManagedScriptHolder.TickComponents and Mission.SpawnAgent) with a
prefix and a finalizer, and no transpiler. An offline benchmark puts
the brackets at about 0.48 microseconds per simulated frame, against a
target of 0.5 percent of a 10 ms frame; the install line logs a
separate start-up measurement of the profiler's bookkeeping alone. The
clip loading check calls the engine once a frame: the first measured
mission logs its median cost, and the check runs only while that
median is within 20 microseconds.

Per-type attribution (which behaviour, spawn callback or scene script
component) stays behind "Enable Tick Profiler", off by default,
because it needs two more transpilers. The frame boundary moved from
the tick profiler's patch to the probe's, so both share it; with the
profiler on, switching the probe off does not stop its timing, since
the profiler measures through the probe's patches. The
"[TickProfiler] off:" line now ends "no per-behaviour transpilers
installed", and the "probe install:" or "probe off:" line after it
says whether the probe is in.

Mission.SpawnAgent, Mission.WaitTickCompletion and
ManagedScriptHolder.TickComponents join PatchShield's exclusion list
for every player, so no shield finalizer runs on the bracketed
methods. The trade-off: a missing-API exception from any patch on
those three is no longer swallowed, and the patch is no longer
stripped, Patch23's banner colour patches on SpawnAgent included.
Whether SpawnAgent stays on the list is still the maintainer's
decision.
