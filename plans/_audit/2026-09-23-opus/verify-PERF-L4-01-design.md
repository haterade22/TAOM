## PERF-L4-01: Stop paying about 31 s of every game boot for 247 Native2Managed finalizers

- **Outcome**: CONFIRMED
- **Impact today**: HIGH for boot time (about 30 s of every launch, every player with the default),
  not a correctness or crash item.

**Decision search (what I looked for, and what came back):**

- `docs/adrs/*.md` (000 to 011): nothing on crash capture, boot or startup cost, or blanket
  finalizers. `git grep -i "boot|startup time|load time|OnSubModuleLoad"` over ADRs, `.claude/rules`
  and orientation.md finds nothing that covers this.
- `docs/ai-includes/orientation.md` trap index: no crash-report row. The "Persisted MCM defaults" row
  applies to the fix (json2 keeps `true`), and the finding already accounts for it.
- BRIEF "Decided tradeoffs": no row touches CrashReport or boot cost.
- `docs/features/crash-report.md:275-278` ("Performance"): speaks only to steady-state cost
  ("Steady-state cost: zero"; Native2Managed "Same zero-cost property"). It never costs the attach at
  boot, so it records no acceptance of a boot tax.
- **Closest thing to a decision, and why it does not cover this:**
  `Main/Features/CrashReport/CrashReportSettings.cs:7-9` records a "comprehensive capture" posture
  ("every signal is on by default; players who hit perf issues can disable specific sections"), and
  `Native2ManagedPatcher.cs:16-18` says the MCM toggle exists "if perf or compatibility ever becomes
  an issue". Both were written on the premise that the cost is zero, and both anticipate turning the
  sweep off when perf proves otherwise. They make default-on a recorded posture (so flipping the
  default is a posture reversal for the maintainer to approve), but they are not an accepted 30 s
  boot cost.
- `Main/SubModule.cs:187-193` (Codex #46 MED-01): decides the block's early placement for coverage,
  not its cost.
- `docs/features/mcm.md:94-100` and `TAOM.Tests/Features/Mcm/SettingRequireRestartPostureTests.cs:41`:
  decide that the toggle is startup-only and restart-required. They bind the fix (a renamed setting
  needs the same allowlist row; `nameof` makes a rename surface at compile time), not the finding.
- `docs/reviews/rca-native-skin-fixes-port-2026-05-26.md:23`: an accepted boot cost (about 1.4 s of
  signature scans) for a different, now parked, feature. It shows boot cost gets weighed when known;
  it does not extend to this block.
- `docs/migration/v1.5.2-impact.md:141`: notes "PatchShield pass 2 alone took 70 s" as an observation
  of the boot timeline, not a decision, and it is not in that file's "Findings outstanding" list.
- **The repo's own lessons point the other way.** `docs/reviews/lessons/harmony-il.md:5-8` ("Blanket-
  patching infrastructure must cost its per-call overhead against the hottest conceivable target")
  requires any component that patches methods it did not choose to document its wrapper cost.
  Native2Managed is such a component and documents "zero".
