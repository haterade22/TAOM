Run id: 2026-11-04-port148. Lens: RUNTIME-PERF. Read-only investigation; no source edited.
Refs: working tree = c5b84fb41 (local bannerlord-1.4.5); origin/bannerlord-1.4.5 tip 1b00d1acd; 1.5.x = origin/bannerlord-1.5.x tip bf3299143.

## Findings (appended as found)

### RUNTIME-PERF-01: The E3 "first battle freeze" is a main-thread block in the engine's shader compile, not TAOM per-frame code (negative result for this lens; owned by the freeze lens)

- **Severity / confidence**: P0 player impact, but NOT a TAOM hot-path defect. High confidence on the mechanism class, medium on the exact native site.
- **Evidence**
  - E3 `taom_debug_2026-11-01_20-00-00.log:217-223`: `FinishMissionLoadingDone` at +7909 ms, `WaitingForRender waitedMs=1 shaders=2720`, `WaitingForRender waitedMs=2259 shaders=2697`, then only `[MemSample]` every 30 s until 20:04:22 with `heapMB=466` flat on all four samples.
  - E4 `taom_debug_2026-11-01_20-16-56.log:2125-2153` (first tournament, `arena_aserai_a`): `WaitingForRender waitedMs=1 shaders=1569` at 20:21:20, nothing from the main thread for 68 s, then `waitedMs=68290 shaders=0` at 20:22:29 and `BattlePlayable agents=559`. Throughput about 23 shaders/s.
  - E4 `:5138-5164` (second tournament, `arena_empire_a`): `shaders=806`, then 3.5 min of `[MemSample]` only and the log ends with no `BattlePlayable`.
  - The marker is throttled to ONE line per second for as long as `MissionState.OnTick` runs: `Main/Features/BattleLoadDiagnostics/BattleLoadDiagnosticsService.cs:285-307` (`RenderWaitEmitIntervalMs = 1000`, `:37`), called every frame from `Hooks/MissionState_OnTick_RenderWait_Patch.cs:52-73` (working tree c5b84fb41). A 68 s gap between two lines therefore means `MissionState.OnTick` was not called at all for 68 s: the managed frame loop itself stopped, not just the first `Mission.Tick`.
  - `[MemSample]` keeps printing because it is a thread-pool `System.Threading.Timer` (`MemoryPressureSampler.cs:54`), which is why the log "looks alive".
- **Mechanism**: `FinishMissionLoading` ends with `Scene.ResumeLoadingRenderings`, which starts the compile flood (TAOM's own research, `BattleLoadRenderWaitProbe.cs:8-14`, `MissionState_OnTick_RenderWait_Patch.cs:10-23`, verified there against v1.4.8 `MissionState.cs:110-113`). The 1.5.x line states for its engine that "the engine's main thread is blocked while it compiles" (`git show origin/bannerlord-1.5.x:docs/features/shader-compile-notice.md`, Architecture); the 68 s gap is the v1.4.8 evidence of the same block mid-session. With no managed main-thread tick, none of TAOM's 25 mission behaviours, Harmony postfixes or loggers can run, so no TAOM hot path can be the cause. At E4's 23 shaders/s, E3's 2,697 pending would need about 117 s; the E3 log ends about 92 s into the block, and E3 line 17 says the previous session's load of the same scene `battle_terrain_031` "never reached playable". Reading: the player killed a working compile twice. Whether a killed compile keeps the shaders it finished is UNVERIFIED (verify: rgl_log of a session killed mid-compile, then the next session's `compile_shader` count for the same scene).
- **Why it matters to this lens**: it rules TAOM's per-frame code out of the headline freeze, and it means the same block can happen MID-battle whenever a not-yet-compiled material variant first renders (audio runs on its own thread, matching brief fact 3, "combat sounds continue"). That mid-battle case is UNVERIFIED: verify with an rgl_log `compile_shader` burst inside a frozen battle window.
- **Fix direction (freeze lens owns it)**: tell the player the game is compiling instead of letting them kill it; keep the runtime shader cache warm across module-list churn.
- **1.5.x**: `ShaderCompileNotice` (boot-time only, does not cover battle loads), `ShaderCacheKeeper` (native, `372773d1a` per the brief), `ShaderSourceInstall`. None covers the in-battle render wait; this lens found no 1.5.x commit that addresses the battle-load case.

### RUNTIME-PERF-03: Every native-to-managed callback and every TAOM-patched engine method pays two always-run finalizers, one of which binds `__originalMethod`

- **Severity / confidence**: P1. Mechanism verified on 1.4.x and in the bundled Harmony; per-call cost measured on 1.5.x; frame-time share UNVERIFIED.
- **Evidence (working tree c5b84fb41 unless noted)**
  - `Main/SubModule.cs:161-171`: `Native2ManagedPatcher.AttachAll` runs in `OnSubModuleLoad`, default ON (`EnableCrashCapture ?? true`, `EnableNativeToManagedCapture ?? true`). `Main/Features/CrashReport/Hooks/Native2ManagedPatcher.cs:72-80` patches EVERY static method of the three `*CallbacksGenerated` classes; E3 line 4 reports `attached 247 Finalizer(s)`.
  - The v1.4.8 shims are the engine's hot native-to-managed boundary: `C:\Decompiled_Bannerlord\_categories_v1.4.8\MountAndBlade\TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade.AutoGenerated\ManagedCallbacks\CoreCallbacksGenerated.cs` includes `Agent_IsInSameFormationWith`, `Agent_GetFormationUnitSpacing`, `Agent_UpdateAgentStats`, `Agent_GetWeaponInaccuracy`, `Mission_MeleeHitCallback`, `Mission_MissileHitCallback`, `Mission_CanPhysicsCollideBetweenTwoEntities`, `Mission_MissileCalculatePassbySoundParametersCallbackMT` (the `MT` suffix marks a worker-thread caller) and `Mission_TickAgentsAndTeams`; `EngineCallbacksGenerated.cs` (86 statics) carries the script-component tick dispatch.
  - `Dependencies/SubModule.cs:288`: PatchShield pass 2 at `OnGameInitializationFinished`, after the shims exist. `Dependencies/Foundation/PatchShield.cs:60-69` excludes only the Gauntlet/2D namespaces, and the "own methods" skip keys on the DECLARING assembly starting with `TAOM`, so the 247 engine shims and every engine method TAOM patches (`Formation.GetOrderPositionOfUnit`, `Mission.OnTick`, `MissionState.OnTick`, `CharacterTableau.OnTick`, `Agent.EquipItemsFromSpawnEquipment`, the blow handlers) receive `ShieldFinalizerVoid/WithResult(MethodBase __originalMethod, Exception __exception)` (`PatchShield.cs:244`, `:255`).
  - Harmony 2.4.2 runs finalizers on the SUCCESS path too: `C:\Decompiled_Bannerlord\_modules_build\TAOM.Dependencies__0Harmony.cs:3085-3095` emits `AddFinalizers(catchExceptions: false)` inside the try block after the postfixes. The comment at `Native2ManagedPatcher.cs:16-17` ("Finalizers cost ~0 unless an exception is thrown") is wrong for this Harmony.
  - Cost of the `__originalMethod` binding, measured by 1.5.x plan 034 on .NET Framework 4.8.1 with Harmony 2.4.2 against the Debug-built TAOM.Dependencies (the build TAOM ships): about 64 ns and 241 bytes of garbage per call on one thread, about 1,146 ns per call with 8 threads contending, against about 5.4 ns and 0 bytes without the binding (`git show origin/bannerlord-1.5.x:plans/_audit/2026-10-02-perf/REPORT.md`, row B6). - **Mechanism**: Harmony emits `MethodBase.GetMethodFromHandle` per call to feed `__originalMethod`; under worker-thread contention it serialises. Shims called from the parallel agent tick or physics (`...MT`, formation and collision queries) pay the contended figure, and every call adds 241 bytes to gen0.
- **What is NOT known**: how many shim calls a v1.4.8 battle makes per frame. No 1.5.x document measured it (searched plans 007 and 034 and the 2026-10-02 audit). Verify with a temporary Interlocked counter in `Native2ManagedBridge.Finalizer`, logged once per 5 s in a 600-agent battle; or port the fixes, which remove the cost whatever the count is.
- **Fix direction (port, in order)**: (1) narrow the crash-capture sweep to the six shims that cover managed work no other finalizer wraps: 1.5.x `1d94df9c4` (plan 006 step 2) with the plan 006 follow-ups `65b691b6a`, `707275290`, `42624b95b`, `8e6b0935c`, `8c84fa20e`, `9e8c99bdb`, `9cfb91b15`, merge `5f6a96d2e`; (2) PatchShield skips the callback shims: plan 007, merge `d90b4be31` (with `578ac7d61`, `31a31f16b`, `0bf2409e6`, `900920dca`, `b0a93fb4c`, docs `88408869f`); (3) finalizers take only `__exception` and resolve the original from the stack after a throw: `5ee49d05f` (plan 034) plus the integration fix-ups in `43ca92ee9`. Step (3) alone removes the tax from every shielded method, including the TAOM-patched engine methods step (2) does not cover. Re-check each against the 1.4.x `Dependencies/` before cherry-picking: `bd1ac8292` (2026-10-21, a co-op rethrow revert) touched the same file later and is co-op only.
- **Boot-time side effect (other lens)**: 1.5.x measured the 247-shim sweep at 29 to 33 s of every launch on the maintainer's desktop (about 1 to 3 s on player machines per `plans/README.md` on that ref).

### RUNTIME-PERF-04: Engine callbacks that arrive off the main thread still write unlocked collections in six of the 25 battle behaviours (the 1.5.x fix for a 1.4.5-line mid-battle freeze is not ported)

- **Severity / confidence**: P1 (freeze class; overlaps the threading and freeze lenses). Writers verified on 1.4.x; that they cause brief fact 3's freeze is UNVERIFIED.
- **Evidence**
  - E4 (brief fact 5): `[ERROR] [TAOM] BehaviorTreeMissionLogic.OnAgentPanicked ran off the main mission thread (thread 20, main 17)`, plus `OnAgentShootMissile` on thread 21 and `OnAgentDismount` on thread 22. Three different non-main threads, so callbacks arrive from the async tick AND from TWParallel workers.
  - `Main/BehaviorTreeWrapper/BehaviorTreeMissionLogic.cs:159-167, 224-357` already PARKS every off-thread callback in a `DeferredCallbackQueue` and replays it in `OnMissionTick` (`:122-123`). The E4 ERROR lines are that tripwire firing; the tree logic itself is safe.
  - The other behaviours are not. Callback overrides with zero `IsOnMainThread`/`RunOrDefer` checks (grep of the 25 behaviour files): `AdvancedCombatBehavior.OnAgentDeleted` (`AdvancedCombatBehavior.cs:71-76`: adapter-cache evict plus `SpatialGrid.Remove`, a `List<Agent>.Remove` on cells the main thread reads, `SpatialGrid.cs:51-58`); `WargMissionBehavior.OnAgentDismount` (the very callback E4 caught on thread 22); `MountDespawnMissionBehavior.OnAgentRemoved/OnAgentDeleted` (`MountDespawnMissionBehavior.cs:46-75`: `_pending[affectedAgent.Index] = affectedAgent` on a plain `Dictionary<int, Agent>`); `FieldCommissionMissionLogic.OnAgentRemoved` to `FieldCommissionMeritService.RegisterKill` (`FieldCommissionMeritService.cs:78-85`: `_battleKills[troopId] = current + 1` on a plain `Dictionary<string,int>`); `EnlistmentMeritMissionBehavior.OnAgentRemoved` (kill count); `CareerPerkMissionBehavior.OnScoreHit/OnAgentRemoved/OnAgentDeleted`.
- **Mechanism**: `Mission.OnPreTick` waits in `WaitTickCompletion` for the previous frame's agent tick. A plain `Dictionary` written from two threads can corrupt its bucket chain, and a corrupted chain can make `FindEntry`/`Insert` loop forever. A thread spinning there is one busy core (Task Manager "looks normal"); the main thread then waits forever while audio keeps playing. That is brief fact 3's shape. 1.5.x `f75288ebf` was written for exactly this report on the 1.4.5 line ("heap flat, no exception, no crash report") and itself states that the cause is not proven.
- **Fix direction**: port `f75288ebf` "fix(mission): v2.0.30 - park off-thread callback writes (#634)": `DeferredCallbackQueue.RunOrDefer` for the writers above, a lock in `RegisterKill`, an Interlocked enlistment count, off-thread reports at WARNING (file only), and `Patch91_MissionTickStallProbes` plus `MissionTickStallWatchdog`, which logs every stuck thread's stack as `[MissionStall]` at 10/20/40 s. The watchdog is the part that turns the next player freeze into evidence. Drop its `SignatureStrikes` hunk (1.5.x-only feature). Its RCA `docs/reviews/rca-offthread-agent-removed-2026-09-22.md` is absent on origin/bannerlord-1.4.5 (checked with `git cat-file`).
- **1.5.x**: `f75288ebf`.

### RUNTIME-PERF-09: TAOM's logger writes every INFO/WARNING/ERROR line synchronously, with a flush, on whichever thread calls it, including engine worker threads

- **Severity / confidence**: P2. Verified on 1.4.x. Answers brief question 3.
- **Evidence**: `Main/Core/Logging/FileLogger.cs:79-89`: `LogInfo/LogWarning/LogError` enqueue then call `Drain()` on the caller; `:93-130` `Drain` takes `_writeLock`, writes every queued line and calls `StreamWriter.Flush()` (one `WriteFile` per call into the OS cache; no write-through). `LogDebug` is formatted on the caller (timestamp `ToString` plus interpolation, no level gate) and drained by a background thread that sleeps 50 ms when idle (`:132-139`). A durable call that lands while the writer thread is draining a DEBUG batch waits on the same lock.
- **Volume in the public build (E4, 1 MB, 35 min)**: `[BattleLoad]` INFO 492 KB / 3,429 lines (3,279 of them the per-agent `AgentEquipBegin/AgentEquipOk/AgentBuildDone` phases of the two arena loads: 559 and 534 agents); `[AutoResolve]` 421 KB / 225 lines; the "mesh and material dumps" are the `[TableauDiag] Render census` blocks, 29 of them, about 49 KB of continuation lines. ## Summary

The single most important conclusion: **TAOM's per-frame code is not what froze the Harad player's first battle.** In E3 the managed frame loop stopped entirely after `FinishMissionLoadingDone` (the 1 Hz `WaitingForRender` marker went silent while a thread-pool timer kept printing `[MemSample]`); E4 shows the same stop lasting 68 s and then completing, with 1,569 shaders compiling at about 23 per second. E3 had 2,697 pending and the log ends about 92 s in, so the most likely reading is a working shader compile that the player killed (twice, per the stale inflight marker). That case belongs to the freeze lens (RUNTIME-PERF-01), and brief fact 6's "111 s town load" is mostly the player sitting in town menus (RUNTIME-PERF-02).

For runtime performance proper, the 1.4.x line carries four structural costs that 1.5.x already removed: a `__originalMethod` finalizer on every native-to-managed callback shim and every TAOM-patched engine method, running on every call and contended on worker threads (03); unlocked collection writes from engine callbacks that arrive on worker threads, the mechanism 1.5.x attached to a mid-battle freeze report from this very line (04); always-on diagnostics that walk every agent every frame or write one synchronous disk line per AI battle (06, 07, 09); and per-read MCM resolves on blow, frame and party paths (08, 12). Missions in E4 are calm (2 to 3 gen0/s); the campaign map is where the GC churns (up to 15.7 gen0/s and a gen2 every 9 to 16 s), driven by saves, an inventory open and TAOM's auto-resolve stream among others. No magnitude below can be ranked by measurement on 1.4.x today, because the line has no frame-time instrument (13).
