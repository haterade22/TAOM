TAOM made six compatibility changes on 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10:
- __init__.py imports both modules explicitly before reloading them.
- Patch.refresh passes self.context instead of an undefined name.
- The optional menu method references MdUtilMultiPetik instead of an undefined operator.
- Petik Line snapping uses the line supplied by its caller instead of the selected line.
- Selected closed edge cycles are discovered and retain their closing vertex.
- Control-point growth on a closed line samples the original polyline instead of indexing past it.

petik_preferences.py and blender_manifest.toml remain byte-identical to upstream.
No other executable Petik logic was changed.
