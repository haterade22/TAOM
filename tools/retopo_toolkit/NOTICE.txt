TAOM Retopology Toolkit

The independent TAOM engine, Blender adapter, operators, packaging tools, tests,
and original workflow text are MIT licensed.

Petik 2.0.7 is a separate add-on by Mihai Dobrin, redistributed under
GNU GPL-3.0-or-later with six recorded compatibility fixes. Its source and license are under Petik/ in its installable
archive and tools/petik/Petik/ in the source archive.

Retopo Planes 1.6.3 by Nodes Interactive was used as a behavioral reference and for measured
synthetic output comparisons. The independently structured TAOM implementation is a behavioral
port based on docs/research/retopo-planes-1-6-3-clean-room.md, with explicitly documented validity
corrections. No Retopo Planes .blend file, Geometry Nodes groups, node layout, demo meshes,
textures, materials, presets, activation data, or other product assets are included.

Exoside Quad Remesher is an optional separate licensed dependency for TAOM's other
retopology backend. No Exoside binaries or license data are included.
