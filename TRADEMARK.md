# TAOM Trademark Policy

TAOM's code is free (MIT) and its content is freely shareable for non-commercial use
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). **The project's name and branding are neither.** This file says so explicitly,
because neither of those licenses grants trademark rights and silence has been mistaken for
permission.

## What is claimed

The following are unregistered (common-law) marks of the TAOM project, established by continuous
public use:

- **TAOM**
- **Tales From the Age of Men**
- The TAOM logo and project wordmark
- The visual identity of the TAOM mod pages and community channels

## No trademark grant

Neither the [MIT License](LICENSE) nor [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT.md) conveys any right to
use these marks. MIT grants rights in software. CC BY-NC-SA grants rights in content. Both are
silent on trademarks, and that silence is not permission. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 says as much in its own
text at Section 2(b)(2).

You can take every line of TAOM's code and every asset it is entitled to license, and still not be
entitled to call the result TAOM.

## What you may do without asking

These uses are fine, and we would rather say so plainly than have people guess:

- Describing your work accurately: "a fork of TAOM", "based on TAOM", "compatible with TAOM",
  "a patch for TAOM", "TAOM: <your distinct name>" where your name is the prominent one.
- Naming TAOM in documentation, changelogs, credits, reviews, videos, and discussion.
- Attribution required by CC BY-NC-SA, which necessarily names the project.
- Linking to TAOM's pages and repositories.

This is nominative use. It is how people talk about software, and nothing here restricts it.

## What requires permission

- Naming a derivative work **TAOM**, **Tales From the Age of Men**, or anything close enough that
  a player looking for TAOM would find yours by mistake.
- Using the TAOM logo, wordmark, or mod-page art as the branding of another project.
- Presenting a fork or derivative as official, endorsed, sponsored, or a continuation of TAOM.
- Using the marks on merchandise, or in any commercial context.

If you want to do one of these, ask. The answer is not automatically no.

## Enforcement

Where a derivative is distributed under a confusable name or branding, the project will ask for a
rename first and use the distribution platform's brand-confusion process second. Nexus Mods and
Steam both provide one. This policy exists so that request is a matter of record rather than a
surprise.
